## Evidence & Document Management

**Tools for organizing, storing, and managing documents**

These features keep your evidence safe, organized, and ready to use.

### Evidence Locker: Secure Document Storage

**What it does:** A locked digital safe for your documents. Upload PDFs, photos, medical records, decision letters — anything you need for your case.

**Why it matters:** You need to keep evidence safe and organized so you can find it fast when you need it. Evidence Locker encrypts everything with AES-256-GCM security (the same encryption banks use).

**How to use it:**
1. Tap the Evidence Locker icon
2. Choose "Upload Document"
3. Select from camera, photo library, or files
4. Add title and tags (optional)
5. Tap "Save" — it's encrypted immediately

**Who it helps:**
- Anyone building a legal case
- People with poor memory or fatigue
- Those managing many documents
- Persons with disabilities gathering evidence

**Real-world example:**
You get a denial letter. Photograph it and upload to Evidence Locker immediately. Add the tag "denial-2026-may". Later, when you need it for an appeal, search "denial" and find it in seconds.

**Tips & Best Practices:**
- Photograph documents as soon as you get them (don't lose them)
- Use consistent tags: "decision", "medical", "letter", "email", "photo"
- Add a title like "Denial Letter - May 15 2026" (include dates)
- Check the encryption status (lock icon) to confirm it's saved securely
- Review your Evidence Timeline weekly to spot gaps

**Common Mistakes:**
- Uploading low-quality photos (use good lighting)
- Forgetting to add titles (make it searchable)
- Uploading everything to the same category (organize with tags)

**Troubleshooting:**
- **File won't upload?** Check file size (max 50 MB). Try a different format.
- **Can't find old documents?** Use the search bar and try different tags.
- **Photo is blurry?** Delete and retake with better lighting.

**Accessibility:**
- Fully works with screen readers
- All buttons have 48px+ touch targets
- Works with keyboard navigation
- High contrast mode compliant

## Advocacy & Legal Tools

**Tools for understanding your rights, managing deadlines, and building your case**

These features help you understand decisions, fight denials, and manage legal timelines.

### Parse Claim: Decode Your Decision Letter

**What it does:** Upload a decision letter (denial, approval, partial approval) and the app reads it. It tells you:
- What was decided
- Why it was decided
- Your deadlines
- Your options (appeal, review, reconsideration)
- Plain-language explanation

**Why it matters:** Decision letters use confusing legal language. Parse Claim translates it into plain English so you understand exactly where you stand and what to do next.

**How to use it:**
1. Get a decision letter (email, mail, PDF)
2. Photograph it or save as PDF
3. Tap "Parse Claim" from Home
4. Select "Upload Decision Letter"
5. Follow the steps
6. Read the plain-language summary
7. Note your deadlines

**Who it helps:**
- Anyone who got a decision (approval, denial, or partial)
- People with brain fog who struggle with legal text
- Those managing multiple tribunal cases
- People new to the system

**Real-world example:**
You receive a WSIB decision letter. It uses legal language and you don't understand if you've been approved, denied, or partially approved. Upload to Parse Claim. It tells you: "Your claim was partially approved. You have 30 days to appeal this part. Here's how."

**Tips:**
- Use high-quality photos (good lighting, all pages visible)
- Upload immediately after receiving a decision
- Save the summary for your records
- Share the summary with your lawyer/advocate

**Troubleshooting:**
- **It couldn't read my letter?** Try taking a better photo (straight on, good light, full page)
- **It says I have 30 days but I think it's longer?** Always verify with official tribunal websites
- **It didn't find all my deadlines?** Check the original letter or call the tribunal

**Related Features:**

### Deadline Tracker: Never Miss a Filing Date

**What it does:** Keeps all your important deadlines in one place. Shows you:
- What deadlines you have
- How many days until each deadline
- Notifications as deadlines approach
- Status of each deadline (pending, met, missed)

**Why it matters:** Missing a deadline can destroy your case. One day late and you might lose your right to appeal. Deadline Tracker ensures you never lose track.

**How to use it:**
1. Open Deadline Tracker
2. Add deadlines manually OR import from Parse Claim
3. For each deadline, add: date, type (appeal, document due, hearing, etc.), notes
4. The app shows countdown
5. Get notifications 7 days before, 1 day before, and on the day

**Who it helps:**
- Anyone with deadlines (legal cases, applications)
- People with memory problems or fatigue
- Those managing multiple cases
- People with ADHD or executive dysfunction

**Real-world example:**
Parse Claim found you have a 30-day appeal deadline. You tap "Add to Tracker". The app now shows "Appeal deadline: 27 days left" on your Home screen. On day 23, you get a notification. On day 29, it reminds you again.

**Tips:**
- Add deadlines as soon as you know them
- Add a 7-day buffer (file 7 days early, not on the last day)
- Color-code by case or tribunal type
- Check your deadlines every Sunday

**Related Features:**
- [Parse Claim](#parse-claim) — find deadlines in decision letters

## Wellness & Health Tracking

**Tools for managing pain, energy, mental health, and daily living**

These features help you track your health and manage your conditions while doing advocacy work.

### Pacing Partner: Energy-Smart Activity Planning

**What it does:** Helps you do more without causing flare-ups. You set your energy baseline, and Pacing Partner suggests activities and breaks to stay within your safe zone.

**Why it matters:** If you have ME/CFS, EDS, Fibromyalgia, or other conditions, pacing is critical. Doing too much triggers flare-ups that set you back weeks. Pacing Partner helps you stay sustainable.

**How to use it:**
1. Tap Pacing Partner
2. Set your energy baseline (the activity level you can safely do daily)
3. Log planned activities for the day
4. Pacing Partner shows you if you're at risk
5. Get suggestions to stay in your safe zone
6. Log actual activities
7. Review how you felt

**Who it helps:**
- People with ME/CFS or chronic fatigue
- Those with conditions causing flare-ups
- Anyone managing energy/pain/disability
- People doing intensive advocacy work

**Real-world example:**
Your safe activity level is 50% of normal. You plan to: work 4 hours, go to a hearing (2 hours), and run errands (1 hour) = too much. Pacing Partner warns you. You reschedule errands for next week and now you're at 60% — still slightly risky but safer.

**Tips:**
- Set your baseline conservatively (better to underestimate)
- Log activities even on bad days
- Review weekly patterns
- Use the 50% rule: do 50% of your capacity today, rest tomorrow

**Related Features:**
- [Energy Mood Dashboard](#energy-mood) — track energy patterns
- [Symptom Tracker](#symptom-tracker) — track if pacing helps
- [Wellness Check-in](#wellness-check-in) — daily quick check-in

**Who it helps:**

### Feedback & Bug Reporting

**What it does:** Report bugs, suggest features, or tell us what's confusing.

**How to use it:**
1. Open Settings
2. Tap "Send Feedback"
3. Describe your issue/suggestion
4. Tap "Send"
5. You'll get a response within 48 hours

**Severity Levels:**
- **Critical:** App crashed, data lost, urgent issue
- **High:** Feature doesn't work, blocking your use
- **Medium:** Something confusing, workaround exists
- **Low:** Nice-to-have improvement, cosmetic issue

**Who it helps:**
- Anyone finding problems
- People wanting to suggest features
- Those wanting to report confusing UI
