## This Week in Brief

This week focused on Ontario and national tribunal data integration, visualization improvements, content structure cleanup, and deployment reliability.

## New Features

- Added major enhancements to keyword network visualizations, including tribunal-specific views and zoom controls.
- Added Ontario tribunal scope updates across WSIAT, HRTO, ONSBT, and ONWSIB datasets.
- Added dark mode and high contrast support in additional analysis surfaces.
- Added Tier C enrichment pipeline and direct tribunal scraper support for unresolved cases.
- Added comprehensive CanLII tribunal data integration work.

## Fixes

- Fixed UTF-8 and encoding issues affecting content rendering.
- Fixed category formatting for Jekyll consistency.
- Fixed threshold behavior in visualization views to display expected keyword ranges.
- Fixed contact and metadata placement in selected blog entries.

## Documentation

- Added language clarifications for abandonment-rate context and detection framing.
- Added updates to research heading and scope wording for clarity.

## System Operations

- Continued campaign and event automation, trend updates, and analytics data refresh.
- Continued daily content and summary generation runs.

## Why It Matters

For injured workers and persons with disabilities, this improves access to understandable legal trend data. For families, advocates, and the general public, this improves trust through clearer context and stronger data transparency.
