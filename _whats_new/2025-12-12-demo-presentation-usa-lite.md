## 🎤 First Demo Presentation Complete!

**December 9, 2025** - We're thrilled to announce our **first official demo/intro presentation for 3mpwr App was a success!** 

We presented to the **Thunder Bay & District Injured Workers Support Group**, marking a major step in our journey to empower persons with disabilities and injured workers across Canada and beyond.

### 🌟 Key Highlights

- **Successful live demonstration** of core app features
- **Positive reception** from attendees across Canada and the USA
- **Strong interest** from USA-based supporters and allies
- **Production-ready status confirmed** with 721 tests passing
- **Phase 1 Closed Internal Testing** about to begin!

## 🇺🇸 USA Lite Now Available!

**Great news for our American friends!** Following strong interest from USA listeners during our demo presentation, we're excited to announce:

### USA Lite Features

**Now available for US-based supporters and allies:**

- **Wellness & Mental Health Tools** - Energy tracking, mood monitoring, pacing partner AI
- **Community Connection** - Connect with the broader disability rights movement
- **Letter Wizard** - Templates adaptable for US contexts
- **Evidence Locker** - Secure document storage with AES-256 encryption
- **Crisis Resources** - US crisis hotlines (988, Crisis Text Line)
- **Accessibility Features** - Full WCAG AAA compliance, Complexity Mode, voice navigation

### What's Different in USA Lite

| Feature | Canada (Full) | USA Lite |
|---------|---------------|----------|
| Workers' Comp Templates | WSIB/WCB/provincial | General templates |
| Benefit Programs | ODSP, AISH, CPP-D, EI | General disability resources |
| Legal Workflows | Canadian tribunals | Advocacy tools only |
| External Resources | 96 Canadian resources | US crisis & advocacy links |
| Community | Full access | Full access |
| Wellness Tools | Full access | Full access |

**Note:** USA Lite focuses on universal disability advocacy tools while Canadian-specific legal and benefits features remain Canada-only.

### 🎯 Initial Focus States

We're prioritizing these **10 states** for USA Lite development:

**West Coast & Southwest:** California, Texas, Washington  
**Midwest:** Michigan, Ohio  
**Northeast:** New York, Pennsylvania  
**South:** Tennessee, Georgia, Mississippi

*Selected based on community interest and workers' compensation system complexity. More states coming soon!*
