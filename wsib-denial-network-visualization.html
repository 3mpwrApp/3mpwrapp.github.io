📊 What This Visualization Shows:

  • Nodes (circles): Keywords from 11,430 tribunal decisions (2020-2026)
  • Edges (lines): Co-occurrence frequency (how often keywords appear in the same case)
  • Thicker lines: More frequent co-occurrence (statistically significant clustering)
  • Node size: Larger = appears in more cases overall (frequency-weighted)
  • Colors: Different categories (denial reasoning patterns, body parts, medical/legal terms)

What This Shows: Certain keywords cluster together more than random chance would predict (measured using statistical lift).
What This Suggests: May indicate template-based reasoning or widespread training practices.
What This Does NOT Prove: Coordination between decision-makers (would require evidence of communication/citation, not just word co-occurrence).
