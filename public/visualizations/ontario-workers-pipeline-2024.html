💡 Key Insights

  • You are not alone: 7.0% of injured workers (5,000/year) appeal to WSIAT.
  • Long-term cases are common: 15.18% (10,896) need benefits beyond 90 days.
  • Appeals often succeed: 57% of WSIAT appeals result in benefits being reinstated or increased.
  • System costs are high: ~$131M/year spent on disputed claims.
  • Individual costs matter: Average appeal takes 2-3 years and costs $107k-$120k in lost income and expenses.
