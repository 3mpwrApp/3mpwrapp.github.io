## Phase 1: Foundation & Core Features (Q1 2026)

### Week 1-2: Legal Action Hub - Foundation
**Status**: 🟢 In Development  
**Target Launch**: Mid-January 2026

#### Legal Action Hub - 5-Tab PowerTool
A unified hub consolidating 10+ legal features into 5 integrated tabs:

##### **Tab 1: Accountability** 📋
- **Purpose**: Track cases and action items against entities
- **Features**:
  - Active case list with status tracking
  - Case timeline and updates
  - Quick action buttons (new case, view all, network)
  - Integration with Accountability Tracker
- **Data**: Firestore cases with real-time sync
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AAA compliant, full screen-reader support

##### **Tab 2: Coach** 🎓
- **Purpose**: Communication scripts and accountability guidance
- **Features**:
  - Accountability Coach AI assistant
  - Pre-written communication templates
  - Interactive scenario planning
  - Progress tracking for coaching milestones
- **Data**: Firebase Realtime & local cache
- **Roadmap**: AI-powered script generation (Phase 2)

##### **Tab 3: Legal Help** ⚖️
- **Purpose**: Find lawyers, resources, and collective legal support
- **Features**:
  - Lawyer finder with specialty filters
  - Legal DNA testing (basic/full)
  - Collective Legal support directory
  - Know Your Rights educational content
  - Resource library (searchable PDFs)
- **Data**: Firestore index + remote API
- **Roadmap**: Video tutorials, localized resources (Phase 2)

##### **Tab 4: Automation** ⚡
- **Purpose**: Automated legal tools and processes
- **Features**:
  - Legal automation templates
  - Justice as a Service workflows
  - Automated letter generation
  - Document upload and management
  - Evidence queue integration
- **Data**: Firestore + Cloud Storage
- **Roadmap**: Advanced AI summaries, batch processing (Phase 2)

##### **Tab 5: Policy** 📢
- **Purpose**: Policy advocacy and campaign participation
- **Features**:
  - Active campaigns list
  - Policy advocacy templates
  - Campaign participation tracker
  - Share to social media
  - Impact metrics and feedback
- **Data**: Firestore campaigns + analytics
- **Roadmap**: Geolocation-based campaigns (Phase 2)

---

### Week 2-4: Ally & Support Network - Phase 1A
**Status**: 🟢 In Development  
**Target Launch**: Late January 2026

#### Peer Mentor Discovery System
A matching system connecting users with trained peer mentors:

##### **Component 1: Mentor Discovery Interface**
- **Purpose**: Find and connect with peer mentors
- **Features**:
  - Searchable mentor directory
  - Filter by expertise (legal, emotional, practical)
  - Mentor profiles with ratings and reviews
  - Quick mentorship request buttons
  - Chat integration
- **Data**: Firestore mentor profiles + reviews
- **Accessibility**: Keyboard navigation, voice search ready

##### **Component 2: Mentor Profiles**
- **Purpose**: Showcase mentor qualifications and experience
- **Profile Includes**:
  - Photo, name, and bio
  - Expertise areas with proficiency levels
  - Certifications and training
  - Languages spoken
  - Availability and timezone
  - User ratings and testimonials
  - Contact preferences

##### **Component 3: Mentorship Matching**
- **Purpose**: AI-powered mentor-mentee pairing
- **Algorithm**:
  - Expertise relevance scoring
  - Language matching
  - Timezone compatibility
  - User preference matching
  - Load balancing across mentors
- **Roadmap**: ML-based matching optimization (Phase 2)

##### **Component 4: Mentor Onboarding**
- **Purpose**: Train and certify new mentors
- **Includes**:
  - 8-week training program
  - Certification exam
  - Code of conduct agreement
  - Privacy and confidentiality training
  - Continuing education credits
- **Status**: Framework ready, content in progress

## 🚀 Competitive Positioning

### vs. Legal Tech Apps
- **LegalZoom**: We focus on empowerment, not document filling
- **Avvo**: We include peer support, not just lawyer finding
- **Rocket Lawyer**: Free for all, not freemium model

### vs. Community Platforms
- **Facebook Groups**: Moderated, safe, verified mentors
- **Reddit**: Professional structure, legal expertise, privacy-first

### vs. Mental Health Apps
- **BetterHelp**: Therapy + legal support integration
- **Talkspace**: Community-first, peer-led

### Unique Value Propositions
1. **The Only App** that combines legal empowerment + peer mentorship
2. **Accessibility-First** (WCAG AAA)
3. **Evidence Tracking** for legal proceedings
4. **AI Coach** for communication and strategy
5. **Community Justice** focus (advocacy + legal change)
