### Video 4.1: "BYOC Mode - Bring Your Own Cloud (Complete Data Ownership)"
**Length**: 10 minutes  
**Keywords**: data privacy, encryption, cloud storage, BYOC, data sovereignty

**Script**:

**[0:00-0:30] Hook**
"What if I told you that you don't have to trust US with your health data? You can choose ANY cloud provider you want - or none at all. 3mpwr App's BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud) mode gives you 100% data ownership. Let me show you."

**[0:30-1:30] The Cloud Problem**
"Most health apps force you to use THEIR cloud:
- Google Cloud, AWS, Azure - you don't choose
- They control the encryption keys
- They can read your data anytime
- They're subject to government subpoenas
- They can shut down your account without notice

Even if they promise 'we don't look at your data' - you have to TRUST them. Why trust when you can VERIFY?"

**[1:30-3:00] How BYOC Works**
"3mpwr App supports:
1. **No Cloud (Default)**: All data stays on your phone, encrypted with AES-256
2. **Your Private Cloud (BYOC)**:
   - WebDAV (self-hosted Nextcloud, ownCloud)
   - Google Drive (if you trust Google)
   - Dropbox (if you trust Dropbox)
   - OneDrive (if you trust Microsoft)
   - AWS S3 (your own bucket with your keys)
   - iCloud (Apple users)
   - ANY WebDAV-compatible service

The KEY difference: YOU control the encryption keys. We never see your data."

**[3:00-6:00] Setting Up BYOC**
"[Screen recording]

1. Open Settings → Privacy & Security
2. Scroll to 'Data Management'
3. Tap 'Configure Private Cloud'
4. Choose provider:
   - For self-hosted: Select 'WebDAV'
     - Enter URL: https://your-nextcloud.com/remote.php/dav/files/username/
     - Enter username
     - Enter password (stored in iOS Keychain / Android Keystore)
   - For Google Drive: Tap 'Connect Google Drive'
     - OAuth flow (you grant permission)
   - For Dropbox: Tap 'Connect Dropbox'
     - OAuth flow
5. Test Connection (app sends encrypted test file)
6. If successful: '✅ Connected to your private cloud'
7. Enable 'Auto-Sync' (syncs every 24 hours or on demand)

**What Gets Synced:**
- Mood entries (encrypted)
- Pacing logs (encrypted)
- Evidence files (encrypted)
- Deadlines (encrypted)
- User preferences (encrypted)

**What NEVER Gets Synced:**
- Encryption keys (stay on your device only)
- Biometric data (fingerprint, face ID)
- Device-specific settings"

**[6:00-8:00] Self-Hosted Nextcloud Setup (Advanced)**
"For maximum privacy, self-host:

1. Rent a VPS (DigitalOcean, Linode, Hetzner): $5-10/month
2. Install Nextcloud (one-click installer available)
3. Enable encryption (server-side)
4. Configure BYOC in 3mpwr App (WebDAV URL)
5. Now you have:
   - 100% data ownership
   - No third-party access
   - Geographic control (choose server location)
   - GDPR/PIPEDA compliance

Tutorial link in description."

**[8:00-9:00] Hybrid Mode**
"Don't want full BYOC? Use Hybrid:
- Non-sensitive data (preferences, theme): Firestore (convenient)
- Sensitive data (mood, pain, evidence): Your private cloud
- Best of both worlds: convenience + privacy

Set in Settings → Privacy & Security → Data Policy → 'Hybrid BYOC'"

**[9:00-10:00] Call to Action**
"Your health data is TOO sensitive to trust anyone else. Take control today.

If you're tech-savvy: Self-host Nextcloud (tutorial linked below)
If not: Use any provider YOU trust, not our defaults

Comment below: What cloud provider do you trust most? Like and subscribe for more privacy tutorials!"
