**Purpose:** Guide for presenters to walk audience through the app during live demonstrations

## 🎯 Presentation Overview

**Audience:** Disability advocates, potential users, investors, partners, testers  
**Goal:** Showcase core features and demonstrate value proposition

## 🚀 Demo Flow (15-Minute Version)

### **Opening (1 min)**
> "3mpwr App is a free, accessible platform designed by and for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters. Everything works offline, your data stays private, and it's built to meet WCAG AAA accessibility standards."

### **Stop 1: Home Screen (2 min)**
**What to show:**
- Clean, simple interface
- Quick access tiles
- Personalized recommendations (if Disability Wizard completed)
- SOS/Crisis button (tap to show menu, but don't activate)

**Talking points:**
- "The Home screen adapts to your needs - Simple Mode shows 5 features, Standard shows 20, Power User shows everything"
- "Notice the SOS button - tap it anytime to access 988, Crisis Text Line, or emergency contacts"

---

### **Stop 2: Wellness Tab (3 min)**
**What to show:**
1. **Daily Energy Coins** - Show the spoon/energy tracking
2. **Mood Check-In** - Demonstrate a quick mood entry
3. **Pacing Timer** - Show activity/rest balance tool

**Talking points:**
- "The app uses 'energy coins' based on spoon theory - you decide how many you have each day"
- "All wellness data stays on your device unless you choose to sync"
- "The Pacing Timer helps manage boom-and-bust cycles"

**Demo action:** Log a quick mood entry or energy level

---

### **Stop 3: Events & Campaigns (3 min)**
**What to show:**
1. **Events Tab** - Browse community events
   - Show holiday/observance calendar
   - Show a TBDIWSG event or community event
   - Demonstrate "Add to Calendar" button
   
2. **Campaigns Tab** - Show active campaigns
   - Show "Every Canadian Counts" campaign
   - Show how to join/support a campaign
   - Point out "Submit to 3mpwr App" button for user-created content

**Talking points:**
- "Events sync automatically from our website to your app"
- "Users can create their own events and campaigns and submit them for review"
- "The calendar includes Canadian holidays, disability observances, and health awareness days"

**Demo action:** Add an event to calendar or join a campaign

---

### **Stop 4: Resources Hub (3 min)**
**What to show:**
1. **Letter Wizard** - Show template categories
   - Show 22+ letter types
   - Demonstrate opening one template
   
2. **Evidence Locker** - Show secure document storage
   - Explain offline queue feature
   - Show encryption/privacy

3. **Deadline Tracker** - Show how to track important dates

**Talking points:**
- "Resources are IN-APP tools that work completely offline"
- "The Evidence Locker encrypts your documents and never shares them"
- "Letter Wizard has templates for appeals, accommodation requests, complaints"

**Demo action:** Open a letter template or add a sample deadline

---

### **Stop 5: Community (2 min)**
**What to show:**
- Provincial channels
- Topic-based discussions
- Support threads

**Talking points:**
- "Community is moderated for safety"
- "Find peer support from people with similar experiences"

**What to show:**

## 💡 Tips for Presenters

### Accessibility Considerations
- **Audio description:** Describe what you're showing for visually impaired audience members
- **Screen magnification:** Zoom in when showing small text
- **Pace:** Move slowly through screens, give time to absorb
- **Repeat:** Say feature names as you show them

### Common Questions

**Q: Is it really free?**
> "Yes, 100% free for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters. No premium tier, no paywall."

**Q: How do you make money?**
> "Grants, corporate supporters who pay-it-forward, and organizational partnerships. Never from user data."

**Q: Is my data safe?**
> "Your data stays on your device. Optional cloud sync uses YOUR accounts (Google Drive, iCloud). We can't access your data."

**Q: Does it work offline?**
> "Yes! Core features work completely offline. Data syncs when you reconnect."

**Q: Who made this?**
> "Built by disabled people, for disabled people. We follow 'Nothing About Us Without Us' principles."
