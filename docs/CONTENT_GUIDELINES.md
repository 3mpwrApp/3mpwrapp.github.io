# Content Guidelines for 3mpwrApp Blog & Documentation

## Language Standards

### ❌ NEVER USE:
- Profanity or swear words (e.g., "bullshit", "crap", "damn", etc.)
- Offensive language
- Derogatory terms
- Slurs of any kind

### ✅ ALWAYS USE:
- Professional, respectful language
- Accessible terminology (avoid jargon when possible)
- Disability-first or person-first language (as appropriate to context)
- Empowering, solution-focused framing

## Tone Guidelines

### DO:
- Be direct and honest about systemic barriers
- Show empathy for struggles without being condescending
- Use strong, clear language to describe injustice (e.g., "unjust", "unfair", "discriminatory")
- Be optimistic about solutions while realistic about challenges

### DON'T:
- Use casual profanity (even to emphasize frustration)
- Patronize or pity people with disabilities
- Use ableist language or metaphors
- Make assumptions about what readers can/cannot do

## Examples of Appropriate Alternatives

| ❌ Avoid | ✅ Use Instead |
|---------|---------------|
| "the same bullshit reason" | "the same unjust reason" |
| "this is crap" | "this is unacceptable" |
| "damn bureaucracy" | "oppressive bureaucracy" |
| "screwed over" | "treated unfairly" |
| "crazy policy" | "nonsensical policy" |

## Why This Matters

1. **Accessibility:** Screen readers read every word - profanity can be jarring
2. **Professionalism:** We're building credibility with advocacy orgs, government, legal community
3. **Inclusivity:** Some readers may be offended by casual profanity
4. **Focus:** Strong descriptive language is more powerful than swearing

## Quick Check Before Publishing

- [ ] No profanity or swear words
- [ ] Respectful of all disability communities
- [ ] Clear, actionable language
- [ ] Empowering tone (not pity)
- [ ] Professional enough for legal/government audiences
- [ ] Accessible to screen readers
