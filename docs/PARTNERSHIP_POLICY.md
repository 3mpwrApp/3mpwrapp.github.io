## 🎯 Purpose

This document defines:
1. What qualifies as a "partnership" vs. "supporter" vs. "endorsement"
2. Criteria for accepting/rejecting partnership proposals
3. Red lines we will never cross
4. How to formalize partnerships (MOU templates)
5. Conflict resolution process

**Philosophy:** We partner to amplify impact, never to compromise values. The disability community comes first—always.

---

## 📊 Partnership Tiers

### **Tier 1: Community Supporter** (Informal)

**What it means:**
- Organization publicly supports 3mpwrApp
- May share our links on their website/social media
- May mention us in newsletters/events
- NO formal agreement needed
- NO exclusivity, NO obligations

**Example:**
- Thunder Bay Injured Workers (current relationship)
- Local disability groups
- Individual advocates

**Benefits to Supporter:**
- Listed on our `/supporters` page (if they want public recognition)
- Can use "We support 3mpwrApp" badge on their website
- Early access to new features (beta invites)

**Obligations from 3mpwrApp:**
- None (we appreciate support but owe nothing)
- Optional: Shout-out on social media

**How to establish:**
- Email or verbal agreement
- Add to supporters list
- Send thank-you note

**Current Tier 1 Partners:**
- Thunder Bay Injured Workers ✅

---

### **Tier 2: Affiliated Organization** (Formal, Non-Exclusive)

**What it means:**
- Mutual promotion (we recommend them, they recommend us)
- May co-host events or campaigns
- May share resources (content, data, expertise)
- Formal MOU signed
- Non-exclusive (both can work with others)

**Example:**
- IAVGO (Injured and Affected Victims Group Ontario)
- Community legal clinics
- Disability advocacy organizations
- Unions (disability committees)

**Benefits to Partner:**
- Co-branding on joint initiatives
- Access to our tribunal data for research
- Featured on our `/partnerships` page
- Joint fundraising (optional)

**Obligations from 3mpwrApp:**
- Honor MOU terms (promotion, data sharing, etc.)
- Respond to partnership inquiries within 7 days
- Annual review meeting (check in on alignment)

**Obligations from Partner:**
- Honor MOU terms
- Respect our values (see below)
- Notify us if relationship changes

**How to establish:**
- Initial conversation (email, call)
- Negotiate MOU terms
- Sign MOU (template below)
- Announce partnership publicly

**Current Tier 2 Partners:**
- None yet (Thunder Bay could upgrade if desired)

---

### **Tier 3: Strategic Partner** (Formal, High-Commitment)

**What it means:**
- Deep integration (embedded features, shared infrastructure)
- Significant resource commitment (funding, staff time, technology)
- Long-term agreement (2+ years)
- Governance involvement (partner may have advisory role)
- Exclusive in specific domain (e.g., only legal clinic partner in Ontario)

**Example:**
- Major union (OPSEU, CUPE)
- Law school clinic (Osgoode, UofT)
- Healthcare system (CAMH, community health centers)
- Technology provider (Expo, Firebase—though these are vendors, not partners)

**Benefits to Partner:**
- Significant brand visibility
- Input on roadmap (advisory, not control)
- Priority access to data/features
- Joint grant applications

**Obligations from 3mpwrApp:**
- Dedicated relationship manager (founder or delegate)
- Quarterly check-ins
- Annual impact report for partner
- Honor exclusivity agreements (if any)

**Obligations from Partner:**
- Significant resource contribution ($ or in-kind)
- Promote 3mpwrApp to their networks
- Respect our independence (no editorial control)
- Long-term commitment (2+ years)

**How to establish:**
- Multi-month courtship period
- Legal review of agreement
- Board approval (once Governance Board exists)
- Public announcement event

**Current Tier 3 Partners:**
- None (aspirational for 2027-2028)

## ✅ Partnership Acceptance Criteria

**For ANY partnership, we evaluate on 5 dimensions:**

### 1. **Mission Alignment** (MANDATORY)

**Question:** Does this organization share our core values?

**Our Core Values:**
- Disability-led, community-first
- Privacy & data ownership
- Free access for all (no paywalls)
- No corporate exploitation
- Accessibility as foundation (WCAG AAA)
- Intersectional justice (race, class, gender, ability)

**Red Flags (automatic rejection):**
- Insurance companies (conflict of interest)
- Employers with bad disability track records
- Organizations that profit from disabled people's suffering
- Ableist language or practices
- Opposition to accessibility legislation
- History of union-busting (for union partnerships)

**Green Flags (strong alignment):**
- Led by disabled people / injured workers
- Track record of advocacy wins
- Transparent funding sources
- Intersectional approach
- Accessible communications

---

### 2. **Community Benefit** (MANDATORY)

**Question:** Will this partnership help disabled people & injured workers?

**Must achieve at least ONE of:**
- **Access:** More people discover 3mpwrApp
- **Resources:** More tools/data/support for users
- **Advocacy:** Stronger collective voice for systemic change
- **Sustainability:** Financial/volunteer support to keep platform alive

**Examples:**
- ✅ Legal clinic partnership → Free legal advice for users
- ✅ Union partnership → Access to thousands of injured workers
- ✅ University partnership → Research validates our tribunal data
- ❌ Corporate sponsor with no tangible user benefit → No

---

### 3. **No Strings Attached** (MANDATORY)

**Question:** Are we giving up control in exchange for this partnership?

**Automatic Rejection if Partner Wants:**
- Editorial control (what content we publish)
- User data access (violates privacy principles)
- Exclusive rights (e.g., "you can't work with other unions")
- Branding overrides (our brand must remain dominant)
- Feature prioritization (we decide roadmap, not them)

**Acceptable Asks:**
- Credit/attribution (e.g., "Data analysis supported by [Partner]")
- Co-branding on joint projects (but 3mpwrApp brand stays primary)
- Advisory input (we listen, but final decision is ours)

---

### 4. **Capacity Match** (HIGHLY DESIRABLE)

**Question:** Do we have capacity to honor this partnership?

**Current Reality:**
- One founder, 40 hours/week (reducing to 10 with automation)
- No staff, minimal volunteers
- Limited bandwidth for relationship management

**Capacity Assessment:**
- **Tier 1 (Supporter):** Low capacity needed (1 hour setup, 0 ongoing)
- **Tier 2 (Affiliated):** Medium capacity (5 hours setup, 1 hour/month ongoing)
- **Tier 3 (Strategic):** High capacity (20 hours setup, 5 hours/month ongoing)

**Decision Rule:**
- If we don't have capacity NOW, can we defer until we do?
- If partner requires immediate action, can they wait for us to recruit volunteers?

---

### 5. **Exit Strategy** (HIGHLY DESIRABLE)

**Question:** Can we walk away if things go wrong?

**Must define:**
- Partnership duration (1 year? 3 years? Ongoing with annual review?)
- Termination conditions (either party can exit with 30 days notice)
- What happens to shared resources (data, content, money)
- Public communication plan (how do we announce separation without drama)

**Example:**
- "This partnership is for 12 months (June 2026 - May 2027), with automatic renewal unless either party gives 30 days notice. All contributed funds are non-refundable. Joint content remains publicly available under CC-BY license."

## 🚫 Red Lines (Non-Negotiable)

**We will NEVER partner with organizations that:**

1. **Profit from workers' compensation denials** (insurers, IME clinics, defense lawyers)
2. **Oppose accessibility legislation** (anti-AODA, anti-disability rights)
3. **Require user data access** (violates privacy principles)
4. **Demand editorial control** (we answer to community, not partners)
5. **Have poor disability employment records** (check Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act compliance)
6. **Engage in union-busting** (for union partnerships—must be pro-labor)
7. **Have history of ableism** (discriminatory practices, inaccessible services)
8. **Require exclusivity that limits community benefit** (e.g., "you can only work with us, not other clinics")

**If a partner crosses a red line AFTER partnership begins:**
1. Immediate conversation to address concern
2. 30-day window to rectify
3. If not resolved, terminate partnership immediately
4. Public statement (if necessary to protect community)

## 📋 Partnership Evaluation Rubric

**Use this to decide whether to pursue a potential partnership:**

| Criteria | Weight | Score (1-5) | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------------|-------|
| **Mission Alignment** | 30% | ___ | Do they share our values? |
| **Community Benefit** | 30% | ___ | Will users benefit? |
| **No Strings Attached** | 20% | ___ | Are we keeping control? |
| **Capacity Match** | 10% | ___ | Can we handle this? |
| **Exit Strategy** | 10% | ___ | Can we leave if needed? |
| **TOTAL** | 100% | ___ | ≥3.5 = Consider; ≥4.0 = Pursue |

**How to score:**
- 5 = Excellent (strong alignment, high benefit, no concerns)
- 4 = Good (mostly aligned, clear benefit, minor concerns)
- 3 = Neutral (some alignment, moderate benefit, some concerns)
- 2 = Poor (weak alignment, unclear benefit, major concerns)
- 1 = Terrible (misaligned, no benefit, red flags)

**Decision Thresholds:**
- **≥4.0:** Actively pursue partnership
- **3.5-3.9:** Consider, but investigate concerns first
- **3.0-3.4:** Politely decline (not a good fit right now)
- **<3.0:** Hard no (fundamental misalignment)

## 🔄 Partnership Lifecycle

### **Stage 1: Inquiry**
**Trigger:** Organization reaches out OR we reach out  
**Timeline:** Day 1-7  
**Actions:**
- [ ] Log inquiry in partnerships tracking doc
- [ ] Initial conversation (email or call)
- [ ] Evaluate using rubric above
- [ ] Decision: Pursue, Defer, or Decline

---

### **Stage 2: Courtship**
**Timeline:** Day 8-60  
**Actions:**
- [ ] Share partnership policy with potential partner
- [ ] Discuss mutual goals and commitments
- [ ] Draft MOU (if Tier 2+)
- [ ] Legal review (if Tier 3)
- [ ] Get community input (if major partnership)

---

### **Stage 3: Formalization**
**Timeline:** Day 61-90  
**Actions:**
- [ ] Sign MOU or agreement
- [ ] Update website (/partnerships page)
- [ ] Public announcement (social media, press release if major)
- [ ] Onboard partner contacts (add to email list, invite to Discord)

---

### **Stage 4: Active Partnership**
**Timeline:** Ongoing  
**Actions:**
- [ ] Honor MOU commitments
- [ ] Quarterly check-ins (Tier 2) or monthly (Tier 3)
- [ ] Track metrics (users referred, events co-hosted, funds raised, etc.)
- [ ] Address conflicts promptly

---

### **Stage 5: Renewal or Termination**
**Timeline:** 60 days before MOU expiration  
**Actions:**
- [ ] Review partnership effectiveness
- [ ] Community feedback (did users benefit?)
- [ ] Decision: Renew (same terms, modified, or upgrade tier) OR Terminate
- [ ] If renewing: Sign updated MOU
- [ ] If terminating: Follow exit protocol (30 days notice, public statement if needed)

## 💬 FAQ

**Q: Can we have multiple partnerships in the same category (e.g., 2 legal clinics)?**  
A: Yes! Unless we explicitly grant exclusivity (rare, only for Tier 3 after deep consideration).

**Q: What if a partner asks for exclusivity?**  
A: Default answer is no. If we consider it, must justify why it benefits community more than non-exclusive partnership.

**Q: Can individuals be "partners" or only organizations?**  
A: Organizations only. Individuals can be volunteers, beta testers, or donors—but not formal partners.

**Q: What if Thunder Bay doesn't want to formalize?**  
A: Totally fine! They remain Tier 1 Supporter, we continue to appreciate them.

**Q: Who reviews MOUs for legal issues?**  
A: Ideally a pro-bono lawyer (ARCH Disability Law, community clinic). If none available, founder reviews + community input on major terms.
