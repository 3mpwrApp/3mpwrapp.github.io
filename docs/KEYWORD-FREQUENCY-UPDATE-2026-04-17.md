## ⚠️ Important Data Limitations

### What This Analysis Captures
✅ Keywords assigned by CanLII (3-7 summary phrases per case)
✅ Conservative minimum estimates
✅ Statistically significant patterns across large dataset
✅ Co-occurrence clustering with statistical lift

### What This Analysis Does NOT Capture
❌ Full decision text (would require CAPTCHA solving)
❌ Total frequency of terms across all decisions (only presence/absence)
❌ Causation (patterns suggest, but don't prove, systematic bias)
❌ Individual case details beyond keywords

### Why Full-Text Analysis Not Performed
- CanLII has CAPTCHA protection for web scraping
- Would require manual solving for 11,430 cases
- Playwright automation abandoned due to rate limits
- Keyword-based analysis provides defensible minimum estimates
