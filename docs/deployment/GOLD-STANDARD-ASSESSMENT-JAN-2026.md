#### Priority 3: Font Loading Strategy

## ⚖️ Part 8: Ethical Considerations

### Maintaining Community Trust

#### Avoiding Common Pitfalls

**1. Corporatization Risk**
- ❌ **Never**: Accept venture capital funding
- ❌ **Never**: Introduce paid tiers or premium features
- ✅ **Always**: Transparent funding sources
- ✅ **Always**: Community governance in major decisions

**2. Tokenization Risk**
- ❌ **Never**: Use disabled people for marketing without compensation
- ❌ **Never**: "Inspiration porn" imagery or language
- ✅ **Always**: Center disabled people as leaders and experts
- ✅ **Always**: Pay disabled consultants fairly

**3. Data Exploitation Risk**
- ❌ **Never**: Sell or share user data
- ❌ **Never**: Use dark patterns to extract information
- ✅ **Always**: Minimal data collection
- ✅ **Always**: User control over their data

**4. Accessibility Washing Risk**
- ❌ **Never**: Claim perfection (accessibility is ongoing)
- ❌ **Never**: Use accessibility as marketing while excluding disabled people
- ✅ **Always**: Acknowledge gaps and limitations
- ✅ **Always**: Continuous improvement with community feedback
