## What "Production App" Means

**Production App** = the version that goes live in the Apple App Store and Google Play Store where anyone can download it (not just beta testers).

**Your role:** Help us get there by completing **2 weeks of mandatory stress testing** required by Apple and Google before they approve the app.

**What stress testing involves:**
- **Daily usage** (15-30 min/day): Upload evidence, browse community, check events, use key features
- **Edge cases**: Try things that might break it (large file uploads, slow internet, rapid feature switching)
- **Bug reporting**: If it crashes, freezes, or acts weird, report it immediately with screenshots
- **Real-world use**: Treat it like you're fighting an actual claim - push the app hard

**Why this matters:** App stores reject apps with major bugs or crashes. Your stress testing ensures real injured workers download a stable, reliable app. You're the quality control team that protects the community.
