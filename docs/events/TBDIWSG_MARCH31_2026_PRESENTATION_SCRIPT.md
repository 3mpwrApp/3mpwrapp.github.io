>
> **[PAUSE]**
>

>
> **156 people. Same systemic problem. Zero coordination.**
>
> That's the broken system. And that's what 3mpwrApp fixes.

**[SLIDE 4: The 3 Flywheels Framework]**

**Visual:** Diagram showing 3 interconnected flywheels

> 3mpwrApp is built on what I call the **3 Flywheels of Change**.
>
> A flywheel is an engine that gets easier to spin the more momentum it has. In our case, each new user makes the system more powerful for everyone who comes after them.
>
> Let me break down each flywheel.

### **FLYWHEEL #1: The Evidence Flywheel** (6 minutes)

**[SLIDE 5: Evidence Flywheel Diagram]**

**Visual:** 
```
Evidence Collection → Win Case → Share Win → Community Inspiration
       ↑                                              ↓
       ←────────── Better Strategies ─────────────────
```

**Script:**

> **Flywheel #1 is the Evidence Flywheel.**
>
> Here's how it works:
>
> **Step 1:** You collect evidence in the app - medical records, denial letters, correspondence, photos, whatever you need for your case.
>
> **Step 2:** You win your case. CPP-D approved, accommodation granted, appeal successful.
>
> **Step 3:** The app prompts you: "Would you like to share what helped you win?" You write a quick summary - what evidence mattered, what arguments worked, what mistakes to avoid.
>
> **Step 4:** That win gets published to the community feed. Other users see it. They copy your strategy.
>
> **Step 5:** More wins. Better strategies get refined and reused.
>
> **[PAUSE - Let that sink in]**
>
> Now imagine this at scale. We have our first 20 beta testers. By the time we hit 1,000 users, you'll have hundreds of documented wins to learn from. By 10,000 users? Thousands of proven strategies.
>
> **That's the flywheel.** Each win makes the next win easier.

**[SLIDE 6: Real Example - Win Card Screenshot]**

> Here's what a win looks like in the app. This is from one of our beta testers - we'll call her Jane.
>
> Jane won her CPP-D appeal after a second denial. She documented:
> - The RFC form from her doctor was the turning point
> - She submitted a timeline showing progression of her condition
> - She included a letter from her employer confirming she couldn't perform essential duties
>
> Now, when someone else is fighting a similar battle, they can see exactly what worked for Jane. They don't have to reinvent the wheel.

### **FLYWHEEL #2: The Collective Action Flywheel** (7 minutes)

**[SLIDE 7: Collective Action Flywheel Diagram]**

**Visual:**
```
Evidence → Patterns → Campaigns → Policy Change
    ↑                                  ↓
    ←──────── Fewer Violations ────────
```

**Script:**

> **Step 2:** The app suggests a campaign: "End Fibromyalgia Claim Denials." I review it, approve it, publish it.

> **Step 5:** The app generates a joint submission - a professional policy brief documenting the pattern, backed by real anonymized data from our 62 members.
>
> **Step 6:** We send it to targets: CPP-D Review Tribunal, media, MPs, disability rights organizations.
>
> **Step 7:** Media coverage. Political pressure. Policy change.
>
> **[PAUSE]**
>

### **Common Q&A Prompts** (Prepare for these)

**Q: "Is this free?"**

> Yes, 100% free. No premium tiers, no paywalls, no ads. Ever.
>
> We're funded by grants and donations, not user fees.

**Q: "What about privacy? You're collecting a lot of sensitive data."**

> Great question. Three protections:
>
> 1. **Encryption** - All evidence encrypted on your device  
> 2. **Local-first** - Data stays on your phone unless you share it  
> 3. **Anonymization** - If you share wins or join campaigns, we strip identifying info
>

**Q: "What if I can't afford a smartphone?"**

> We're working on a web version (coming summer 2026) that works on any computer or tablet.
>
> We're also partnering with community organizations to provide loaner devices in some regions.

**Q: "Will this work in my province? I'm not in Ontario."**

> Yes! The app works Canada-wide. Templates are customized by province. Campaigns can be national or regional.
>
> We currently have users in 8 provinces.

**Q: "What happens to campaigns after they succeed?"**

> They move to "Past Campaigns" archive with:
> - Full timeline of what happened  
> - Outcome summary  
> - Lessons learned  
> - Credit to all members
>
> That way future users can study what worked.

## 🎤 Speaker Notes & Tips

**Pacing:**
- Speak slower than you think (injured workers may have cognitive fatigue)
- Pause after major points (let them absorb)
- Watch chat for questions/confusion in real-time

**Energy:**
- Enthusiastic but not overwhelming  
- Authentic and personal (tell your story)  
- Acknowledge struggles (don't minimize their pain)

**Accessibility:**
- Describe visuals ("This slide shows...")  
- High contrast slides  
- Large text  
- Offer to send slides/transcript afterward

**Handling Difficult Questions:**
- "That's a great question, and honestly I don't have the answer yet. Can I follow up with you after I research it?"  
- "I hear your concern. Here's what we're doing to address it..."  
- "You're right, that's a limitation. Here's why we made that choice and what the trade-off is..."

**If Demo Fails:**
- Have backup screenshots ready  
- "That's exactly the kind of bug we need beta testers to find!"  
- Don't panic - technical issues happen  

**Emotional Moments:**
- If someone shares a painful story, pause and acknowledge it  
- "Thank you for sharing that. I'm so sorry you went through that."  
- Validate before moving on
