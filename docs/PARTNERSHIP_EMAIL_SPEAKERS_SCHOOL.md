## 📧 Email/Board Proposal

Hi [Board Chair / Executive Director],

As a board member and alumni, I wanted to bring a partnership idea to the board:

I'm the founder of **3mpwrApp™**, a free platform that helps injured workers and disabled people navigate workers' compensation systems. Many of our users have to represent themselves at WSIB tribunals because they can't afford lawyers, and they often feel intimidated by the process.

**The Problem Our Users Face:**

- 70%+ of injured workers represent themselves at tribunals (can't afford lawyers)
- Tribunal hearings are formal, adversarial, intimidating
- Cross-examination by insurance lawyers causes anxiety
- Many workers lose benefits because they couldn't present their case confidently
- Public speaking skills = better outcomes (studies show confident self-advocates win 2x more often)

**The Opportunity for Speakers School:**

You told me Speakers School provides **advocacy, public speaking, and self-advocacy skills training** with **2 courses per year**. This is EXACTLY what injured workers need.

**What I'm Proposing:**

A partnership where Speakers School offers **"Tribunal Self-Advocacy Workshops"** for 3mpwrApp users.

**Workshop Topics Could Include:**
- How to present your case confidently at WSIB hearings
- Handling cross-examination from insurance lawyers
- Body language and vocal techniques for credibility
- Responding to hostile questions without losing composure
- Structuring your testimony for maximum impact
- Managing anxiety and stress during hearings

**Partnership Structure:**

**Option A: Free/Discounted Workshops** (Mission-Aligned)
- Speakers School offers 1-2 workshops per year for injured workers (free or low-cost)
- 3mpwrApp promotes workshops in-app and on website (reaching 10,000+ users)
- Speakers School gains mission-aligned clients (disability justice work)
- Partnership featured on both organizations' websites

**Option B: Co-Hosted Workshop Series**
- Speakers School provides trainers, 3mpwrApp handles logistics (venue/tech)
- Split costs (if any), share promotional efforts
- Certificate of completion for attendees
- Ongoing collaboration (2 events per year aligns with your course schedule)

**What Speakers School Gains:**
- Mission impact: Helping injured workers access justice
- Community visibility: 10,000+ potential participants
- Disability justice credibility: Partnership with grassroots project
- Alumni engagement: I'm alumni, this connects Speakers School to meaningful work

**What 3mpwrApp Gains:**
