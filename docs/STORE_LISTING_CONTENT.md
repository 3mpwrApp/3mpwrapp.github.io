## 📝 Full Description

### App Store Connect (4000 characters max)

**Empower your rights. Own your data. Build your future.**

3mpwr App is a free, accessible app built by and for the disability community. Whether you're navigating disability benefits, documenting workplace accommodations, or connecting with advocates, 3mpwr puts powerful tools in your hands—with complete privacy and data ownership.

**🔒 100% YOUR DATA**
Unlike other apps, 3mpwr never owns your data. Everything stays on your device unless you choose to sync to YOUR cloud (Google Drive, iCloud, OneDrive, Dropbox, AWS S3, or WebDAV). No tracking. No hidden servers. No data sales. Ever.

**✨ KEY FEATURES**

**🏠 Wellness Hub**
- Daily planner with energy tracking
- Reflection journal for mental health
- Guided exercises (breathing, grounding, gratitude)
- Mood tracking and wellness insights

**⚖️ Rights & Resources**
- Disability rights explainer (plain language)
- Appeal coach for denied benefits
- Deadline calculator with reminders
- Evidence checklist for your case
- Voice-to-text case notes
- Template gallery (letters, forms, requests)
- Support directory (advocates, legal aid, peer support)

**📢 Advocacy Tools**
- Policy Made Simple: understand laws affecting you
- Campaign coordination for community organizing
- Legislative tracker for rights-related bills
- Direct advocacy: contact your representatives

**👥 Community**
- Safe, moderated spaces for connection
- Beta Testers chat
- Regional channels (coming soon)
- Private messaging
- Events calendar

**♿ Built for Accessibility**
- Full VoiceOver, TalkBack, and NVDA support
- High contrast mode
- Dyslexia-friendly spacing and fonts
- Large text scaling (up to 200%)
- One-handed mode
- Cognitive assistance (memory aids, simplified navigation)
- Motor accessibility (dwell click, tremor filtering, large tap targets)
- Multilingual: English, Español, Français, Indigenous languages

**🤖 AI-Powered (Private & Local)**
- Legal workflow automation (benefit applications, accommodations)
- Document analysis and interpretation
- Form validation and error checking
- All AI processing happens on YOUR device—no data sent to external servers

**🔐 ENTERPRISE-GRADE SECURITY**
- AES-256 encryption
- Hardware-backed key storage
- Biometric authentication
- Anti-tampering protection
- OWASP Mobile Top 10 compliant
- Works 100% offline (air-gapped architecture)

**💡 WHY 3MPWR?**
- **Free Forever:** No subscriptions, no paywalls, no premium tiers
- **Community-Driven:** Built with input from disability rights advocates
- **Culturally Sensitive:** Indigenous language support and territorial acknowledgments
- **Privacy-First:** GDPR, CCPA, and PIPEDA compliant
- **Open & Transparent:** Verify our privacy claims through code inspection

**🏅 PERFECT FOR:**
- Individuals applying for or appealing disability benefits
- People documenting workplace accommodations or discrimination
- Disability rights advocates and organizers
- Anyone navigating complex legal or administrative processes
- Community members seeking connection and peer support
- Organizations supporting the disability community

## 📸 Screenshot Requirements

### iOS App Store
- **Required sizes:**
  - iPhone 6.7" (1290 x 2796 pixels) - iPhone 15 Pro Max, 14 Pro Max, 13 Pro Max, 12 Pro Max
  - iPhone 6.5" (1242 x 2688 pixels) - iPhone 11 Pro Max, XS Max
  - iPhone 5.5" (1242 x 2208 pixels) - iPhone 8 Plus, 7 Plus, 6s Plus
  - iPad Pro 12.9" (2048 x 2732 pixels) - 3rd gen and later
- **Count:** 3-10 screenshots per size
- **Format:** PNG or JPEG

### Google Play
- **Required sizes:**
  - Phone: 16:9 aspect ratio (recommended: 1080 x 1920 pixels)
  - Tablet 7": 16:9 or 16:10 (recommended: 1920 x 1080 pixels)
  - Tablet 10": 16:9 or 16:10 (recommended: 1920 x 1200 pixels)
- **Count:** 2-8 screenshots per device type
- **Format:** PNG or JPEG (max 8 MB each)
- **Feature Graphic:** 1024 x 500 pixels (required for Play Store listing)

### Recommended Screenshot Content
1. **Home/Dashboard:** Show main navigation and wellness overview
2. **Resources Hub:** Display Rights Checker or Evidence Checklist
3. **Advocacy Tools:** Policy Made Simple or Campaign view
4. **Community:** Chat or events calendar
5. **Accessibility:** High contrast mode or large text
6. **Privacy:** Data ownership guarantee or BYOC settings
7. **Wellness:** Daily planner or reflection journal
8. **AI Features:** Legal workflow automation or document analysis

**Design Tips:**
- Use device frames for context
- Include descriptive captions (1-2 lines per screenshot)
- Highlight accessibility features prominently
- Show diverse user scenarios
- Emphasize privacy and data ownership
- Use high contrast for readability
- Avoid text-heavy screenshots

## 📱 App Configuration

### Expo app.json
```json
{
  "expo": {
    "name": "3mpwr App",
    "slug": "empowrapp-new",
    "version": "1.0.0",
    "ios": {
      "bundleIdentifier": "com.empowrapp.app",
      "buildNumber": "1"
    },
    "android": {
      "package": "com.empowrapp2.empowrapp",
      "versionCode": 1
    }
  }
}
```

### EAS Build Configuration
Add to `eas.json`:
```json
{
  "build": {
    "production": {
      "ios": {
        "resourceClass": "m1-medium"
      },
      "android": {
        "buildType": "app-bundle"
      }
    }
  }
}
```
