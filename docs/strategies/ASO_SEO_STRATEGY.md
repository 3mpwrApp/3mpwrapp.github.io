# App Store Optimization (ASO) Strategy

## Overview

This document outlines the ASO/SEO strategy for 3mpwr App on Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

---

## App Title & Subtitle

### Google Play (50 char title, 80 char short desc)
```
Title: 3mpwr - Disability Advocacy & Wellness
Short: Manage health, track deadlines & advocate for your rights. Accessible.
```

### Apple App Store (30 char title, 30 char subtitle)
```
Title: 3mpwr Disability Advocate
Subtitle: Health & Wellness Tracker
```

```

3mpwr (Empower) is your all-in-one companion for managing disability life—from tracking your health to fighting for your rights.

✨ WHY 3MPWR?

Built by people with disabilities who understand the daily challenges of:
• Managing chronic illness symptoms
• Fighting benefit denials
• Tracking endless appointments & deadlines
• Explaining your condition to others
• Pacing your limited energy

🎯 KEY FEATURES

📊 WELLNESS TRACKING
• Mood & energy tracking with spoon theory support
• Symptom logging with visual patterns
• Medication reminders & tracking
• Sleep quality monitoring
• Pacing tools to prevent crashes

📋 ADVOCACY TOOLS
• AI-powered letter generator for appeals
• Document translator (bureaucratic → plain language)
• Evidence locker for medical records
• Deadline tracker with reminders
• Rights & benefits information

🤝 COMMUNITY
• Connect with others who understand
• Share advocacy wins & strategies
• Anonymous support groups
• Peer resource sharing

♿ ACCESSIBILITY FIRST
• Full screen reader support (TalkBack/VoiceOver)
• High contrast mode
• Adjustable text sizes
• Energy-aware interface
• Complexity modes (Simple/Standard/Power User)

🔒 PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Your data stays on YOUR device
• No selling data to advertisers
• Optional cloud backup (encrypted)
• HIPAA-conscious design
• Clear all data anytime

Unlike generic health apps, 3mpwr understands that disability life involves:
• Fighting for benefits you're entitled to
• Explaining the same things repeatedly
• Managing unpredictable symptoms
• Conserving limited energy
• Advocating in a system not built for you

We built the app we wished existed.

💪 JOIN THE MOVEMENT

3mpwr is more than an app—it's a community of advocates supporting each other. Download now and take control of your disability journey.

Made with ❤️ by the disability community, for the disability community.
```

## Visual Assets

### App Icon
- Clean, recognizable design
- Works at all sizes (small notification icons)
- Represents empowerment/advocacy
- Accessible color contrast

### Screenshots Strategy (8 screenshots)

1. **Hero Shot**: Main dashboard with key features visible
2. **Wellness**: Mood/energy tracking in action
3. **Advocacy**: Letter generator or translator
4. **Evidence**: Document organization
5. **Community**: Thread/support view
6. **Accessibility**: Settings showing a11y options
7. **Power Tools**: Consolidated feature view
8. **Simple Mode**: Clean, uncluttered interface

## Review Strategy

### Prompt Timing
- After completing first week of tracking
- After generating first advocacy letter
- After positive app interactions
- Never during frustrating moments

### Response Template
```
Thank you for your feedback! We're a small team building this for our 
community, and reviews like yours help other disabled folks find us. 
[Address specific concern if negative]. 
— The 3mpwr Team ♿💪
```
