## 📋 Executive Summary

**3mpwr** is establishing a dual-entity structure to protect intellectual property, ensure mission permanence, and enable sustainable growth while keeping the app **forever free** to users.

### The Structure:
1. **3mpwr Foundation** (Canadian Registered Charity) - Mission & IP Owner
2. **3mpwr Technologies Inc.** (For-Profit Subsidiary) - Operations & Revenue

### Core Principle:
> "Technology that empowers the disability community must be accessible to all, funded by those who can afford to support the mission, not by exploiting user data or charging those who need help most."

## 🎯 Mission Statement

### 3mpwr Foundation

**Mission:**  
To empower people with disabilities through accessible technology, mutual aid, and advocacy tools that preserve dignity, autonomy, and community connection.

**Vision:**  
A world where disability is not a barrier to accessing support, resources, or justice—where technology serves people, not profits.

**Core Values:**
1. **Accessibility First** - Technology designed with and for disabled people
2. **Privacy as a Right** - User data belongs to users, period
3. **Mutual Aid** - Community support over corporate extraction
4. **Radical Transparency** - Open financials, open roadmap, open governance
5. **Nothing About Us Without Us** - Disabled leadership at all levels

**Commitment:**  
The 3mpwr App will remain **free forever** to end users. No paywalls, no data selling, no ads. Revenue from optional services and partnerships funds free access for all.

## 🏢 Dual Entity Structure

### Entity 1: 3mpwr Foundation (Nonprofit)

**Legal Structure:** Canadian Federally Incorporated Charity  
**CRA Charitable Purpose:** Relief of poverty, advancement of education, other purposes beneficial to the community (disability support)

**Owns & Controls:**
- ✅ **All Intellectual Property** (app, brand, code, data models)
- ✅ **3mpwr Trademark & Logo**
- ✅ **Mission & Values** (enshrined in bylaws)
- ✅ **Controlling Interest** in 3mpwr Technologies Inc. (51%+)
- ✅ **User Data Governance** (privacy policies, ethical guidelines)

**Activities:**
- Grant applications & fundraising
- Donor relations & tax receipts
- Advocacy campaigns & policy work
- Community governance & user councils
- Research & accessibility standards
- Mutual Aid Fund administration

**Governance:**
- Board of Directors: 7 members (minimum 51% with lived disability experience)
- User Advisory Council: 12-15 members from disability community
- Annual General Meetings: Public & accessible
- Transparency: Annual reports, audited financials online

**Tax Status:** Tax-exempt, can issue charitable receipts

---

### Entity 2: 3mpwr Technologies Inc. (For-Profit)

**Legal Structure:** Canadian Corporation (BC or Federal)  
**Ownership:** 51% 3mpwr Foundation, 49% impact investors/founders (optional)

**Purpose:**
Generate sustainable revenue to fund Foundation's charitable mission while maintaining operational flexibility.

**Revenue Streams** (See FINANCIAL_OVERVIEW.md):
1. **B2B Licensing**: Corporate accessibility training, HR tools ($999-9,999/year)
2. **Premium Services**: AI legal tools for lawyers/advocates ($49/month)
3. **API Access**: Accessibility data for researchers ($299/month)
4. **Consulting**: Custom integrations for organizations ($5,000-25,000/project)
5. **Grants & Sponsorships**: Corporate ESG partnerships

**Profit Distribution:**
- 100% of profits transfer to 3mpwr Foundation as charitable donations
- Foundation uses funds for:
  - Free app operations (Firebase, hosting, updates)
  - Mutual Aid Fund grants
  - Accessibility grants for community projects
  - Staff salaries (Foundation employees)

**Key Protection:**
- Cannot sell IP (owned by Foundation)
- Cannot change mission (bylaws protected)
- Cannot IPO or VC exit (Foundation majority control)
- Cannot introduce user fees (licensed to Foundation)

## 📜 Founding Documents

### Document 1: Articles of Incorporation (3mpwr Foundation)

**Name:** 3mpwr Foundation / Fondation 3mpwr  
**Jurisdiction:** Canada (Federal - CNCA)  
**Purpose:** 
1. To relieve poverty among persons with disabilities by providing free access to technology, resources, and advocacy tools
2. To advance education in the field of disability rights, accessibility, and assistive technology
3. To support the physical and mental well-being of persons with disabilities through community connection and mutual aid programs

**Powers:**
- Receive and maintain funds for charitable purposes
- Own intellectual property and license to affiliated entities
- Establish for-profit subsidiaries for revenue generation
- Accept donations, grants, and bequests
- Issue charitable tax receipts

**Dissolution Clause:**  
Upon dissolution, all assets (including IP) transfer to another Canadian charity serving the disability community. Assets cannot be distributed to private individuals.

---

### Document 2: Foundation Bylaws (Key Provisions)

#### Article I: Mission Protection
1. The 3mpwr App shall remain free to end users in perpetuity
2. User data shall not be sold, shared, or monetized without explicit consent
3. The mission and core values cannot be amended without:
   - 75% Board approval
   - Majority approval from User Advisory Council
   - Public consultation period (90 days)

#### Article II: Board Composition
1. **Size:** 7-11 directors (odd number)
2. **Disability Representation:** Minimum 51% must have lived disability experience
3. **Diversity Requirements:** 
   - At least 30% from equity-seeking groups (BIPOC, 2SLGBTQ+, low-income)
   - At least 30% women/non-binary
   - Age diversity (minimum 1 member under 30, 1 over 60)
4. **Term Limits:** 3-year terms, maximum 3 consecutive terms (9 years)
5. **Compensation:** Directors serve without pay (expense reimbursement only)

#### Article III: User Advisory Council
1. **Purpose:** Ensure user voices shape product decisions
2. **Composition:** 12-15 members from disability community
3. **Selection:** Open application + random lottery for equity
4. **Powers:**
   - Veto new features that harm accessibility
   - Recommend roadmap priorities
   - Review privacy policy changes
   - Allocate Mutual Aid Fund grants

#### Article IV: Conflict of Interest
1. No director may have financial interest in 3mpwr Technologies Inc. beyond Foundation's controlling stake
2. Directors must recuse from votes involving personal benefit
3. Annual disclosure of all affiliations

#### Article V: Financial Transparency
1. Annual audited financials published online
2. Quarterly reports to User Advisory Council
3. Real-time donation tracking dashboard (optional)
4. Salaries of top 5 paid positions disclosed annually

## 🛡️ Intellectual Property Protection Strategy

### 1. Copyright Protection (Automatic)

**What's Protected:**
- Source code (all 50,000+ lines)
- UI/UX designs and graphics
- Documentation and written content
- Database schema and API designs

**Copyright Notice:**
```
© 2025 3mpwr Foundation. All rights reserved.

This software is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0)
for non-commercial use. Commercial use requires a separate license from 3mpwr Foundation.

For licensing inquiries: legal@3mpwr.org
```

**Registration:** File with Canadian Intellectual Property Office (CIPO) - $50 fee

---

### 2. Trademark Registration

**Primary Mark:** "3mpwr"  
**Classes:**
- Class 9: Computer software, mobile applications
- Class 42: Software as a service (SaaS)
- Class 45: Personal support services for disabled persons

**Geographic Coverage:**
- **Phase 1:** Canada (CIPO) - $330 per class
- **Phase 2:** United States (USPTO) - $350 per class
- **Phase 3:** International (Madrid Protocol) - $1,000+

**Timeline:** 12-18 months for registration  
**Cost (Canada):** ~$1,000 (3 classes + legal fees)

**Secondary Marks to Protect:**
- "Spoon Economist" (wellness feature)
- "Evidence Locker" (legal tool)
- "Mutual Aid Fund" (charitable program)

---

### 3. Trade Secret Protection

**What Qualifies:**
- AI prompt engineering techniques
- User engagement algorithms
- Accessibility scoring methodology
- Crisis detection models

**Protection Measures:**
- Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) for all contractors
- Limited code access (need-to-know basis)
- Encrypted repositories with audit logs
- Annual trade secret audits

---

### 4. Open Source Strategy (Dual Licensing)

**Public License:** GNU AGPL-3.0  
- Anyone can use, modify, distribute
- Must keep source code open
- Cannot create closed-source derivative
- Perfect for community contributions

**Commercial License:** Available from Foundation  
- For-profit companies pay licensing fee
- Can create closed-source integrations
- White-label solutions for enterprises
- Revenue supports free app

**Example:**
```
Community organization wants to adapt 3mpwr for seniors:
→ Free under AGPL (must share their changes)

Hospital system wants internal version with proprietary integrations:
→ $25,000/year commercial license from Foundation
```

## 🔐 Risk Mitigation

### Risk 1: CRA Charitable Status Denied

**Likelihood:** Low (disability support is clearly charitable)  
**Mitigation:**
- Hire experienced charity lawyer
- Provide detailed charitable purposes
- Include poverty relief component (free app)
- Prepare detailed program descriptions

**Backup Plan:**
- Operate as nonprofit corporation without charitable status
- Re-apply with stronger application
- Partner with existing charity for fiscal sponsorship

---

### Risk 2: Trademark Opposition

**Likelihood:** Low (comprehensive search shows "3mpwr" is unique)  
**Mitigation:**
- Professional trademark search before filing
- File "intent to use" early
- Monitor opposition period closely
- Budget for potential legal defense

**Backup Plan:**
- Rebrand to alternative name
- Use "Empowr App" or "DisabilityMutualAid"
- Community vote on new name

---

### Risk 3: Founder Departure

**Likelihood:** Medium (life happens)  
**Mitigation:**
- IP already assigned to Foundation (protected)
- Board majority can continue operations
- Succession planning for key roles
- Documentation of all systems and processes

**Backup Plan:**
- Recruit replacement Board members
- Hire contract developers if needed
- Foundation owns all IP (no individual dependency)

---

### Risk 4: Revenue Shortfall (Technologies)

**Likelihood:** Medium (B2B sales uncertain)  
**Mitigation:**
- Diversified funding (grants, donations, revenue)
- Zero-cost operation mode available (see FINANCIAL_OVERVIEW.md)
- Technologies can operate at break-even (no profit requirement)
- Foundation has contingency fund (20% of budget)

**Backup Plan:**
- Scale back to free tier services only
- Pause paid features development
- Focus on grant fundraising
- Reduce operational costs (use free tools)

---

### Risk 5: Mission Drift Over Time

**Likelihood:** Low (bylaws protections strong)  
**Mitigation:**
- **Supermajority requirement** for mission changes (75% Board + User Council)
- **Dissolution clause** ensures IP goes to similar charity
- **User Advisory Council** veto power on harmful features
- **Annual transparency reports** keep community informed
- **Irrevocable IP assignment** prevents sale

**Backup Plan:**
- Community can fork codebase (AGPL license)
- User Advisory Council can petition to remove Board
- CRA can revoke charitable status if mission violated (reputational damage)
