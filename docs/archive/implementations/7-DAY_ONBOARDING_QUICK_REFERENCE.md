## 🗓️ Day-by-Day Breakdown

### Day 1: Getting Started & Your First Save ⭐
**Status:** Foundation building  
**Location:** Advocacy → Translator → Resources → Evidence Locker  
**What You Do:**
1. Set up accessibility settings (High Contrast, Text Size, Screen Reader)
2. Use AI Translator on a confusing letter
3. Save the simplified version
4. Create a note about an important conversation

**Key Skills:**
- Navigate between tabs
- Use AI tools
- Save and tag information

**Success:** You have 1 saved note and know where Evidence Locker is

---

### Day 2: Wellness Check-In & Energy Tracking 🌟
**Status:** Understanding yourself  
**Location:** Wellness tab  
**What You Do:**
1. Set up Daily Energy Coins (start with 10-12)
2. Plan today's activities by "spending" coins
3. Use Reflections Calendar to track mood
4. Set up daily mood check-ins

**Key Skills:**
- Recognize energy limits
- Budget time/energy like money
- Track patterns over time

**Success:** You have 1 day of mood tracked and understand Energy Coins

---

### Day 3: Understanding Your Rights & Documents ⚖️
**Status:** Knowledge + power  
**Location:** Advocacy → Policy Made Simple → Resources → Master Letter Generator  
**What You Do:**
1. Read about your workplace accommodation rights
2. Create a professional accommodation request letter
3. Save a copy for records
4. Understand your legal foundation

**Key Skills:**
- Know your rights
- Create professional documents
- Document formal requests

**Success:** You have 1 professional letter generated and saved

---

### Day 4: Building Your Support Network 🤝
**Status:** Connection + community  
**Location:** Community tab  
**What You Do:**
1. Browse your provincial community
2. Read posts related to your situation
3. Understand community norms
4. Make your first post asking a question or sharing

**Key Skills:**
- Navigate community safely
- Read and understand others' experiences
- Post without sharing personal details
- Build connections

**Success:** You've made 1 post and read 5+ community posts

---

### Day 5: Making Your Documents Count 📋
**Status:** Organization + preparation  
**Location:** Resources → Evidence Locker & Doctor Visit Prep  
**What You Do:**
1. Create multiple organized notes (medical, work, impact)
2. Use tags to organize (medical, workplace, impact, evidence)
3. Create a doctor visit prep document
4. Export or print for an appointment

**Key Skills:**
- Organize evidence systematically
- Create summaries
- Prepare for important meetings
- Export documents

**Success:** You have 5+ organized notes and 1 prepared summary

---

### Day 6: Advocacy in Action 💪
**Status:** Taking steps forward  
**Location:** Advocacy → Legal Workflow Automation  
**What You Do:**
1. Browse available workflows
2. Choose one matching your situation
3. Start the workflow (complete Steps 1-2)
4. Understand your action plan
5. Track deadlines and progress

**Key Skills:**
- Identify your advocacy goal
- Follow a step-by-step process
- Understand deadlines
- Track progress toward completion

**Success:** You've started 1 workflow and understand next steps

---

### Day 7: Celebration & Building Habits 🎉
**Status:** Reflection + continuation  
**Location:** Home → Disability Wizard & Wellness → Reflections  
**What You Do:**
1. Explore Disability Wizard recommendations
2. Try a recommended feature
3. Reflect on week in Reflections Calendar
4. Plan how to keep using the app

**Key Skills:**
- Understand personalization
- Reflect on progress
- Build sustainable habits
- Know how to continue forward

**Success:** You've documented your progress and have a plan

## ⚙️ Accessibility Setup Quick Guide

**Do this FIRST on Day 1:**

### For Vision Needs
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **High Contrast** (ON)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Text Size** (adjust slider)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Colors** (if specific colors hard to see)

### For Hearing Needs
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Captions** (ON)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Visual Alerts** (ON)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Vibration** (ON)

### For Motor Needs
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Larger Touch Targets** (ON)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Dwell Click** (if available)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **One-Handed Mode** (if needed)

### For Cognitive Needs
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Simplified Mode** (if overwhelming)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Reduce Motion** (ON)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Text Simplification** (ON)

### For Dyslexia Support
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Dyslexia Font** (choose OpenDyslexic)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Letter Spacing** (increase)
- **Settings** → Accessibility → **Overlay Color** (choose preference)

## ❓ FAQ: First Week Edition

**Q: Can I skip a day?**
A: Absolutely! This is YOUR pace. Go through whenever works for you.

**Q: What if I don't understand something?**
A: Each section has explanations. Try it, and if confused, ask community.

**Q: Can I go faster than 1 day per day?**
A: Yes! But be gentle with yourself. Learning takes time.

**Q: Do I have to do all 7 days?**
A: No, but each day builds on the last. Going through all 7 helps you understand the full power.

**Q: What if I make a mistake?**
A: You can edit or delete most things. It's okay to experiment.

**Q: Will my information be safe?**
A: Yes. All your information stays on your phone unless you share it.

**Q: Can I go back to earlier days?**
A: Yes! The user guide stays there. Return anytime you want to review.
