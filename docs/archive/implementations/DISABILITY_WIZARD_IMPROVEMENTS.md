# Disability Wizard - Comprehensive App Improvements

## 🎯 Overview

The **Disability Wizard** transforms the personalization engine into a disability-aware guidance system that creates a seamless, interconnected experience tailored to each user's unique needs.

---

## ✨ What's New

### 1. **Disability-Aware Personalization**

Instead of generic recommendations, the wizard now considers:
- **Disability types** (physical, cognitive, sensory, mental health, chronic illness, neurodivergent)
- **Energy levels** throughout the day
- **Cognitive load** capacity
- **Accessibility needs** (screen reader, simplified language, etc.)
- **Support level** required

**Example:** A user with ADHD during a low-energy afternoon will see:
- ✅ Simple, quick tasks (mood tracker, translator)
- ❌ NOT complex workflows that require sustained focus

### 2. **Daily Feature Rotation**

- **Different features each day** based on day of week
- **Personalized to YOUR patterns** (not random)
- **Prevents recommendation fatigue** - you won't see the same thing repeatedly
- **Gradual feature discovery** - new features introduced naturally

**How it works:**
- Monday: Featured tools might be Coach + Evidence Locker
- Tuesday: Translator + Legal Workflow
- Wednesday: Resource Hub + Wellness
- (Adapts to your usage - if you love mood tracker, it appears more often)

### 3. **Smart Interconnections**

Every feature now knows its "flow" - what naturally comes next:

```
Evidence Locker → Legal Workflow → Advocate Finder
     ↓
  Coach
     ↓
Letter Generator
```

**Benefits:**
- Smooth transitions between tasks
- Contextual suggestions: "You documented evidence - ready to start the legal workflow?"
- Related tools surfaced at the right moment

### 4. **Context-Aware Intelligence**

The wizard detects:
- **Time of day** (morning = high focus tasks, evening = wellness)
- **Stress levels** (app switching patterns suggest stress → prioritize wellness)
- **Energy patterns** (learns your peak hours)
- **Completion patterns** (suggests finishing what you started)
- **Consecutive days** (streak bonuses for engagement)

### 5. **Transparent Reasoning**

Every suggestion shows WHY it's recommended:
- 🎯 "Designed for cognitive support"
- ⚡ "Perfect for your energy level right now"
- 🌅 "Great morning activity"
- ⭐ "Today's featured tool"
- 🆕 "New feature to explore"

## 🎨 User Experience Improvements

### A. Personalized Onboarding

**First-time Setup:**
1. "Tell us about your needs" (optional, skip-friendly)
2. Select disability types (if comfortable)
3. Set energy peak hours
4. Choose accessibility preferences
5. Done! Wizard is personalized

**Why it matters:**
- No one-size-fits-all
- Respects privacy (all optional)
- Immediate value

### B. Daily Welcome Experience

**Every morning:**
```
☀️ Good morning, [Name]!

Today's suggestions for you:
─────────────────────────
🎯 AI Accountability Coach
   "Perfect for your energy level | Monday favorite"
   15 min | Medium energy

🌱 Wellness Exercises  
   "Start your day right | Stress relief"
   10 min | Light

📁 Evidence Locker
   "Continue documenting your case"
   10 min | Medium
```

### C. Interconnected Workflows

**Example Flow: Complete Advocacy Journey**

1. **Start:** Mood Tracker (feeling overwhelmed)
   - Wizard suggests: "Need support? Try AI Coach"

2. **Next:** AI Accountability Coach
   - Builds action plan
   - Wizard suggests: "Translate confusing documents?"

3. **Then:** Case Translator
   - Makes sense of legal jargon
   - Wizard suggests: "Save evidence in locker?"

4. **Continue:** Evidence Locker
   - Documents everything
   - Wizard suggests: "Ready for legal workflow?"

5. **Finally:** Legal Workflow
   - Structured process
   - Wizard suggests: "Find an advocate to help?"

**Each step flows naturally to the next!**

### D. Accessibility-First Design

All wizard suggestions show:
- ⏱️ **Time estimate** (respects limited energy)
- ⚡ **Energy level** (low/medium/high)
- 🧠 **Cognitive load** (light/moderate/heavy)
- ♿ **Accessibility features** (screen reader ready, voice input, etc.)

**Visual aids:**
- Color-coded energy levels
- Icons for quick scanning
- Large tap targets (min 44x44)
- High contrast mode support

### E. Stress Detection & Relief

Wizard detects when you might be stressed:
- Rapid app switching
- Many incomplete actions
- Repeatedly viewing same page

**Response:**
- Prioritizes wellness tools
- Suggests breathing exercises
- Recommends peer support
- Offers simplified tasks

## 🎨 Design Principles

### 1. **Never Overwhelming**
- Show 3 suggestions max at once
- "See more" for additional options
- Always option to dismiss/skip

### 2. **Always Optional**
- Every suggestion is optional
- Easy to ignore
- No penalties for skipping
- Can disable categories

### 3. **Transparent**
- Clear why something is suggested
- Show how it helps
- Explain interconnections
- Privacy-respecting

### 4. **Accessible by Default**
- Screen reader friendly
- Keyboard navigable
- High contrast support
- Reduced motion respect
- Cognitive load indicators

### 5. **Disability-Positive**
- Language emphasizes capability
- Respects limitations without judgment
- Celebrates progress
- Assumes competence
