## 📋 Executive Summary

**3mpwr App** is a privacy-first empowerment platform designed for people with disabilities and chronic conditions in Canada. Built with enterprise-grade security, Indigenous language support, and cutting-edge AI wellness features, it provides comprehensive tools for advocacy, wellness management, community support, and legal assistance.

### Key Differentiators
- ✅ **100% Privacy-First**: All data encrypted with AES-256, optional air-gapped mode
- ✅ **AI-Powered Wellness**: Machine learning that learns YOUR patterns, not population averages
- ✅ **Cultural Inclusivity**: 6 Indigenous languages, traditional protocols integrated
- ✅ **Legal Automation**: 22 letter templates, 14-jurisdiction coverage across Canada
- ✅ **WCAG AAA Compliant**: Industry-leading accessibility for all disabilities
- ✅ **Tiny & Fast**: 3.0 MB bundle, sub-2s startup with Hermes engine

## 📊 Demo Script Tips

### Do's:
✅ Start with a relatable pain point (lost benefits, missed deadline, isolation)
✅ Show real workflows (log a mood, generate a letter, find a peer)
✅ Emphasize privacy at every turn ("Your data, your control")
✅ Use inclusive language ("people with disabilities" not "disabled people")
✅ Celebrate small wins (streak badges, achievements)
✅ Acknowledge limitations (AI can be wrong, always verify legal advice)

### Don'ts:
❌ Rush through features (depth > breadth)
❌ Use technical jargon (speak to non-technical audience)
❌ Skip accessibility demo (it's a core differentiator)
❌ Ignore cultural features (Indigenous support is unique)
❌ Overpromise AI ("AI-assisted" not "AI-powered legal advice")
❌ Forget disclaimers (medical, legal, financial disclaimers are CRITICAL)

## 🎤 Q&A Preparation

### Common Questions:

**Q: Is my data really private?**
A: Yes. All data is encrypted with AES-256 and stored locally on YOUR device. You can operate 100% offline (air-gapped mode) or choose ANY cloud provider you trust (BYOC mode). We never see your data, never sell your data, and all tracking is opt-in (OFF by default).

**Q: How does the AI work?**
A: All AI computation happens on YOUR device using YOUR data. We use machine learning algorithms that learn your unique patterns (energy rhythms, mood triggers, pacing needs). It requires 7+ days of data for accurate predictions. It's privacy-preserving - no data leaves your phone unless you explicitly choose cloud sync.

**Q: Can I use this if I don't speak English?**
A: Yes! The app supports English, French, Spanish, and 6 Indigenous languages (Plains Cree, Ojibwe, Inuktitut, Mi'kmaq, Mohawk, Dene). We're adding more languages based on community requests.

**Q: Is this legal advice?**
A: No. The app provides educational information and helps you draft letters using templates. It does NOT provide legal advice, create attorney-client relationships, or guarantee legal outcomes. Always consult a licensed lawyer for legal matters.

**Q: Does it work with my screen reader?**
A: Yes! The app is WCAG AAA compliant with full screen reader support (TalkBack on Android, VoiceOver on iOS). All interactive elements have proper ARIA labels, semantic HTML, and keyboard navigation.

**Q: How much does it cost?**
A: The app is currently in closed beta (invitation only). Pricing for public release has not been announced, but we're committed to keeping it affordable and offering free tiers for those who can't pay.

**Q: What provinces are supported?**
A: All 14 Canadian jurisdictions: Federal, 10 provinces (ON, BC, AB, SK, MB, QC, NB, NS, PE, NL), and 3 territories (YT, NT, NU). Each jurisdiction has specific forms, deadlines, and benefit programs documented.

**Q: Can I export my data?**
A: Yes! You can export all your data at any time (CSV, PDF, JSON formats). You own your data - no lock-in, no fees, no restrictions.
