**9 Core Features Introduced Across 7 Days:**

1. **Accessibility Settings** (Day 1)
   - High Contrast, Text Size, Screen Reader
   - Motor & Cognitive accessibility options

2. **AI Advocate Translator** (Day 1)
   - Turn confusing text into simple English
   - Saves simplified version automatically

3. **Evidence Locker** (Days 1, 5)
   - Save important documents and notes
   - Use tags to organize
   - Export when needed

   - Budget energy like money

   - Avoid over-commitment

   - Track mood and patterns
   - Export for healthcare providers
   - Celebrate progress

   - Create 22 types of professional letters
   - Pre-filled templates
   - Legally sound documents

   - Connect with others
   - Share experiences safely
   - Get advice and support

8. **Legal Workflows** (Day 6)
   - Step-by-step process guidance
   - Track deadlines
   - Auto-generated documents

   - Learns your patterns

## 💡 Real-World Example

### Sarah's First Week Journey

**Day 1 (Monday):**
- Enables High Contrast (easier to read)
- Uses AI Translator on confusing insurance letter
- Saves it to Evidence Locker with tag "insurance"

**Day 2 (Tuesday):**
- Sets Energy Coins at 12 for the day
- Plans: emails (1), doctor call (2), rest (0) = 3 coins left
- Tracks mood as "okay"

**Day 3 (Wednesday):**
- Reads about accommodation rights
- Creates professional workplace accommodation letter
- Saves to Evidence Locker, tags "legal"

**Day 4 (Thursday):**
- Reads posts in community about workplace issues
- Makes first post: "Just requested accommodation at my job"
- Gets supportive replies from others

**Day 5 (Friday):**
- Organizes all her medical notes with tags
- Creates doctor visit prep document
- Feels ready for Monday appointment

**Day 6 (Saturday):**
- Starts "Disability Accommodation Request" workflow
- Completes first 2 steps
- Sees clear path to goal

**Day 7 (Sunday):**
- Sees Disability Wizard recommendations
- Reflects on week: "Feeling more prepared and less alone"
- Plans to follow through on workflow next week

**Result:** 2 weeks later, Sarah's accommodation was approved. She credits the app for organizing her evidence and giving her confidence.
