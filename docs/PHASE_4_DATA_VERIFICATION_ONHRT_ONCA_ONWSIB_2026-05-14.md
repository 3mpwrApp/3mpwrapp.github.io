## 🔍 Why This Matters

### 1. **ONSBT is NOT for injured workers**
- ONSBT handles **ODSP (Ontario Disability Support Program)** eligibility appeals
- Recipients include: people with mental health conditions, chronic illness, developmental disabilities, autism, cerebral palsy, etc.
- Many ONSBT appellants have **never been injured at work**

### 2. **HRTO serves the broader disability community**
- Human rights discrimination claims
- Workplace accommodation denials (not injury-related)
- Housing, services, goods discrimination

### 3. **User Memory Note (Critical):**
From [user-style-3mpwr.md](/memories/user-style-3mpwr.md):
> "3mpwr language must consistently speak to injured workers and the broader disability community, not injured workers alone."
> "Inclusive wording should also mention advocates/supporters when relevant."

### 4. **Data Speaks for Itself:**
- **57.7% of tribunal data** (ONSBT + HRTO) serves the disability community beyond workplace injury
- Only **28.6% of data** (WSIAT) is exclusively workplace injury

**Current language does not reflect this reality.**
