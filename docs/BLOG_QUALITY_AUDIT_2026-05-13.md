### ✅ EXCELLENT Voice Examples (Use These as Templates)

**From Beta Tester Contribution:**
> "One email. One question about implementation. **Everything changed.**"

### ✅ Core Voice Principles (Consistently Applied Across Posts)

1. **"We" Voice** — Collective injured worker/disability community perspective
2. **Data Transparency** — Always disclose limitations upfront
3. **Plain Language** — Define jargon immediately, use short sentences
4. **Human Impact** — Connect numbers to real consequences
5. **Professional but Personal** — Credible analysis with authentic experience
6. **Action-Oriented** — Give readers next steps

### ❌ Voice Pitfalls to Avoid

1. ❌ Academic tone ("Analysis reveals statistical significance...")
2. ❌ Defensive framing ("FACTS vs EXTRAPOLATION" headers)
3. ❌ Policy wonk jargon without translation
4. ❌ Hiding behind data instead of leading with impact
