## Installation & Signup

### Step 1: Download the App

**📱 iOS (iPhone/iPad):**
1. Open Apple App Store
2. Tap Search (magnifying glass at bottom)
3. Type "Empowr" in search box
4. Tap the Empowr app (purple icon)
5. Tap "Get" (or cloud icon if already installed)
6. Use Face ID or Apple ID to confirm
7. Wait for "Installed" confirmation
8. Tap "Open" to launch

**Android (Samsung, Google Pixel, etc.):**
1. Open Google Play Store app
2. Tap Search icon at bottom
3. Type "Empowr"
4. Tap the Empowr result
5. Tap "Install"
6. Grant permissions when asked
7. Wait for installation
8. Tap "Open"

**🌐 Web Browser:**
- Visit: [www.empowrapp.org](https://www.empowrapp.org)
- No download or installation needed
- Works in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge
- Bookmark for quick access next time
- Tip: Add to home screen (tap share → Add to Home Screen) for app-like experience

**💾 Minimum Requirements:**
- **Storage**: At least 200 MB free space
- **Internet**: WiFi or mobile data (though app works offline for saved data)
- **Device**: iPhone 12+, Android 10+, or modern browser

**🚫 Storage Full?**
- On iPhone: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → delete unused apps/photos
- On Android: Settings → Storage → delete unused apps/photos
- Free up at least 500 MB for best performance

### Step 2: Create Your Account

**Option A: Email Signup (Recommended)**
1. Tap "Sign Up" or "Create Account"
2. Enter your email address (will receive confirmation)
3. Create a strong password:
   - At least 8 characters
   - Mix of letters, numbers, symbols (e.g., P@ssw0rd!)
   - **Password tips**: Don't use birthdate, pet name, or repeated characters
4. Confirm your password (type it again)
5. Check your email for verification link:
   - Look in inbox and spam/junk folder
   - Click the confirmation link
   - Should be received within 5 minutes
6. Return to app (link takes you back automatically)
7. Set up your profile:
   - First name (or nickname for privacy)
   - Last name (optional)
   - Profile photo (optional - tap camera icon)
   - Bio/About me (optional - 200 characters max)
   - Interests/areas of focus (select 2-3 tags):
     - Legal advocacy
     - Accountability tracking
     - Wellness & self-care
     - Community & mentorship
     - Resources & tools
8. Tap "Complete Profile" and you're in!

**Email Verification Troubleshooting:**
- Didn't receive email?
  - Check spam/junk folder
  - Wait 5 minutes, check again
  - Tap "Resend Verification" to get another link
  - Try a different email address if persistent issues
- Email is wrong?
  - Tap "Change Email" 
  - Enter correct email
  - Verify new email
  - Old email becomes inactive

**Option B: Guest Mode (Explore First)**
1. On login screen, tap "Continue as Guest"
2. Explore all features freely
3. Your progress is saved temporarily
4. ⚠️ **Important**: Guest data is deleted after 30 days of inactivity
5. **To keep your progress**: Create account anytime (Settings → Upgrade Account)
6. Your guest data transfers to your new account

**When to use Guest Mode:**
- Testing before committing
- Quick access without signup
- Sharing device with others

**Option C: Google Sign-In (Fastest)**
1. Tap "Sign in with Google"
2. Select your Google account from the list
3. Tap "Continue"
4. Grant permissions (we only access email and profile picture):
   - ✅ We can see: Email, name, profile picture
   - ❌ We cannot see: Password, calendar, files, other data
5. Done! Your profile is auto-created
6. Complete optional profile info (bio, interests, photo)

**Option D: Apple Sign-In (iOS Only)**
1. Tap "Sign in with Apple"
2. Use Face ID or passcode to confirm
3. Choose whether to share your email:
   - **Show Email**: We see your real email
   - **Hide Email**: We get a private Apple email address
4. Grant permissions
5. Your account is created

**Which option is most secure?**
- **Email** (most control, easy to reset)
- **Google/Apple** (faster, but tied to those accounts)
- Pick whichever you're most comfortable with!

**Changing Your Mind?**
- Created email account, want Google?
  - Not directly. Create new Google account to start fresh
  - But you can link them later in Settings → Linked Accounts
- Created account with wrong email?
  - Settings → Account → Change Email Address

## Finding Your Peer Mentor

**What is a Peer Mentor?**
Someone who's been through similar challenges and trained to support you. Not a therapist—a trusted guide.

### Step 1: Request a Mentor

1. **Tap Community tab** (👥)
2. **Tap "Find a Mentor"** or **Go to Home → Tap "Get Support"**
3. **Answer Quick Questions:**
   - What area do you need support? (legal, emotional, practical)
   - Any language preference?
   - Your timezone
4. **Tap "Find Me a Mentor"**

### Step 2: Meet Your Mentor

1. **You'll receive a notification** when matched
2. **Open the mentor profile** to see:
   - Their background and experience
   - Languages they speak
   - Availability
   - User reviews
3. **Tap "Start Chat"** to introduce yourself
4. **Schedule your first session** (usually within 48 hours)

### Step 3: Your First Mentoring Session

**What to Expect:**
- 30-60 minute conversation
- Mentor will ask about your situation
- You'll discuss goals and next steps
- Mentoring is confidential and free

**What Happens Next:**
- Ongoing check-in messages
- Scheduled regular sessions (weekly, bi-weekly, etc.)
- Progress tracking on shared goals
- Access to resources and tools

**Ending Mentorship:**
- If the match isn't working, you can request a new mentor
- No penalty—we want you to feel supported
- Mentors train to handle "not a good fit" feedback

## Troubleshooting

### "I Can't Log In"

**Forgotten Password:**
1. On login screen, tap "Forgot Password?"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email inbox (and spam/junk folder) for password reset link
4. Click the link in the email
5. Create a new password:
   - Must be different from old password
   - 8+ characters, mix of letters/numbers/symbols
   - Confirm by typing twice
6. Close the browser/tab
7. Go back to app and log in with your new password
8. You're in!

**Reset Link Expired?**
- Links expire after 24 hours for security
- Tap "Forgot Password?" again to get a new link

**Email Not Verified:**
1. Check your email inbox and spam/junk folder for "Verify your email" message
2. If not found, go back to app and tap "Resend Verification"
3. A new verification link will be emailed
4. Click the link
5. Should say "Email verified!" and return to app automatically
6. If you get an error, contact support@empowrapp.org

**"Invalid Email or Password" Error:**
- Email or password is wrong
- **Forgot email?** Account created with different email than you remember
- **Forgot password?** Use "Forgot Password?" link
- **Tried Google Sign-In before?** You might have a Google account, not email
- **Case-sensitive:** Password IS case-sensitive (P ≠ p)

**"Too Many Login Attempts" Error:**
- You've tried 5+ times incorrectly
- Wait 15 minutes before trying again
- Use "Forgot Password?" to reset
- Or try a different sign-in method (Google, Apple)

**Still Can't Log In?**
- Clear browser cache (if on web):
  - Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+Delete (or Cmd+Shift+Delete on Mac)
  - Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+Delete
  - Safari: Menu → Develop → Empty Web Storage
- Uninstall and reinstall app (if on mobile)
- Contact support: support@empowrapp.org (include email, device, error message)

**Account Locked for Security?**
- Tried wrong password 10+ times
- Account temporarily locked for protection
- Check email for security alert
- Click link in email to verify it's you
- Choose: Unlock account or reset password
- Usually resolves within 1 hour

### "My Data Isn't Saving"

**Quick Diagnosis:**
1. **Check internet connection:**
   - WiFi: Look for WiFi icon (⚙️ shape) at top of screen
   - Cellular: Look for signal bars (📶) at top of screen
   - If no signal: Move closer to WiFi router or go outside for cellular
   
2. **Check app permissions:**
   - Device Settings → Apps → Empowr → Permissions
   - Ensure "Storage" is allowed (so app can save data)
   - Ensure "Internet/Data" is not restricted

3. **Wait for sync:**
   - After making changes, app needs 10-30 seconds to sync
   - Look for spinning icon (⏳) at top = syncing in progress
   - Don't close app during sync!

4. **Manually sync:**
   - Pull down on screen (swipe from top)
   - Wait for "Syncing..." then "Updated" message
   - Data should now be backed up

5. **Restart app:**
   - Close app completely (swipe up on iOS, back button on Android)
   - Wait 5 seconds
   - Reopen app
   - Check if data is there

6. **Restart device:**
   - Turn off phone/tablet
   - Wait 10 seconds
   - Turn back on
   - Open Empowr
   - Try saving again

**Data is Saved Two Ways:**
- **Local storage** on your device (instant save)
- **Cloud backup** on Empowr servers (when online)
- Even if cloud sync fails, your data is locally safe

**Data Lost After App Closed?**
- This shouldn't happen, but it means local save failed
- Check device storage (may be full)
- Try the sync steps above
- Contact support with details

**Syncing Really Slowly?**
- Internet might be slow
- App has lots of data to sync
- Be patient, it will complete
- Don't force-close the app
- Check sync progress: pull down to refresh

### "The App is Slow or Crashing"

**Performance Checklist:**

1. **Free up device storage:**
   - iPhone: Settings → General → iPhone Storage (see what's using space)
   - Android: Settings → Storage (delete old downloads, unused apps)
   - Target: At least 500 MB free space
   - How to free space: Delete unused apps, old photos, videos, cached files

2. **Close other apps:**
   - Close apps running in background
   - On iPhone: Swipe up from bottom
   - On Android: Tap recent apps button
   - Swipe or drag apps away to close
   - This frees up RAM for Empowr

3. **Clear app cache (NOT data):**
   - **iPhone:**
     - Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Empowr
     - Tap "Offload App" (keeps data, clears cache)
     - Wait 5 seconds
     - Tap to reinstall app
   - **Android:**
     - Settings → Apps → Empowr → Storage → Clear Cache
     - ⚠️ Don't tap "Clear Data" (that deletes everything)
   - **Web:** Settings → History → Clear browsing data (cache only)

4. **Update the app:**
   - **iPhone:** App Store → Search "Empowr" → Tap "Update" if available
   - **Android:** Play Store → Search "Empowr" → Tap "Update" if available
   - Close app while updating
   - Reopen after update completes

5. **Restart your device:**
   - Turn off phone/tablet completely
   - Wait 10 seconds (important!)
   - Turn back on
   - Open Empowr normally

6. **Check for iOS/Android updates:**
   - **iPhone:** Settings → General → Software Update
   - **Android:** Settings → System → System Update
   - Install if available (requires WiFi, charger)
   - Restart after update

7. **Reinstall the app (last resort):**
   - Uninstall Empowr (press and hold app → Remove App → Delete App)
   - Restart device
   - Reinstall from App Store/Play Store
   - Your data syncs back when you log in

**Still Crashing?**
- Email: support@empowrapp.org
- Include:
  - Device type (iPhone 12, Samsung Galaxy S21, etc.)
  - OS version (Settings → About Phone/iPad → Version)
  - What were you doing when it crashed?
  - How many times has it crashed?
  - Screenshot of any error messages

**App Crashes Immediately Upon Opening?**
- Don't reinstall yet
- Try these first:
  1. Force close app (Settings → Apps → Empowr → Force Close)
  2. Wait 30 seconds
  3. Reopen
  4. If still crashes, try clearing cache (see #3 above)
  5. If still crashes, then reinstall

### "Accessibility Features Aren't Working"

**Check In-App Settings:**
1. Tap Settings (⚙️)
2. Tap "Accessibility"
3. Verify each feature is enabled:
   - ✅ Text Size slider turned on? 
   - ✅ High Contrast toggle is ON?
   - ✅ Screen Reader enabled?
   - ✅ Dyslexia-Friendly Font selected?
   - ✅ Reduce Motion enabled?

**Check Device Settings:**
- Some features need device-level permission
- **For Screen Reader:**
  - iOS: Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver → toggle ON
  - Android: Settings → Accessibility → TalkBack → toggle ON
- **For Text Size:**
  - iOS: Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size (drag slider)
  - Android: Settings → Accessibility → Text and Display → Font Size
- **For High Contrast:**
  - iOS: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Increase Contrast → toggle ON
  - Android: Settings → Accessibility → Display → High Contrast Text → toggle ON

**After Enabling in Device Settings:**
1. Return to Empowr
2. Features should now work
3. They may need app restart (close and reopen)

**Web Accessibility:**
- Text size: Browser settings (Ctrl + and Ctrl -) or browser zoom
- High contrast: Use browser's reader mode or extensions
- Screen reader: Built-in (NVDA on Windows, VoiceOver on Mac)

**Specific Features Not Working?**
- **Font looks too small despite text size setting:**
  - Adjust device-level text size as well
  - Both settings work together
- **Screen reader not reading:**
  - Make sure it's turned on in device settings first
  - Some app elements may have labels that need updating
  - Report to: accessibility@empowrapp.org
- **High contrast making things hard to read:**
  - Try "On" vs. "Maximum" contrast
  - Some people prefer Maximum, others On
  - Experiment to find what works

**Still Having Issues?**
- Email: accessibility@empowrapp.org
- Include:
  - Which accessibility feature isn't working?
  - Your device type and OS version
  - What do you expect to happen?
  - What actually happens?
  - Screenshot if possible

## 🎯 Advanced Scenarios & Workflows

### Scenario: You Just Got Diagnosed or Received Bad News

**In Empowr:**

1. **Start with "Bad Day Mode":**
   - Tap SOS button in app
   - Select "Bad Day Mode"
   - App simplifies to essentials
   - See resources and support options

2. **Connect with Peer Mentor (Quick):**
   - Go to Community tab
   - Tap "Find Mentor"
   - Select "Urgent Support"
   - Mentor will be matched quickly (within hours)

3. **Document What Happened:**
   - When you're ready (today, or tomorrow)
   - Create new case
   - Don't worry about perfect details
   - You can edit and add more later
   - Just get the basics down: date, what happened, where

4. **Ask for Help:**
   - In your case, use "Ask Coach" feature
   - Tell the AI coach what you need
   - Get suggestions without judgment
   - No response time pressure

5. **Reach Out:**
   - Tell a friend or family
   - Share your case with them (optional)
   - Ask them to help document details
   - Often helps to talk it out

**Examples of What to Document:**
- "Heard diagnosis of [condition] on [date]"
- "Doctor said I need [treatment/medication]"
- "I'm scared about [specific fear]"
- "Don't know where to start"

---

### Scenario: You Have a Complaint but Don't Know What Kind of Case It Is

**Finding Your Case Type:**

1. **Start Creating a Case:**
   - Go to Legal Action Hub
   - Tap "New Case"
   - Don't worry about picking the perfect category

2. **Describe What Happened (In Your Own Words):**
   - "My boss told me I can't take medical leave even though I'm sick"
   - "The landlord won't fix the broken heater in winter"
   - "I was fired the day after telling HR about discrimination"
   - Just write naturally, don't overthink it

3. **Use Coach to Find Case Type:**
   - Tap "Ask Coach" in the case
   - Tell coach: "What kind of case is this?"
   - Coach will analyze your description
   - Coach will suggest case type (Employment, Housing, Discrimination, etc.)
   - You can accept or edit

4. **Or Browse Examples:**
   - Go to Resources tab
   - Look at "Case Examples"
   - See descriptions of each case type
   - Click on the one that matches yours
   - Create case using that template

**Case Type Guide:**
- **Employment/Wages**: Boss issues, pay problems, hours, working conditions, wrongful termination
- **Housing**: Landlord problems, repairs, evictions, discrimination
- **Healthcare**: Medical errors, insurance denial, access to treatment
- **Discrimination**: Based on race, gender, disability, age, religion, sexuality, national origin
- **Harassment**: Sexual harassment, bullying, targeted mistreatment
- **Family**: Custody, child support, visitation, abuse, restraining orders
- **Consumer**: Billing, products, services, fraud
- **Education**: School discipline, accommodations, discrimination
- **Immigration**: Visa, deportation, work authorization
- **Other**: Anything else not listed above

### Scenario: You Have Lots of Documents and Don't Know How to Organize Them

**Building Your Evidence Folder:**

1. **Create Organization System First:**
   - Open your case
   - Go to Evidence tab
   - Create folders for each document type:
     * Emails & Messages
     * Photos & Screenshots
     * Contracts & Official Docs
     * Medical Records
     * Receipts & Payments
     * Other Important Docs

2. **Upload Strategically:**
   - Start with most important (contracts, official notices)
   - Then emails and messages
   - Then photos and screenshots
   - Then everything else
   - Don't upload all 500 documents at once

3. **Label Clearly:**
   - Each document needs a clear label
   - Example: "Email from Boss - Jan 15, 2024 - Harassment"
   - Not: "IMG_2024" or "Document 1"
   - Future you will thank present you

4. **Add Context Notes:**
   - For complex documents, add notes
   - Why this document matters
   - What it proves or supports
   - "This email proves boss knew about the injury"

5. **Digital vs. Physical:**
   - Digital documents: Upload directly
   - Physical documents: Photograph or scan first
   - Use good lighting and clear images
   - Center the document in photo
   - No blurry photos (hard to read)

**Pro Tips for Organization:**
- ✅ Create a "Master Document Checklist" in your case notes
- ✅ Check off documents as you upload them
- ✅ Number your documents (Doc 1, Doc 2, etc.)
- ✅ Back up frequently to cloud storage
- ✅ Keep originals in safe place (don't lose them)

## 🔧 Advanced Troubleshooting

### App Opens But Frozen on Loading Screen

**Step 1: Force Quit and Restart**
- Close app completely (don't just minimize)
- Wait 10 seconds
- Open app again
- Wait up to 30 seconds for loading

**Step 2: Check Internet Connection**
- Make sure WiFi is connected OR have cellular data
- Try opening any website to test connection
- Switch between WiFi and cellular (test both)
- If WiFi is slow, try cellular instead

**Step 3: Clear App Cache**
- **iOS**: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Empowr → Offload App (wait) → Reinstall
- **Android**: Settings → Apps → Empowr → Storage → Clear Cache
- **Web**: Browser menu → Settings → Clear Browsing Data → Clear cache
- Restart app after clearing

**Step 4: Update the App**
- **iOS**: App Store → Updates → Update Empowr
- **Android**: Play Store → My apps → Update Empowr
- **Web**: Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R on PC, Cmd+Shift+R on Mac)

**Step 5: Check Server Status**
- Visit: empowrapp.org/status
- See if servers are down for maintenance
- If down: Wait 30 minutes and try again
- If not down: Continue troubleshooting

**Step 6: Restart Device**
- Shut device completely off
- Wait 30 seconds
- Turn back on
- Open app

**Step 7: Free Up Storage**
- Check device storage: Settings → Storage
- Need at least 2GB free space
- Delete old photos, videos, or apps you don't use
- Empty trash/recently deleted folder
- Restart device
- Try Empowr again

### Can't Sync Data Between Devices

**Understanding Sync:**
- When you log in on second device, your data should appear
- Works best on WiFi (faster sync)
- Can take 5-10 minutes even on WiFi
- Sometimes takes longer if lots of data

**Troubleshooting:**

1. **Check You're Logged In:**
   - Tap Profile → See your name and email
   - If not logged in: Log in with same email as device 1
   - Make sure spelling of email is exactly same

2. **Make Sure Both Devices Online:**
   - Device 1: WiFi on, open app briefly
   - Device 2: WiFi on, open app
   - Wait 10 minutes
   - Close and reopen app on device 2

3. **Force Sync:**
   - On device 2: Pull down from top of case list
   - Wait for "Synced" message
   - If doesn't sync: Check internet connection

4. **Check Sync Settings:**
   - Settings → Data & Privacy → Cloud Sync
   - Make sure toggle is ON
   - Make sure "Sync on WiFi" is ON
   - If OFF: Turn ON

5. **Wait Longer:**
   - First sync can take 30+ minutes
   - Don't force quit during sync
   - Don't turn off device
   - Let it run in background

6. **Try Manual Sync:**
   - Go to case list
   - Tap Settings → Sync Now
   - Wait for notification
   - Try again on other device

7. **If Still Not Working:**
   - Log out on device 2: Profile → Logout
   - Wait 1 minute
   - Log back in with same email
   - Wait 5 minutes
   - Check if data appears

### Cases/Documents Appear and Disappear

**This Usually Means a Sync Issue:**

1. **Wait for Full Sync:**
   - App is still downloading data
   - Can take 5-30 minutes
   - Don't close app during sync
   - Leave app open and device plugged in

2. **Check Your Filters:**

   - Tap filter icon (funnel shape)
   - Make sure you're not filtering to hide your cases
   - Check: Date range, status, case type
   - Clear all filters

3. **Check Sorting:**

   - Try sorting by "Date Created"
   - Or by "Last Updated"
   - Scroll down to find case

4. **Check You're In Right Tab:**

   - Make sure you're not in wrong section
   - Tap "All Cases" to see everything

5. **Search for Case:**
   - Use search feature (magnifying glass)
   - Search by case name or first few letters
   - Search by date (month name)
   - Search by person's name mentioned in case

6. **Try Pulling Down to Refresh:**
   - Go to case list
   - Pull down from top
   - Hold until refresh symbol appears
   - Release and wait

7. **Force Quit and Restart:**
   - Completely close the app
   - Wait 10 seconds
   - Reopen
   - Wait for app to fully load (1-2 minutes)

### App Keeps Asking Me to Verify Email

**This is a Security Feature:**

1. **First Time:**
   - Check your email (check spam folder!)
   - Click the link in the email
   - Wait 5 minutes
   - Open Empowr again
   - Should be verified now

2. **Can't Find Verification Email:**
   - Check spam/promotions folder
   - Search for "empowr" or "verify"
   - If truly not there: Ask for new email
   - Settings → Email Settings → Send New Verification

3. **Resend Verification Email:**
   - Tap "I don't see the email" (or similar)
   - Choose "Send again"
   - Should arrive in 2-5 minutes
   - Check spam folder again

4. **Wrong Email Address:**
   - If email is wrong: Settings → Account
   - Change email to correct one
   - Verify new email
   - Old email won't work anymore

5. **Verification Link Expired:**
   - If you waited more than 24 hours
   - Link might have expired
   - Request new verification email
   - Complete within 24 hours this time

6. **Keep Getting Asked to Verify:**
   - This is unusual
   - Might indicate account security issue
   - Contact support: security@empowrapp.org
   - They'll investigate
   - You might need to change password

### Accessibility Features Not Working

**Problem: Screen Reader (VoiceOver, TalkBack) Not Reading**

1. **Make Sure Screen Reader is Enabled:**
   - **iOS**: Settings → Accessibility → VoiceOver → ON
   - **Android**: Settings → Accessibility → TalkBack → ON
   - Both should show enabled

2. **Make Sure Empowr Accessibility is Enabled:**
   - Tap Settings → Accessibility → Enable Accessibility
   - Make sure toggle is ON
   - Restart app

3. **Update App and Device:**
   - App Store/Play Store → Check for updates
   - Install Empowr updates
   - Also update device OS
   - Restart device
   - Try again

4. **Specific Elements Not Being Read:**
   - Some elements might be images without labels
   - Report specific element: Tap Help → Report Accessibility Issue
   - Include: What's not being read, what device/OS
   - We'll fix it quickly

**Problem: Text Size Too Small/Large**

1. **Device Level:**
   - **iOS**: Settings → Display & Brightness → Text Size
   - **Android**: Settings → Display → Font Size
   - Adjust slider and tap Empowr to test

2. **App Level:**
   - Settings → Accessibility → Text Size
   - Choose: Small, Normal, Large, Extra Large
   - Restart app to apply

3. **Still Not Big Enough:**
   - Use device zoom instead:
   - Pinch zoom in any area
   - App should scale smoothly
   - (If not: Report bug)

**Problem: High Contrast Mode Not Working**

1. **Enable High Contrast:**
   - Settings → Accessibility → High Contrast
   - Turn toggle ON
   - Restart app

2. **Also Enable Device Level:**
   - **iOS**: Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Increase Contrast
   - **Android**: Settings → Accessibility → Display → High Contrast Text
   - Enable both app and device level

3. **Problem Text Still Hard to Read:**
   - We might have missed some text
   - Report: Tap Help → Report Bug
   - Include screenshot of hard-to-read text
   - We'll fix it immediately

**Problem: Dyslexia-Friendly Font Not Working**

1. **Enable Dyslexia Font:**
   - Settings → Accessibility → Font Style → Dyslexia-Friendly
   - App should change to OpenDyslexic font
   - Restart app

2. **Font Didn't Change:**
   - Make sure you tapped "Dyslexia-Friendly" option
   - Close app completely (not just minimize)
   - Reopen
   - Check if font changed

3. **Still Hard to Read:**
   - Combine with larger text size
   - Settings → Accessibility → Text Size → Large or Extra Large
   - Combine with high contrast
   - Should be much easier now

### Different Issues on Different Devices

**Problem: Works on Phone, Not on Tablet**

1. **Same Email, Same Password:**
   - Make sure you're using exact same login
   - Email spelling must be identical
   - Password must be exact (case-sensitive)

2. **Device Storage:**
   - Tablet might not have enough space
   - Check tablet storage: Settings → Storage
   - Delete apps/files to free up space
   - Need at least 2GB free

3. **Different OS Versions:**
   - iPhone (iOS) and iPad (iOS) both work
   - Older devices might not support new app version
   - Check app requirements: App Store page
   - If incompatible: Update device OS

4. **Network Issues:**
   - Works on phone's cellular, not tablet's WiFi?
   - WiFi might be slow
   - Try toggling WiFi off/on
   - Try moving closer to router
   - Try cellular hotspot from phone

5. **Sync Between Devices:**
   - Second device needs WiFi to sync
   - First sync takes 10-30 minutes
   - Tap pull-down to force sync
   - Wait patiently (don't keep closing/opening)
