3mpwr App — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2025

This policy describes how 3mpwr App ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information in the 3mpwr App mobile application and web experience (if enabled). By using the app, you agree to these practices.

Data Ownership Statement

Your data belongs 100% to you. 3mpwr App is built on the fundamental principle of complete user data sovereignty:

  • 100% User Ownership: All data created in 3mpwr App belongs entirely to you. We never own, claim, or retain your personal information.
  • Local-First & Air-Gapped: All processing happens on your device. Data stays in your possession unless you choose to sync to your own cloud.
  • Your Cloud, Your Control: Optional sync connects only to your chosen services (Google Drive, iCloud, WebDAV). No data passes through our servers.
  • No Tracking: Zero embedded analytics, trackers, or third-party data collection. No hidden network calls.
  • Encryption & Privacy: Local data encrypted using your device security. Cloud encryption handled by your chosen provider.
  • Open & Transparent: Our codebase contains no data-logging functions. You can inspect and verify our privacy claims.

For our complete Data Ownership and Security Statement, see DATA_OWNERSHIP_STATEMENT.md.

Summary

  • Local-first architecture: All data stored on your device by default with optional user-controlled sync
  • BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud) support: Connect your own storage for 100% user-owned data persistence
  • We practice data minimization and store most preferences locally on your device by default
  • We do not sell personal data
  • You can use Guest Mode without creating an account (limited features)
  • You control notifications and certain analytics in Settings.
  • You can request access, correction, or deletion of your data.

Information We Collect

1) Information you provide

  • Account information (if you create one): name, email, and similar identifiers. Guest Mode collects no account identifiers.
  • Preferences and settings (e.g., accessibility options, quiet hours, language).
  • Community contributions (if enabled): messages, posts, uploaded media, reactions, and profile details.
  • Contact messages (e.g., About/Contact form or email requests).
  • Evidence Locker notes and attachments: by design these remain on your device unless you explicitly export or share them. Avoid storing personal identifiers in these notes where possible.

2) Information collected automatically

  • App diagnostics and performance telemetry (e.g., app version, device OS version, basic device capabilities, crash/error logs where enabled).
  • App interactions (analytics events) using pseudonymous identifiers to help improve features and reliability.
  • Push notification token (if you enable notifications) so that we can deliver messages to your device.
  • Network/presence status (for community features) and basic counts of viewed items.

3) Information from third parties

  • Content metadata from integrated services (e.g., YouTube API for videos; Advocate Directory API for listings). We request public metadata only and do not receive your account data from these providers.

We do not intentionally collect sensitive categories of data unless you directly provide them (e.g., in notes or community posts). Do not include sensitive personal identifiers in free-text fields where not required.

How We Use Information

  • Provide core functionality (navigation, saved items, localization, accessibility options).
  • Deliver optional features (voice navigation, quiet hours, micro-coach overlays) and improve usability.
  • Send notifications you opt into (e.g., reminders, updates). You can change your choices in Settings.
  • Maintain safety, integrity, and security of the app and services.
  • Measure and improve performance and reliability (aggregated analytics and diagnostics).
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce terms.

Legal Bases (EEA/UK where applicable)

  • Contract: to provide services you request.
  • Legitimate interests: to improve and secure the app in a way that does not override your rights.
  • Consent: where required for optional features such as push notifications or analytics; you may withdraw consent in Settings.

Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data. We share limited information with service providers as necessary to operate the app:

  • Push Notifications: Expo Notifications or equivalent provider to deliver notifications to your device (stores device push token).
  • Cloud Services (if enabled): Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore/Storage) for account, community, and sync features.
  • Error Monitoring (optional): Sentry or similar to capture crash logs and errors.
  • Content Integrations: YouTube API (video metadata), Advocate Directory API (professional listings). We receive public content metadata only.
  • Hosting/Delivery (web): Hosting/CDN provider for the web app and static assets.

Service providers process data on our behalf under contracts that require confidentiality and appropriate security. We may disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a merger, acquisition, or similar corporate transaction.

International Transfers

If we transfer your information internationally, we rely on approved safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) where required by law.

Data Retention

  • Local device data (preferences, saved items, notes) persists until you delete the app or clear app data.
  • Accounts and community content (if enabled) are retained while your account is active; you may delete posts or request account deletion.
  • Analytics and diagnostic logs are retained for a limited period (e.g., 13 months for analytics; 30–90 days for logs).
  • Backups (if any) retain content per operational needs and then are deleted securely.

Your Choices and Rights

  • Notifications: Enable/disable push and choose categories in Settings. Quiet Hours can silence notifications during certain times.
  • Accessibility & Language: Adjust text size, contrast, and language in Settings.
  • Analytics: Where available, you can opt out of analytics in Settings.
  • Access/Deletion/Correction: Contact us at empowrapp08162025@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data. We may need to verify your identity to process requests.

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights (e.g., data portability, restriction/objection to processing). We will honor rights requests as required by applicable law (e.g., GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, PIPEDA).

Children’s Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the age required by your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us to request deletion.

Security

We use administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information, including encryption in transit and restricted access. No system is 100% secure; you should also protect your device with a passcode and avoid storing unnecessary identifiers in free-text notes.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes to our practices or legal requirements. The updated policy will be posted with a new “Last updated” date. Material changes will be communicated within the app or via other appropriate channels.
