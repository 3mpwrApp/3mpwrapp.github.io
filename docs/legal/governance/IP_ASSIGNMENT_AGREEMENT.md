**4.2 Additional Consideration**

(b) **Perpetual Free Access:** The Assignor (and all users) shall have free access to the 3mpwr App in perpetuity;

    - The mission cannot be changed without supermajority approval

## 5. FOUNDATION'S COMMITMENTS

**5.1 Mission Commitment**

The Foundation covenants and agrees to:

(a) Operate the 3mpwr App as a **free platform** for end users;
(b) Never sell, share, or monetize user data without explicit consent;
(c) Maintain **WCAG AAA accessibility standards** at all times;
(d) Ensure **51% of Board and staff** have lived disability experience;
(e) Publish **annual audited financial reports** publicly;
(f) Operate under **open-source principles** (AGPL-3.0 license for non-commercial use).

**5.2 Dissolution Provision**

The Foundation agrees that upon dissolution or loss of charitable status, all Intellectual Property shall be transferred to another Canadian registered charity serving the disability community, subject to the same mission commitments outlined in Section 5.1.

Under no circumstances shall the Intellectual Property be sold to a for-profit entity or used in a manner inconsistent with the Foundation's charitable purposes.

**5.3 Licensing to 3mpwr Technologies Inc.**

The Foundation may license the Intellectual Property to **3mpwr Technologies Inc.** (a for-profit subsidiary) for the purpose of generating revenue to fund the Foundation's charitable activities, provided that:

(a) The Foundation retains majority ownership (51%+) of Technologies Inc.;
(b) All licensing agreements preserve the free-to-user commitment;
(c) 100% of net profits from Technologies Inc. are donated to the Foundation;
(d) The license is terminable if Technologies Inc. violates the mission.

## 9. NON-COMPETE AND NON-SOLICITATION

**9.1 Non-Compete**

For a period of **two (2) years** from the Effective Date, the Assignor agrees not to:

(a) Create, develop, or contribute to a competing disability support app or platform;
(b) Commercialize any derivative works based on the 3mpwr App;
(c) Use the Intellectual Property for personal profit or competitive purposes.

**Exceptions:**
- Contributing to open-source accessibility projects not directly competitive;
- Participating in disability advocacy or policy work;
- Employment with a company that has non-competing disability products.

**9.2 Non-Solicitation**

For a period of **one (1) year** from the Effective Date, the Assignor agrees not to:

(a) Solicit 3mpwr App users to migrate to a competing platform;
(b) Recruit Foundation staff, contractors, or volunteers for competing ventures;
(c) Interfere with Foundation's relationships with funders, partners, or sponsors.

**9.3 Reasonableness**

The Assignor acknowledges that these restrictions are reasonable in scope, duration, and geography given the charitable nature of the Foundation's mission and the valuable consideration provided.
