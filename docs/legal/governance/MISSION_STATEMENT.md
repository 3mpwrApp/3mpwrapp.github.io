## Mission

**To empower people with disabilities through accessible technology, mutual aid, and advocacy tools that preserve dignity, autonomy, and community connection.**

---

## Vision

**A world where disability is not a barrier to accessing support, resources, or justice—where technology serves people, not profits.**

---

## Core Values

### 1. Accessibility First
We design technology **with and for** disabled people, not as an afterthought. Every feature must meet WCAG AAA standards. Every decision considers cognitive load, energy management, and diverse disability experiences.

**This means:**
- Screen reader testing before any release
- Cognitive accessibility reviews by neurodivergent users
- Energy-aware UI for chronic illness and fatigue
- No "one-size-fits-all" solutions

### 2. Privacy as a Right
User data belongs to users, period. We will never sell, share, or monetize personal information. Users control their data and can export or delete it anytime.

**This means:**
- No data selling, ever
- No advertising or tracking
- No AI training on user content without consent
- Local-first design where possible
- Full transparency on what data we collect and why

### 3. Mutual Aid Over Extraction
We believe in community support, not corporate extraction. Technology should redistribute resources to those who need them, not concentrate wealth at the top.

**This means:**
- App is **free forever** to users
- Mutual Aid Fund redistributes donations directly to users
- Revenue comes from those who can afford it (B2B, grants)
- No paywalls for essential features
- No "growth at all costs" mentality

### 4. Radical Transparency
We operate in the open. Financials, roadmap, governance—all public. We trust our community and they can hold us accountable.

**This means:**
- Annual audited financials published online
- Real-time donation tracking
- Open-source codebase (AGPL-3.0)
- Public Board meetings (livestreamed + recorded)
- Quarterly community town halls

### 5. Nothing About Us Without Us
Disabled people lead every aspect of our work. Minimum 51% of Board and staff must have lived disability experience. User Advisory Council has veto power on features.

**This means:**
- Majority-disabled governance at all levels
- Paid positions prioritize disabled candidates
- User Advisory Council (elected by lottery, not appointed)
- Accessibility audits by disabled users, not just automated tools
- Community decides priorities, not founders or funders

**Tactics:**

## Theory of Change

### The Problem
People with disabilities face systemic barriers to:
- **Economic security** (poverty rate 2x non-disabled population)
- **Healthcare access** (long waits, gatekeeping, ableist providers)
- **Legal justice** (can't afford lawyers, evidence collection burden)
- **Community connection** (isolation, inaccessible spaces)
- **Technology access** (expensive, not designed for diverse needs)

### Root Causes
1. **Ableism:** Society designed by/for non-disabled people
2. **Poverty:** Disability = reduced income + increased expenses
3. **Extraction:** Tech companies exploit users for profit
4. **Siloed Services:** Fragmented support (health, legal, social)
5. **Lack of Power:** Disabled people excluded from decision-making

### Our Solution
**3mpwr App** provides a free, accessible platform that:
- **Centralizes resources** (one app, not 20 websites)
- **Redistributes wealth** (Mutual Aid Fund from rich to poor)
- **Builds community** (peer support, not just top-down services)
- **Empowers advocacy** (tools to fight systems, not just cope)
- **Centers disabled voices** (user governance, not corporate control)

### Intended Outcomes (3-Year)
| Outcome | Indicator | Target |
|---------|-----------|--------|
| Improved access to resources | Users find 3+ new supports via app | 80% |
| Financial relief | Mutual Aid grants received | 200+ users |
| Reduced isolation | Weekly community engagement | 60% |
| Successful advocacy | Legal appeals won with app tools | 25+ cases |
| Disability employment | Paid roles for disabled people | 5 FTE |

### Long-Term Impact (10-Year)
- **10,000+ users** across Canada supported monthly
- **$1M+ redistributed** through Mutual Aid Fund
- **National model** for disability-led tech cooperatives
- **Policy change** on disability data rights and accessibility standards
- **Cultural shift** toward mutual aid over charity

## Commitment to Equity

### We Prioritize:
1. **Multiply marginalized disabled people:**
   - BIPOC, Indigenous, 2SLGBTQ+, low-income, rural, immigrants
   - Intersectional approach to accessibility
   
2. **High-support needs:**
   - People with intellectual disabilities
   - Non-speaking communicators
   - Psych disability/mad-identified
   
3. **Criminalized communities:**
   - Disabled people in prisons
   - Homeless disabled people
   - Drug users with disabilities

### We Do Not:
- ❌ Use inspiration porn or "overcoming" narratives
- ❌ Prioritize corporate partnerships over user needs
- ❌ Accept funding that compromises mission
- ❌ Speak for disabled people (we amplify their voices)
- ❌ Tolerate ableism, racism, queerphobia in our spaces

## Accountability Mechanisms

### To Users:
- **User Advisory Council** elected by lottery, not appointed
- **Quarterly town halls** with open Q&A
- **Feature request voting** (top 3 prioritized each quarter)
- **Transparent roadmap** (public Trello/GitHub board)

### To Funders:
- **Annual impact reports** with measurable outcomes

- **Grant reporting** within 30 days of milestones

### To Community:
