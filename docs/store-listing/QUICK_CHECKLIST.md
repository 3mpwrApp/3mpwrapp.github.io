## 📱 How to Capture on Android

**Physical Device:**
- Press **Volume Down + Power** simultaneously
- Screenshots saved to gallery automatically

**Emulator:**
- Click **camera icon** in toolbar
- Or use **Ctrl + S** (Windows) / **Cmd + S** (Mac)

## 🎬 Screen Recording (Optional Promo Video)

**Android Built-in:**
1. Swipe down from top
2. Tap "Screen record" tile
3. Record 30-60 seconds
4. Stop recording
