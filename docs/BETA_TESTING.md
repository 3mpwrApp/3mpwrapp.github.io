# Closed Beta Testing Guide

Thanks for testing the 3mpwr app. Here’s how to give high‑impact feedback quickly.

What to test
- Navigation & tabs: no dead links, no warnings, back behavior intuitive.
- Lists: loading state appears; empty and error states are clear; counts announced with screen readers.
- Resources/Wellness tools labeled “(Beta)” behave reasonably.
- Offline: toggle airplane mode; app shows banner and cached content.
- Notifications: local reminders fire; push on Android; iOS push pending APNs.

How to send feedback
- Settings → About & Contact → “Send email” (adds device/build info automatically).
- Or email with subject: “Beta feedback: <screen/feature>” and include steps/screenshots.

Known limits
- iOS remote push disabled until APNs is configured.
- Some features labeled Coming Soon.

Tips
- Keep the app open for 1–2 minutes after first launch to warm caches.
- If something feels slow, note the screen and what you tapped.

Thank you — your feedback directly shapes the launch!
