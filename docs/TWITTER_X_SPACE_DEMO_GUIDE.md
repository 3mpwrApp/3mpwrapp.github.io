### Feature Demo #1: Legal Tools (10 min)
**Time: 10:00-20:00**

**Screen Share: Advocacy Tab**

> "Let's start with the legal side - because fighting for benefits is exhausting and 3mpwr makes it simpler.

> **The Letter Wizard:** You can generate a medical accommodation letter in literally 5 minutes. You answer a few questions about your needs, and the app creates a professional letter. No writing from scratch. No wondering if you said the right thing.

> Demo the flow:
> 1. Tap 'Letter Wizard'
> 2. Select letter type (accommodation request, medical documentation, appeal letter)
> 3. Answer questions
> 4. Review and customize
> 5. Download as PDF or email directly

> **Evidence Manager:** This is where you store your medical proof. Medical records, test results, letters from doctors - all encrypted on YOUR device. No company can see it. When you need to submit evidence, it's all organized.

> **Deadline Calculator:** 14 Canadian jurisdictions tracked. Different provinces have different appeal timelines. The app reminds you what's due and when. You'll never miss a deadline again.

> **Appeal Coach:** Multi-step process for appealing a benefits decision. Walk through what evidence helps, how to frame your case, what agencies want to see."

### Feature Demo #2: Wellness Tools (10 min)
**Time: 20:00-30:00**

**Screen Share: Wellness Tab**

> "Now the wellness side - 41 tools organized into 6 categories. These are designed WITH people who have disabilities, not FOR them.

> **AI Mood Tracker:** You log your mood every day (takes 30 seconds). After 7 days, the AI starts seeing patterns. After 30 days, you get insights like:
> - 'Your mood jumps +1.2 when you sleep 7+ hours'
> - 'Social activity gives +0.8 mood boost'
> - 'Rainy weather correlates with -0.6 mood dip'

> You're not guessing anymore - you have data about YOUR body.

> **Pacing Partner:** For chronic fatigue and ME/CFS especially. You log activities and their impact. The AI learns your energy patterns and PREDICTS your energy 24 hours ahead. 'You peaked at 10am-2pm. You'll crash at 4pm if you do more than 1 hour activity.'

> Game-changer for planning your days without crashes.

> **Crisis Tools:** Grounding techniques, emotional first aid, 988 lifeline integration. When you're in crisis, one tap gives you immediate support.

> **Daily Living:** Routines, nutrition, appointments, even housekeeping adapted for chronic illness. No judgment. Some days you can do nothing - the app knows.

> All 41 are organized by:
> - Mental health (anxiety, mood, DBT, grounding, journaling)
> - Physical health (pain, sleep, symptoms, medication)
> - Daily living (routines, nutrition, self-care)
> - Movement (adaptive exercise, breathing, stretching)
> - Creative (art, music, writing therapy)
> - Crisis (emergency resources, planning)"

**Key Point:**
> "Every tool assumes you have a bad day. No judgment. No guilt. Just support."

**Anticipated Questions & Answers:**

**Q: Is this available outside Canada?**
A: Currently optimized for Canada (14 jurisdictions), but the core features work worldwide. We're planning US expansion in 2026.

**Q: How is this free if there are no ads?**
A: We're backed by grant funding and disability advocacy organizations who believe in this mission. We're not dependent on advertising or selling data.

**Q: What if I'm on Android/iOS version X?**
A: We support iOS 14+ and Android 10+. Both regularly updated. If you have device compatibility questions, email us.

**Q: Can this help me win my appeal?**
A: We can't guarantee wins (that depends on your case), but we dramatically improve your odds by organizing evidence and helping you present your strongest case.

**Q: Is there a web version?**
A: Currently mobile-first app. Web version planned for 2026 for accessibility and desktop users.

**Q: How do you handle crisis situations?**
A: The app provides crisis tools and resources (988 Lifeline, crisis text line), but it's a support tool, not emergency services. If you're in immediate danger, call 911.

**Q: Can I use this with my partner/caregiver?**
A: Your data is private, but you can share specific documents or insights with people you trust. Caregiver mode coming in 2026.

## 🎤 HOST TALKING TIPS

### Tone & Delivery
- **Authentic:** Share personal experiences with disability
- **Conversational:** Like talking to a friend, not a presentation
- **Paced:** Give people time to absorb information
- **Interactive:** Ask rhetorical questions, pause for responses
- **Inclusive:** Use plain language, avoid jargon
- **Hopeful but realistic:** This app helps, but isn't a cure-all

### Strong Opening
> "Thanks for being here. Before we dive in, I want to be real with you: Building this took a year because we insisted on doing it right. Right means:
> - Built by people with disabilities, not able-bodied people guessing
> - Accessible from day one (not an afterthought)
> - Privacy-first (your data is yours)
> - Free forever (no paywall)
> 
> Not because we're charity. Because disability advocacy shouldn't be gatekept by price."

### Strong Closing
> "Here's what I hope you take away: You deserve tools built with you in mind. You deserve privacy. You deserve community. You deserve to fight for what's yours without exhaustion killing you first.
> 
> 3mpwr tries to be that tool. It won't be perfect - we'll improve based on YOUR feedback. But it's built on respect for you.
> 
> Thank you for being here. Download, try it, tell us what you think. And if you have friends who need this - they're waiting."
