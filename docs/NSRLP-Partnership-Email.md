## About 3mpwr

We've built a comprehensive research hub analyzing workers' compensation tribunal decisions across Canada:

- **11,430 WSIAT (Ontario) decisions** analyzed (2020-2026) with statistical rigor
- **34,000+ words** of plain-language guides, appeal templates, and investigative blog posts
- **Recent focus:** Employer claim suppression tactics, connecting BC WorkSafeBC investigations to Ontario patterns
- **Data-driven:** 95% confidence intervals, chi-square tests, cross-jurisdiction legal analysis

Our mission: Make complex legal research accessible to injured workers navigating tribunals without legal representation.

## Why This Collaboration Makes Sense

**Your reach:** 354+ SRLs served (2023-2025, 28% growth)  
**Our gap:** Limited direct SRL outreach  
**Shared goal:** Empower self-represented litigants with evidence-based resources

Many workers' compensation claimants are effectively SRLs at tribunals. They face:
- Denial decisions based on incomplete medical information
- "Decision of employer" exclusions for mental stress claims
- Employer retaliation for filing claims
- Limited access to legal representation
- Tribunal procedures they must navigate alone
