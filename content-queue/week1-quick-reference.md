## 💡 Call-to-Action Variations (Copy These!)

Instead of "Check it out!" try:

**For education posts:**
- "What do you think? Does this resonate?"
- "Questions? Comments? Pushback welcome!"
- "Have you seen this pattern in your case?"

**For CanLII posts:**
- "Which cases should we analyze next?"
- "What patterns would help YOU most?"
- "Help us prioritize based on community needs"

**For feature posts:**
- "Would this help you? What else would you need?"
- "Questions about organizing evidence?"
- "What's missing? What would make this better?"

**For website promotion:**
- "Bookmark this. Share it. Use it."
- "What's missing from this resource page?"
