## 🎯 Key Advantages Over AMA

### AMAs require:
- Real-time availability (1-2 hours)
- Existing audience (people to ask questions)
- Expertise positioning (intimidating for new founders)
- Immediate responses (pressure)

### Community questions allow:
- Async responses (reply within 24 hours)
- Works with NO audience (you're asking THEM)
- Collaborative positioning (building together)
- Thoughtful responses (no rush)
