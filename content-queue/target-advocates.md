## Engagement Strategy

### Daily Engagement Routine (15 min, 2x per day)

**Morning (7:30 AM):**
1. Open Mastodon → Search latest posts from your follows
2. Read 5-10 posts from target advocates
3. Reply thoughtfully to 2-3 posts (no promotional content)
4. Boost/like posts that add value to community

**Evening (7 PM):**
1. Open Bluesky → Check notifications + timeline
2. Read 5-10 posts from target advocates
3. Reply thoughtfully to 2-3 posts
4. Repost/like valuable content

### Weekly Deep Engagement (30 min, Sundays)

**Goal:** Build deeper relationships with 2-3 advocates per week

**Process:**
1. Pick 2-3 advocates from your list
2. Read their last 10 posts (understand their current focus)
3. Reply thoughtfully to 2-3 older posts (shows you're paying attention)
4. Share 1 of their posts with your commentary
5. Optional: Send DM if appropriate (e.g., "Your post about WSIB deadlines was so helpful - would you be interested in beta testing an app built for injured workers?")

## Content Types to Engage With

### High Priority (always reply if you have value to add):
- Personal stories of system barriers (WSIB denials, ODSP delays, CPP-D appeals)
- Questions asking for resources/help
- Calls for community input
- Celebrating wins (approvals, appeals won, accommodations granted)

### Medium Priority (engage when relevant):
- Policy analysis/advocacy
- Accessibility tech discussions
- Chronic illness management strategies
- Spoon theory applications

### Low Priority (skip unless very relevant):
- General disability awareness posts (unless adding unique insight)
- Reposted news articles (unless you can add context)
- Political posts not directly related to disability justice

## Monthly Review Process

**First Sunday of each month (30 min):**

1. **Audit your list:**
   - Remove inactive accounts (no posts in 30 days)
   - Remove accounts that became promotional/spammy
   - Add 2-3 new rising voices

2. **Engagement quality check:**
   - Are you getting replies back? (If not, adjust your reply style)
   - Which advocates engage most with YOU? (prioritize them)
   - Any advocates seem annoyed by your presence? (pull back)

3. **Conversion tracking:**
   - How many advocates checked out 3mpwrApp? (traffic sources in analytics)
   - How many signed up for beta? (if trackable)
   - How many became active users? (community chat, feedback)

### ❌ Bad Reply (Promotional):
