# Influencer Outreach Templates & Tracker

**Purpose:** Build genuine relationships with micro-influencers (1K-10K followers) in disability advocacy space  
**Goal:** 5 DMs sent per month → 2 partnerships formed within 60 days  
**Current Status:** Beta testers just onboarding - position as collaborative build, not user story showcase

---

## Core Principles

✅ **DO:**
- Lead with value for THEM (not your app)
- Offer beta access as gift, not sales pitch
- Be specific about why you're reaching out to THIS person
- Keep it short (3-4 sentences max)
- No expectations/pressure

❌ **DON'T:**
- Mass DM (personalize every message)
- Lead with feature lists
- Ask for promotion upfront
- Send follow-up if no reply (respect their time)
- Contact mega-influencers (50K+ followers - they get spammed constantly)

---

## Template 1: Beta Access Offer (Early Stage - No User Stories Yet)

**When to use:** First outreach to advocates you've engaged with 3-5 times already

**Subject/First Line:** "Quick question about [specific topic from their recent post]"

**Template:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Your post about [specific issue they raised - e.g., "WSIB adjudicators dismissing disorganized evidence"] really hit home.

I'm building tools for exactly this problem (disability benefits navigation + evidence management). Beta just opened—would you want early access? No strings, just looking for honest feedback from people who've lived it.

Either way, appreciate your advocacy work. 🧡

— [Your Name]
3mpwrApp (disability rights tools, always free)
```

**Key elements:**
- Reference specific content (shows you're not mass DMing)
- Clear value prop (what problem does it solve?)
- Low pressure ("no strings")
- Acknowledge their expertise ("people who've lived it")
- Keep it under 280 characters if possible (respect their time)

## Template 2: Collaborative Build Invitation

**When to use:** Advocates who are also builders/creators (bloggers, content creators, advocates with platforms)

**Template:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Saw your [blog post/video/thread] on [topic]. Wondering if you'd want to collaborate on 3mpwrApp development?

Building disability rights tools in public (evidence lockers, benefits trackers, AI case interpreters). Looking for advocates to shape features based on lived experience.

Beta access + design input if interested. Zero pressure.

— [Your Name]
https://3mpwrapp.pages.dev/blog
```

**Key elements:**
- "Collaborate" (not "promote")
- "Shape features" (they have input, not just users)
- "Zero pressure" (respects boundaries)
- Link to build-in-public blog (transparency)

**When to use:** Advocates who've shared similar disability/benefits navigation story to yours

## Template 5: Appreciation + Soft Ask

**When to use:** Advocates whose work you genuinely admire, even if not directly related to your app

**Template:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Just wanted to say your work on [specific advocacy project] is incredible. The [specific thing they did] made such an impact.

Building disability rights tools at 3mpwrApp—if you ever want beta access for yourself or community, let me know. Always free.

Keep doing what you're doing. 💪

— [Your Name]
```

**Key elements:**
- Starts with appreciation (genuine, not manipulative)
- "For yourself or community" (removes self-promotion pressure)
- "Always free" (removes financial barrier)
- Ends with encouragement (maintain goodwill even if they don't reply)

## Who to Target (Criteria)

### ✅ Ideal Candidates:
- **Follower count:** 1,000 - 10,000 (sweet spot for authentic engagement)
- **Posting frequency:** 3-5x per week (active but not overwhelming)
- **Content quality:** Thoughtful analysis, lived experience sharing, community organizing
- **Engagement rate:** >5% (replies to comments, engages with followers)
- **Geographic focus:** Canadian advocates (bonus: Ontario/BC for provincial systems)
- **Topic alignment:** WSIB, ODSP, CPP-D, disability justice, accessibility, chronic illness, neurodivergence
- **Community trust:** Followers engage positively, minimal drama/conflict

### ❌ Avoid:
- Mega-influencers (50K+ followers - too broad, get spammed constantly)
- Brands/organizations only (looking for individual voices)
- Promotional accounts (constant product/service selling)
- Controversy-focused accounts (builds wrong association)
- Inactive accounts (<1 post/month)

## Relationship Stages

### Stage 1: Cold Outreach (Day 1)
- Send personalized DM using appropriate template
- No follow-up if no reply within 7 days

### Stage 2: Conversation Started (Day 1-7)
- Reply within 2-4 hours if they respond
- Offer beta access link immediately
- Ask what features would be most helpful for them
- NO pressure to promote

### Stage 3: Beta Access Accepted (Week 1-4)
- Send onboarding email with beta guide link
- Check in after 7 days: "How's it going? Any bugs/confusing parts?"
- Incorporate feedback into development
- Share updates: "Based on your feedback, we added [feature]"

### Stage 4: Active User (Month 2+)
- Ask permission for testimonial: "Can we feature your feedback in a Community Spotlight?"
- Invite to beta tester community (Discord/chat)
- Offer early access to new features
- Optional: Co-create content (interview, guest blog post)

### Stage 5: Partnership (Month 3+)
- They organically mention 3mpwrApp in their content
- They provide user story/testimonial
- They participate in feedback sessions
- Mutual promotion (you share their advocacy work, they share your tool)

## Red Flags (When to Stop Outreach)

❌ **Stop immediately if:**
- They explicitly say "not interested" (respect boundaries)
- They ask you to stop contacting them
- They block/mute you
- They publicly criticize your outreach as spam

❌ **Pull back if:**
- No reply after first DM (don't follow up)
- They reply but conversation feels forced/awkward
- They accept beta access but never use it (they're being polite, not interested)

✅ **Good signs to continue:**
- Quick reply (within 24 hours)
- Asks clarifying questions about features
- Shares feedback (even critical feedback is good!)
- Mentions specific use cases from their experience

## Example Successful Outreach Flow

### Day 1: Initial DM (Template 1)
> "Hey Sarah, your post about WSIB adjudicators dismissing disorganized evidence really hit home. I'm building tools for exactly this problem (disability benefits navigation + evidence management). Beta just opened—would you want early access? No strings, just looking for honest feedback from people who've lived it. — Alex"

### Day 1 (4 hours later): Reply received
> "Hey Alex! This sounds amazing. I've been looking for something like this. What features does it have?"

### Day 1 (2 hours later): Your reply
> "Main features: Evidence Locker (encrypted storage), Benefits Tracker (deadline reminders), AI Case Interpreter (plain-language summaries of legal docs). All offline-first, no login required for crisis access. Beta link: [URL] — let me know what you think!"

### Day 2: Beta access accepted
> "Just signed up! Testing the Evidence Locker now. Quick question: can I upload voice memos? Sometimes I'm too fatigued to type."

### Day 2: Incorporate feedback
> "GREAT question. Not yet, but adding to roadmap based on your feedback. Voice memos make total sense for low-energy days. Will update you when it's ready!"

### Week 2: Follow-up
> "Hey Sarah! Based on your feedback + 3 other beta testers, we're adding voice memo support in next release (2 weeks). Want to test it early?"

### Week 4: User story request
> "Sarah, would you be open to sharing your experience using 3mpwrApp in a Community Spotlight post? Totally anonymous if you prefer. Just want to show others it's actually useful!"

### Month 2: Partnership formed
> Sarah posts organically: "Just used 3mpwrApp's Evidence Locker to organize 18 months of medical records for my WSIB appeal. Game changer for brain fog days."

**Result:** 1 active beta tester → 1 testimonial → organic promotion → credibility boost

## Monthly Review Process

**First Sunday of each month (30 min):**

1. **Audit outreach effectiveness:**
   - Reply rate (# replies / # DMs sent)
   - Conversion rate (# beta users / # DMs sent)
   - Which templates worked best?
   - Which platforms had highest reply rate?

2. **Adjust strategy:**
   - If reply rate < 40%: Messages too salesy? Not personalized enough?
   - If beta acceptance rate < 20%: Value prop unclear?
   - If beta users not engaging: Onboarding too complex?

3. **Identify new targets:**
   - Add 5-10 new advocates to target list
   - Remove inactive/unresponsive contacts
