# Feature Spotlight: Deadlines & Reminders

**Category:** Resources Tools

Never miss critical legal, medical, or benefits deadlines with smart reminder system

* * *

## What Is Deadlines & Reminders?

Deadlines & Reminders is designed to never miss critical legal, medical, or benefits deadlines with smart reminder system. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada.

* * *

## Key Highlights

- **Track all important deadlines in one place**
- **Multiple reminder notifications (1 week, 3 days, 1 day)**
- **Categorize by type: legal, medical, benefits, personal**
- **Snooze and reschedule options**
- **Completed deadline history**
- **Free deadline management**

* * *

## How It Works

Here are real examples of how you can use Deadlines & Reminders:

1. Deadline: "Appeal must be filed by October 31"
2. Reminders: "October 24 (1 week), Oct 28 (3 days), Oct 30 (1 day)"
3. Category: "Legal - Benefits Appeal"
4. Link: "Connected to Evidence Locker documents"
5. History: "Filed on Oct 29 - mark complete"

* * *

## Why Deadlines & Reminders Matters

- Reduce stress about forgetting important dates
- Protect your legal rights with timely action
- Stay organized across multiple processes
- Evidence you met deadlines if questioned
