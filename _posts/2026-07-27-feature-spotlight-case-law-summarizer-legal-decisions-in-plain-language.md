# Feature Spotlight: Case Law Summarizer: Legal Decisions in Plain Language
**Category:** Legal Intelligence
Upload any legal decision and get a plain-language summary highlighting key findings, winning arguments, and relevance to your case
---
## What Is Case Law Summarizer: Legal Decisions in Plain Language?
Case Law Summarizer: Legal Decisions in Plain Language is designed to upload any legal decision and get a plain-language summary highlighting key findings, winning arguments, and relevance to your case. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada.
---
## Key Highlights
- **AI-powered summarization of complex legal decisions**
- **Extracts: key facts, legal issues, tribunal findings, outcome**
- **Highlights winning arguments and reasoning**
- **Assesses relevance to your specific situation**
- **Generates citations in proper legal format**
- **Saves summaries to your Evidence Locker**
---
## How It Works
Example scenario (illustrative only):
An injured worker is preparing an appeal while managing medical appointments and family responsibilities. They use Case Law Summarizer: Legal Decisions in Plain Language to reduce one major barrier so their limited energy can go toward decisions that affect outcomes.
Practical ways this feature can be used:
1. Upload: 40-page WSIB tribunal decision
2. Get: "Claimant won. Key finding: Employer failed to consider ergonomic accommodation"
3. Relevance: "This case is highly relevant - same diagnosis and similar workplace setup"
4. Winning argument: "Tribunal emphasized employer's duty to explore all accommodation options"
5. Citation: "Jones v. WSIB, 2024 WSIAT 456 - use this in your appeal"
