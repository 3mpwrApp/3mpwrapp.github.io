# Feature Spotlight: Document Scanner with OCR: Paper to Searchable Text
**Category:** Productivity Tools
Scan paper documents with your phone camera and convert to searchable, encrypted text - perfect for medical records and legal papers
---
## What Is Document Scanner with OCR: Paper to Searchable Text?
Document Scanner with OCR: Paper to Searchable Text is designed to scan paper documents with your phone camera and convert to searchable, encrypted text - perfect for medical records and legal papers. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada.
---
## Key Highlights
- **Camera-based scanning with automatic edge detection**
- **OCR (Optical Character Recognition) converts images to searchable text**
- **Auto-enhancement: brightness, contrast, de-skew**
- **Multi-page scanning for full documents**
- **Saves to Evidence Locker with encryption**
- **Export as PDF or text for sharing**
---
## How It Works
Example scenario (illustrative only):
An injured worker is preparing an appeal while managing medical appointments and family responsibilities. They use Document Scanner with OCR: Paper to Searchable Text to reduce one major barrier so their limited energy can go toward decisions that affect outcomes.
Practical ways this feature can be used:
1. Scan: Medical report from doctor appointment -> Searchable PDF in Evidence Locker
2. Batch scan: 10-page benefits decision -> All text searchable and organized
3. Search: "Find all documents mentioning 'accommodation'" -> Instant results
4. Share: Export scan as PDF for lawyer or representative
5. Backup: Upload to encrypted cloud sync (optional)
