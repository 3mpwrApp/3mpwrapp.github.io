# Feature Spotlight: Voice Memo Evidence Logger: Document Incidents Hands-Free
**Category:** Evidence Tools
Record voice memos about workplace incidents, symptoms, or important events - auto-transcribed and encrypted with timestamps
---
## What Is Voice Memo Evidence Logger: Document Incidents Hands-Free?
Voice Memo Evidence Logger: Document Incidents Hands-Free is designed to record voice memos about workplace incidents, symptoms, or important events - auto-transcribed and encrypted with timestamps. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada.
---
## Key Highlights
- **One-tap voice recording from anywhere in app**
- **Automatic transcription to text (offline-capable)**
- **Timestamp and GPS coordinates (optional)**
- **Tag with categories: incident, symptom, meeting, conversation**
- **Saves to Evidence Locker with encryption**
- **Export transcripts for legal documentation**
---
## How It Works
Example scenario (illustrative only):
An injured worker is preparing an appeal while managing medical appointments and family responsibilities. They use Voice Memo Evidence Logger: Document Incidents Hands-Free to reduce one major barrier so their limited energy can go toward decisions that affect outcomes.
Practical ways this feature can be used:
1. Incident: Record workplace harassment immediately after it happens
2. Symptom: "Voice log: Pain level 8/10, unable to sit for more than 10 minutes"
3. Meeting: Record post-meeting summary while details are fresh
4. Legal: Auto-transcript provides dated written record for court or tribunal
5. Search: Find all memos tagged "supervisor conversation"
