# Feature Spotlight: Mood Tracker

**Category:** Wellness Tools

Log your daily mood and track emotional patterns over time - completely free

* * *

## What Is Mood Tracker?

Mood Tracker is designed to log your daily mood and track emotional patterns over time - completely free. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada.

* * *

## Key Highlights

- **Log daily mood using emoji-based interface**
- **Track patterns over weeks and months**
- **Export reports for therapist or doctor**
- **Set mood check-in reminders**
- **7-day average trends visualization**
- **Always free - no premium features or paywalls**

* * *

## How It Works

Here are real examples of how you can use Mood Tracker:

1. Record mood each morning and evening
2. Notice patterns like "Mondays are harder"
3. Share monthly report with therapist
4. Get gentle reminders to check in with yourself
5. Spot connections between activities and mood

* * *

## Why Mood Tracker Matters

- Better understand your emotional patterns
- Communicate more clearly with healthcare providers
- Identify triggers and helpful activities
- Track progress in mental health treatment
