# Feature Spotlight: Cognitive Load Reducer: Simplify Complex Information
**Category:** Cognitive Accessibility
Transform dense text, legal documents, and complex instructions into simple, digestible formats - essential for brain fog and cognitive disabilities
---
## What Is Cognitive Load Reducer: Simplify Complex Information?
Cognitive Load Reducer: Simplify Complex Information is designed to transform dense text, legal documents, and complex instructions into simple, digestible formats - essential for brain fog and cognitive disabilities. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada.
---
## Key Highlights
- **AI summarization of complex documents to key points**
- **Bullet-point extraction from dense paragraphs**
- **Reading level adjustment (Grade 5-12)**
- **Visual hierarchy improvements with headings and spacing**
- **Chunking: break long content into manageable sections**
- **Progressive disclosure: show details only when requested**
---
## How It Works
Example scenario (illustrative only):
An injured worker is preparing an appeal while managing medical appointments and family responsibilities. They use Cognitive Load Reducer: Simplify Complex Information to reduce one major barrier so their limited energy can go toward decisions that affect outcomes.
Practical ways this feature can be used:
1. Input: 10-page benefits decision -> Output: 5 key points
2. Simplify: Legal jargon -> Plain language explanation
3. Chunk: Long article -> 7 short sections with headings
4. Reading level: Adjust complex text to Grade 8 reading level
5. Progressive: Main points visible, details expandable
