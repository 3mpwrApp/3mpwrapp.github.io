# Feature Spotlight: Campaign Templates

**Category:** Campaign Tools

Pre-built templates for common advocacy campaigns and organizing efforts

* * *

## What Is Campaign Templates?

Campaign Templates is designed to pre-built templates for common advocacy campaigns and organizing efforts. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada.

* * *

## Key Highlights

- **Templates for: Accessibility, Benefits, Employment, Healthcare**
- **Pre-written tasks, timelines, and resource lists**
- **Customizable to your specific situation**
- **Proven strategies from successful campaigns**
- **Step-by-step guides included**
- **Always free organizing templates**

* * *

## How It Works

Here are real examples of how you can use Campaign Templates:

1. Use: "Accessible Workplace Campaign Template"
2. Includes: "Sample accommodation requests, meeting agendas, email templates"
3. Customize: "Add your organization's specific issues"
4. Follow: "6-week timeline from planning to action"
5. Learn: "What worked for similar campaigns"

* * *

## Why Campaign Templates Matters

- Start campaigns quickly without reinventing wheel
- Learn from successful organizing strategies
- Save time with pre-written materials
- Increase likelihood of campaign success
