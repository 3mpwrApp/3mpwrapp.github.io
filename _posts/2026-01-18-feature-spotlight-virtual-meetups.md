--- # Feature Spotlight: Virtual Meetups **Category:** Community Tools Join accessible online gatherings for community connection and support * * * ## What Is Virtual Meetups? Virtual Meetups is designed to join accessible online gatherings for community connection and support. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada. * * * ## Key Highlights - **Regular scheduled meetups by topic and region**
- **Fully accessible with captions and accommodations**
- **Drop-in or RSVP options available**
- **Facilitated by trained community moderators**
- **Small groups for safety and connection**
- **Free virtual community events** * * * ## How It Works Here are real examples of how you can use Virtual Meetups: 1. Join: "Ontario WSIB support group - Mondays 7 PM"
2. Attend: "Chronic pain peer support - Thursdays 2 PM"
3. Participate: "New to benefits? Orientation - First Fridays"
4. Accessible: Captions, ASL, pace adjustments available
5. Safe: Community guidelines enforced * * * ## Why Virtual Meetups Matters - Face-to-face connection from home
- Real-time support and advice
- Make friends with shared experiences
- Reduce isolation and loneliness * * * ## Getting Started Ready to try Virtual Meetups? Here's how to get started: 1. **Download the app** - Available on iOS and Android (coming soon)
2. **Complete setup** - Takes just 5 minutes
3. **Find the feature** - Look for "Virtual Meetups" in your app
