# Feature Spotlight: Accommodation Request Tracker: Never Let Them Ghost You
**Category:** Workplace Tools
Track accommodation requests with automatic follow-up reminders - hold employers accountable with documented timelines
---
## What Is Accommodation Request Tracker: Never Let Them Ghost You?
Accommodation Request Tracker: Never Let Them Ghost You is designed to track accommodation requests with automatic follow-up reminders - hold employers accountable with documented timelines. This feature is part of 3mpwrApp's commitment to providing comprehensive tools for people with disabilities, injured workers, and their supporters across Canada.
---
## Key Highlights
- **Log every accommodation request with date and details**
- **Automatic follow-up reminders (7 days, 14 days, 30 days)**
- **Status tracking: requested, under review, approved, denied, implemented**
- **Store all related correspondence in Evidence Locker**
- **Timeline shows employer delays and non-responses**
- **Export documentation for human rights complaints**
---
## How It Works
Example scenario (illustrative only):
An injured worker is preparing an appeal while managing medical appointments and family responsibilities. They use Accommodation Request Tracker: Never Let Them Ghost You to reduce one major barrier so their limited energy can go toward decisions that affect outcomes.
Practical ways this feature can be used:
1. Request: "Standing desk requested March 1"
2. Reminder: "No response after 14 days - follow up now"
3. Document: "Email sent March 15 - still no response"
4. Pattern: "Timeline shows 87 days from request to implementation"
5. Evidence: Export full accommodation history for HRTO complaint
