- Mood tracking
- Energy management
- "Good morning" check-ins
- Spoon theory content

**Midday (12-2 PM)** - High engagement for:
- Advocacy tools
- Legal resources
- Evidence management
- "Lunch break learning"
- Educational threads

**Evening (6-8 PM)** - High engagement for:
- Community features
- Support groups
- Personal stories
- Beta testing invites
- Feature showcases

**Avoid Late Night** (except):
- Crisis resources
- Mental health tools
- Insomnia support content

### Engagement Boosters

**Include in every post when possible**:
1. ✅ Question to audience ("Have you experienced this?")

4. ✅ Power words (broken, finally, secret, now, free)
5. ✅ Emoji for visual break (but not excessive)
6. ✅ Clear CTA (Join beta / Learn more / Try it)
