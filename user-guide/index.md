## Account Setup & Security

### Change Your Password

1. Open Settings (gear icon, usually bottom right)
2. Tap "Account Settings"
3. Tap "Change Password"
4. Enter your current password
5. Enter new password (8+ characters)
6. Re-enter new password
7. Tap "Change Password"

### Enable Two-Factor Authentication (Extra Security)

Two-factor authentication means someone needs both your password AND your phone to access your account.

1. Open Settings
2. Tap "Account Settings"
3. Tap "Two-Factor Authentication"
4. Tap "Enable"
5. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, or Authy)
6. Enter the code shown in your authenticator
7. Save backup codes (keep somewhere safe!)

### Sync Your Data to the Cloud

**What is syncing?** Copying your data to Google Drive or our servers so it's backed up.

1. Open Settings
2. Tap "Data & Privacy"
3. Tap "Cloud Sync"
4. Choose:
   - **No sync** (data stays on your phone only)
   - **Google Drive** (your data syncs to your Google Drive)
   - **3MPWRAPP Backup** (your data syncs to our secure servers)
5. Follow prompts
6. Done!

**Privacy:** Data is encrypted. We can't see it.

### Privacy Settings

1. Open Settings
2. Tap "Data & Privacy"
3. Choose what you're comfortable with:
   - **Analytics** (helps us improve the app)
   - **Crash reports** (helps us fix bugs)
   - **Personalization** (app learns your preferences)
4. Your choices are saved immediately

<a name="evidence-section"></a>
## Evidence & Document Management

### Uploading Documents to Evidence Locker

**What you'll need:**
- Your phone and a document to upload (PDF, photo, image, file)
- 5 minutes

**Step-by-step:**
1. From Home screen, tap "Evidence Locker" or tap the Resources tab → Evidence
2. Tap "Add Document" or "+" button
3. Choose source:
   - **Camera** — take a photo of a document right now
   - **Photo Library** — select a photo already on your phone
   - **Files** — upload a PDF or file from your phone
4. Select/take your document
5. Confirm the image is clear and readable
6. Tap "Next"
7. Add a title: "Denial Letter - May 15 2026" (be specific, include dates)
8. Add tags (optional): "decision", "medical", "letter", "evidence"
9. Add notes (optional): "For WSIB claim"
10. Review that it's encrypted (lock icon should be visible)
11. Tap "Save"
12. Document is now encrypted and stored

**Tips:**
- Use good lighting when photographing documents
- Photograph all pages of multi-page documents
- Use consistent tags for easy searching
- Include dates in titles
- Check encryption status (lock = encrypted)

### Organizing Evidence with Tags

**Tags help you find documents fast.**

**How to add tags:**
1. When uploading, tap "Tags" field
2. Type a tag (e.g., "medical")
3. Press Enter
4. Add more tags
5. Save document

**Recommended tag system:**
- By type: "decision", "medical", "letter", "photo", "email", "government"
- By case: "WSIB", "HRTO", "CPP", "disability-appeal"
- By date: "2026-march", "2026-april"

**Searching by tag:**
1. Open Evidence Locker
2. Tap the search/filter button
3. Choose a tag
4. See only documents with that tag

### Using Evidence Timeline

The Timeline shows all your documents in order (oldest to newest).

**How to use it:**
1. Open Evidence Locker
2. Tap "Timeline" view
3. Scroll to see documents chronologically
4. Tap any document to view full details
5. Look for gaps (periods with no documents)

**Why this matters:**
- Shows your case story over time
- Helps you spot missing evidence
- Useful when preparing for hearings

### Viewing & Downloading Documents

**To view a document:**
1. Open Evidence Locker
2. Find the document
3. Tap it
4. Document opens in viewer

**To download/share a document:**
1. Open the document
2. Tap the "Share" button (usually top right)
3. Choose how to share:
   - Email
   - Messages
   - Save to files
4. Follow prompts

**Important:** Make sure you trust the person you're sharing with. Documents may contain sensitive information.

### Deleting Documents

**To delete a document:**
1. Open Evidence Locker
2. Find the document
3. Tap and hold (long press)
4. Tap "Delete"
5. Confirm you want to delete

**Caution:** Deleted documents cannot be recovered. Make sure you're deleting the right thing.

### Troubleshooting Evidence Locker

**"Upload failed" error**
- Check file size (max 50 MB per document)
- Try a different file format
- Check internet connection
- Try uploading again

**"Can't find a document I uploaded"**
- Use search bar (magnifying glass icon)
- Try different tags
- Scroll through all documents
- Check if you accidentally deleted it

**"Photo is blurry/unreadable"**
- Delete it (tap and hold, then delete)
- Retake photo with better lighting
- Hold document flat
- Stand closer so text is large

## Advocacy & Legal Tools

### Understanding Your Decision Letter: Parse Claim

**Parse Claim helps you understand confusing decision letters.**

**What you need:**
- Your decision letter (email, PDF, or physical copy)
- 3 minutes

**Step-by-step:**
1. Photograph or save your decision letter as PDF
2. From Home, tap "Parse Claim" (under Advocacy section)
3. Tap "Upload Decision Letter"
4. Choose source:
   - Camera — take a photo now
   - Library — select from your phone
   - Files — upload a PDF
5. Select/take your document
6. Make sure it's readable (clear text, all pages)
7. Tap "Next"
8. Wait for app to analyze (30 seconds to 2 minutes)
9. Read the results

**You'll see:**
- **Decision Summary** — What they decided (approved, denied, partial)
- **Plain-Language Explanation** — What it means in simple terms
- **Key Facts** — What they considered
- **Your Deadlines** — When you need to act
- **Your Options** — What you can do next (appeal, review, reconsideration)

**Tips:**
- Use high-quality photos
- Make sure all text is readable
- If it didn't work, try a better photo
- Always verify deadlines with official tribunal websites

### Setting Up Deadline Tracker

**Deadline Tracker keeps track of all important dates.**

**Adding a deadline:**
1. Open Deadline Tracker
2. Tap "Add Deadline" or "+" button
3. Enter:
   - Date (use the calendar picker)
   - Deadline type: Appeal, Document Due, Hearing, Filing, Other
   - Description: "Appeal WSIB decision"
   - Notes (optional): "File appeal letter"
4. Tap "Create Deadline"
5. Deadline now appears on your calendar

**Importing from Parse Claim:**
1. After using Parse Claim
2. You'll see "Add deadlines to Tracker"
3. Tap "Add All" or select individual deadlines
4. They're automatically added

**Understanding the countdown:**
- Green: More than 7 days left
- Yellow: 7 days to 24 hours left
- Red: Less than 24 hours left

**Notifications:**
- 7 days before: Reminder notification
- 1 day before: Urgent reminder
- On the day: Final reminder

### Using Case Timeline

**Case Timeline shows your entire case in chronological order.**

**What it shows:**
- When you filed your claim
- Decision dates

- Hearing dates
- Any other important events

**How to use it:**
1. Open Case Timeline from Advocacy tab
2. Scroll to see your entire case history
3. Tap any event for details
4. Add events manually:
   - Tap "Add Event"
   - Enter date and description
   - Tap "Save"

**Tips:**

- Use it to tell your case story

## Wellness & Health Tracking

### Setting Up Pacing Partner

**Pacing Partner helps you do more without triggering flare-ups.**

**Step 1: Establish Your Baseline**
1. From Wellness tab, open Pacing Partner
2. Tap "Set Up Baseline"
3. The app asks: "What's your normal activity level?"
4. Rate it 1-10 (1 = bedridden, 10 = fully active)
5. Enter your average activity level
6. This becomes your baseline

**Step 2: Use the 50% Rule**
- Do 50% of your baseline today
- Rest/recover tomorrow
- This prevents flare-ups

**Example:**
- Your baseline = 6/10
- Safe activity today = 3/10
- Tomorrow = rest at 2/10
- Pattern: 3, 2, 3, 2, 3, 2

**Step 3: Log Daily Activities**
1. Each morning, tap "Plan Today"
2. List planned activities:
   - Work: 4 hours
   - Errand: 30 minutes
   - Hearing: 2 hours
3. Pacing Partner calculates your activity level
4. Shows if you're in your safe zone
5. Suggests adjustments if needed

**Step 4: Track How You Felt**
1. At end of day, tap "How did you do?"
2. Log:
   - Pain level
   - Energy level
   - Symptoms
   - How you felt overall
3. Over time, the app learns your patterns

**Tips:**
- Estimate conservatively (better to underestimate)
- Include everything (work, thinking, socializing, cleaning)
- Log even on bad days
- Review weekly patterns

### Using Wellness Hub Tools

**Wellness Hub has 41+ health tools. Start with one or two.**

**Popular tools to start with:**
1. **Symptom Tracker** — log pain, fatigue, symptoms
2. **Health Tracker** — track multiple conditions
3. **Mood Journal** — track emotions
4. **Sleep Tracker** — track sleep quality

**How to use Symptom Tracker:**
1. Open Wellness Hub
2. Tap "Symptom Tracker"
3. Tap "Log Symptom"
4. Choose your symptom (pain, fatigue, brain fog, etc.)
5. Rate severity 1-10
6. Add notes (what triggered it, what helped)
7. Save entry
8. View patterns over time

**Tips:**
- Track consistently (same time daily)
- Look for patterns (what makes it worse, what helps)
- Use data in medical appointments
- Print data for legal cases

### Connecting Wellness Data to Your Case

**Your health data is evidence.**

**How to use it:**
1. Log wellness data consistently
2. Every week or month, review your entries
3. Look for patterns
4. When preparing for hearings:
   - Print month/year of wellness logs
   - Attach to evidence package
   - Reference in appeal letters
   - Show to your lawyer

**Example:**
You've been tracking pain 7+ out of 10 for 6 months straight. Print and attach to WSIB appeal. It proves your pain is consistent and ongoing.

## Accessibility Options

### High Contrast Mode

**For people with low vision, color blindness, or light sensitivity.**

**How to enable:**
1. Open Settings (gear icon)
2. Tap "Accessibility"
3. Toggle "High Contrast Mode"
4. The app instantly changes to maximum contrast
5. All colors become bold and clear

**What changes:**
- Text becomes bolder
- Colors become more vivid
- Background becomes pure white or pure black
- Focus indicators become more visible

**When to use:**
- You have low vision
- You get eye strain easily
- You have color blindness
- You're sensitive to regular colors

### Dark Mode

**Reduces eye strain and helps in low-light situations.**

**How to enable:**
1. Open Settings
2. Tap "Display"
3. Toggle "Dark Mode"
4. The app switches to dark theme

**Schedules:**
- Off: Always use light mode
- On: Always use dark mode
- Automatic: Switch with device settings

### Dyslexia-Friendly Font

**For people with dyslexia or reading difficulties.**

**How to enable:**
1. Open Settings
2. Tap "Accessibility"
3. Toggle "Dyslexia-Friendly Font"
4. Font changes to OpenDyslexic immediately

**What changes:**
- Letters become larger and more distinct
- Serifs become more unique
- Spacing improves
- Reading becomes easier

### Text Scaling

**Make text bigger or smaller.**

**How to change:**
1. Open Settings
2. Tap "Display"
3. Tap "Text Size"
4. Choose:
   - Small (for good eyesight)
   - Default (medium)
   - Large (easier to read)
   - Extra Large (maximum size)
5. Changes apply immediately

**Remember:** You can also use your phone's built-in text scaling in System Settings for the entire phone.

## Keyboard Navigation

**Use the app entirely with keyboard (no touch needed).**

**Basic keys:**
- **Tab** → Move to next button/field
- **Shift+Tab** → Move to previous button/field
- **Enter** → Activate button
- **Space** → Activate toggle/checkbox
- **Arrow Keys** → Navigate lists/menus
- **Escape** → Close menus/dialogs

**Navigation examples:**
1. **Tab through buttons:** Press Tab repeatedly to move between buttons
2. **Activate a button:** Press Tab until button is focused (you'll see focus ring), then press Enter
3. **Select from a list:** Use Arrow Keys up/down to highlight, then press Enter
4. **Close a menu:** Press Escape

<a name="troubleshooting"></a>
## Troubleshooting

### "App keeps crashing"
1. Close the app completely (swipe it away from recent apps)
2. Restart your phone
3. Open app again
4. If still crashing:
   - Force close the app
   - Uninstall
   - Restart phone
   - Reinstall from App Store/Play Store

### "Can't log in"
1. Check email is spelled correctly
2. Check Caps Lock is off
3. Use "Forgot Password" to reset
4. If still stuck, tap "Contact Support"

### "Document won't upload"
1. Check file size (max 50 MB)
2. Try different file type
3. Check internet connection
4. Try again

### "Deadline notifications not working"
1. Open Settings → Notifications
2. Check 3MPWRAPP notifications are enabled
3. Check volume is on
4. Try restarting the app

### "App is slow"
1. Close other apps running
2. Restart your phone
3. Clear the app cache (Settings → Apps → 3MPWRAPP → Storage → Clear Cache)
4. If still slow, reinstall

### "Lost my data"
1. If you synced to cloud:
   - Open app
   - Log in
   - Data will sync back
2. If you didn't sync:
   - Contact Support (data may be recoverable from backup)
