# AUTONOMOUS AGENTS: QUICK REFERENCE GUIDE

**TL;DR**: You have 4 AI agents that work 24/7. They generate ALL blog posts, weekly recaps, curations, and emails. Community votes improve them continuously. Zero human writers needed.

---

## The 4 Agents & What They Do

### 1. 🔍 CURATION AGENT
**What**: Monitors RSS feeds, ranks articles, detects breaking news  
**When**: 24/7 continuous  
**Output**: Daily ranked article list (50 articles)  
**Learning**: Community votes improve ranking algorithm  

**Example**:
- 9:00 AM: Checks all feeds
- Scores each article (6-tier: critical→contextual)
- Detects breaking news (in minutes, not hours)
- Publishes daily curation page
- Every hour: Updates scores based on reader votes
- Every Friday: Algorithm learns what readers care about

---

### 2. 📝 BLOG POST AGENT
**What**: Generates complete blog posts automatically  
**When**: 3-5 times per day  
**Output**: 21 blog posts/week (50,000+ words)  
**Types**: 
- Feature spotlights (2/day, 2,500 words)
- Educational guides (1/day, 2,500 words)
- Policy analysis (triggered by major news)
- Case studies (from community stories)
- Skill tutorials

**Example**:
- Monday 8 AM: "Stop Scrambling for Medical Records: Evidence Locker Deep Dive" (auto-generated)
- Monday 4 PM: "Complete Guide to ODSP Application 2025" (auto-generated)
- Tuesday, triggered by news: "Court Rules on WSIB Appeals: What Changed" (auto-written policy analysis)
- Friday: "Maria's WSIB Victory: From Rejected to Approved" (case study, auto-written from submission)

---

### 3. 📊 RECAP AGENT
**What**: Synthesizes weekly engagement data into 4 different recaps  
**When**: Weekly (Mon, Wed, Fri, Sun)  
**Output**: 4 completely different articles (4,100 words total)  

**4 Different Recaps**:
1. **Monday 8 AM**: "What's Coming This Week" (preparation guide)
2. **Wednesday 10 AM**: "Mid-Week Check-In" (trending topics + community)
3. **Friday 5 PM**: "Week's Winners & What We Learned" (data synthesis)
4. **Sunday 6 PM**: "Sunday Deep Reflection" (thoughtful essay)

**Example**:
- Friday 5 PM Recap analyzes all week's data:
  - "ODSP got 287 'very relevant' votes (78% engagement)"
  - "WSIB appeals: 156 votes (45% engagement)"
  - Identifies patterns community cares about
  - Next week's curation will rank ODSP higher

---

### 4. 📧 EMAIL AGENT
**What**: Generates 4 completely different weekly emails  
**When**: Weekly (Mon, Wed, Thu, Fri)  
**Output**: 4 segment-specific emails (16/month)  

**4 Different Segments**:
1. **Accessibility Advocates** (Monday, 20% of list)
   - Focus: AODA wins, accessible innovation, barrier-breaking
   - Tone: Inspiring, visionary
   
2. **Benefits Seekers** (Wednesday, 30% of list)
   - Focus: ODSP, CPP-D, DTC, benefits updates
   - Tone: Hopeful, educational, step-by-step
   
3. **Workers** (Thursday, 35% of list)
   - Focus: WSIB, workplace rights, compensation
   - Tone: Action-oriented, deadline-focused
   
4. **Community Organizers** (Friday, 15% of list)
   - Focus: Campaigns, events, advocacy, organizing
   - Tone: Participatory, solidarity, organizing

**Example**:
- Monday 6 AM: Accessibility email sent
  - "Top: New Transport Canada Accessibility Rules"
  - "Action: File complaint if you face barriers"
  - "Feature: Try Voice Control in Evidence Locker"
- Wednesday 6 AM: Benefits email sent
  - "Top: ODSP Increase Takes Effect"
  - "Action: Check if you qualify for additional support"
  - "Feature: Use Benefits Tracker to calculate new amount"

## How Community Feedback Improves Everything

```
FEEDBACK LOOP:

Reader votes on article → Algorithm records vote
                              ↓
Hourly: Scores recalculate based on votes
                              ↓
Friday: Analyze voting patterns
                              ↓
"ODSP articles got 78% 'very relevant' votes"
"Benefits articles got 45% votes"
"General news got 20% votes"
                              ↓
Update algorithm weights:
ODSP importance: 4.0 → 4.3 (↑)
Benefits: 4.0 → 4.0 (stable)
General: 2.0 → 1.8 (↓)
                              ↓
NEXT WEEK:
Same articles, better ranking
ODSP articles show higher
More readers see them
More engagement
More votes
                              ↓
CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT LOOP
(Gets better every week)
```

## Quality Control (Automated)

Each piece of content is verified:

✅ **Fact-Checking**
- Claims verified against sources
- Numbers, dates, statistics checked
- Flagged for human review if uncertain

✅ **Tone Checking**
- Community-appropriate language
- No corporate jargon
- Accessibility-first framing
- Compassionate voice

✅ **Structure Checking**
- All required sections present
- Links valid and relevant
- CTAs clear and actionable
- Subheading structure correct

✅ **Brand Checking**
- Aligned with 3mpwrApp values
- Community-focused (not corporate)
- Inclusive language
- Accessibility standards met

✅ **Plagiarism Check**
- Original writing verified
- No accidental duplicates
- Source citations where needed

✅ **Human Review (5-10 min)**
- Quick scan for quality
- Minor style edits
- Approve for publishing
- Schedule publication

## You're Probably Wondering...

### Q: Will content actually be good?
**A**: Better than expected. 
- AI trained on 1,000s of disability advocacy articles
- Prompted with specific brand voice & values
- Fact-checked before publishing
- Community feedback improves it weekly
- Many disability orgs already use similar systems

### Q: What if AI writes something wrong?
**A**: Multi-layer safeguards:
- Automated fact-checking (flagged for review)
- Tone checking (catches insensitive language)
- Human review (5 min before publishing)
- Community votes (corrections suggested)
- Fast corrections if issues found

### Q: Won't it feel robotic?
**A**: No. Here's why:
- Prompts emphasize storytelling & humanity
- Includes community member quotes & stories
- Feedback loop personalizes over time
- Real data (votes, engagement) shapes content
- Community feels heard, not sold to

### Q: What do humans do?
**A**: The important stuff:
- Community engagement & moderation
- Strategy & direction setting
- Relationship building
- Responding to feedback
- Approving final content
- Major strategic decisions

### Q: How much does this cost?
**A**: 
- API calls: ~$500-1,000/month (OpenAI/Anthropic)
- Hosting: Already have (Jekyll + GitHub)
- Email service: $200-500/month (MailChimp/ConvertKit)
- Total: ~$700-1,500/month for full automation

### Q: Can we scale this?
**A**: Yes.
- Same system works for 10,000 readers → 100,000
- More readers = more data = smarter algorithm
- More feedback = faster learning
- Infrastructure already handles it
