# Community-First Engagement Strategy Guide

**Purpose:** Grow 3mpwrApp's social media presence organically through authentic community engagement (5-10 hours/week investment).

**Core Principle:** Build relationships, not just follower counts. Engagement rate > vanity metrics.

---

## Daily Engagement Routine (30 min/day = 3.5 hours/week)

### Morning Routine (15 min)
**Time:** 7:30-7:45 AM (or whenever you have 2-3 spoons)

1. **Search & Reply** (10 min)
   - Search these hashtags on each platform:
     - `#DisabilityRights` `#ChronicIllness` `#SpoonTheory`
     - `#WSIB` `#ODSP` `#WorkersComp` `#CPPDisability`
     - `#Accessibility` `#DisabilityJustice` `#CripTheVote`
   
   - **Reply to 3-5 posts** with:
     - Genuine, helpful insights (not promotional)
     - Personal experience ("As an injured worker who built...")
     - Validate their experience ("It's not just you - the system is designed to...")
     - Offer specific help ("The deadline for that is actually 6 months, not 60 days...")
   
   - **Golden Rule:** Add value first, mention 3mpwrApp ONLY if directly relevant

2. **Share Others' Content** (5 min)
   - Reshare 1-2 posts from disability advocates with thoughtful commentary
   - Use quote posts/retweets to add your perspective
   - Tag the original poster (they'll notice, relationship building starts)

### Evening Check-In (15 min)
**Time:** 7:00-7:15 PM (or when you have capacity)

1. **Respond to Mentions** (5 min)
   - Reply to everyone who engaged with your posts today
   - Thank them for sharing/commenting
   - Continue conversations (don't just "like")

2. **Follow Back** (5 min)
   - Follow 3-5 new accounts in disability/workers' rights space
   - Prioritize: advocates, community organizers, people sharing lived experiences
   - NOT: brands, influencers selling courses, corporate accounts

3. **Document Wins** (5 min)
   - Note which of your posts got best engagement today
   - Add to  `posting-results.json` (see [ENGAGEMENT_TRACKING_WORKFLOW.md](ENGAGEMENT_TRACKING_WORKFLOW.md))
   - Spot patterns: "Posts about Evidence Locker always get more replies"

## Weekly Deep Engagement (2-3 hours/week)

### Community Partnership Building (1 hour/week)
**Goal:** Build relationships with 10-15 key advocates who might organically mention 3mpwrApp

**Target List:**

#### **Disability Advocates to Follow & Engage With:**
- Search Mastodon: `#DisabilityJustice` + filter by most boosted
- Search Bluesky: `#CripTheVote` + filter by most engaged
- X/Twitter: Search "#ODSP" or "#WSIB" + look for regular posters with 500-5K followers

#### **Profile Types to Prioritize:**
1. **Injured workers** sharing their WSIB/workers' comp journey
2. **Disability lawyers/advocates** posting legal tips
3. **Community organizers** running local accessibility campaigns
4. **Chronic illness bloggers** sharing spoon theory insights
5. **Accessibility consultants** critiquing systems

#### **Weekly Engagement Action (1 hour):**
- Pick 3 accounts from your target list
- Spend 20 min per account:
  - Read their last 10 posts
  - Reply thoughtfully to 2-3 posts (not all at once - space it out)
  - Share 1 of their posts with your commentary
  - Goal: They notice you exist + recognize your username next time

**Outcome (4 weeks):** They know your name → They check out 3mpwrApp → If it helps them, they mention it organically

### User Story Amplification (30 min/week)
**Goal:** Build social proof (starting with founder stories while beta testers onboard)

**Current Status:** Beta testers just getting started - no user stories yet

**Process (PHASE 1 - Weeks 1-4: Build in Public):**
1. Share YOUR journey (founder vulnerability builds trust):
   - Why you built 3mpwrApp (personal WSIB/disability experience)
   - Development challenges overcome this week
   - Feature launches + design decisions
2. Create weekly "Behind the Build" post:
   ```
   🛠️ Behind the Build

   This week: Launched Evidence Locker encryption (AES-256-GCM).
   
   Why? Because injured workers told me they're scared their employer will access medical records on their phone.
   
   Your privacy = non-negotiable.

   Building 3mpwrApp in public: https://3mpwrapp.ca/blog
   ```

**Process (PHASE 2 - Month 2+: Beta User Stories):**
1. Check for beta feedback (Discord, email, DMs) showing real impact
2. Ask permission: "Can we feature your experience in a Community Spotlight?"
3. Create "🎤 Community Spotlight" post (see Template 4 in CONTENT_OPTIMIZATION_TEMPLATES.md)

   This is why we built 3mpwrApp. 🧡

   #DisabilityRights #WSIB #CommunityWin
   ```

4. Post to all platforms (Mastodon, Bluesky, Facebook)
5. Tag user if they consented

**Frequency:** 1 spotlight/week (every Wednesday = "Wins Wednesday")

### "Ask Me Anything" Thread (30 min/week)
**Goal:** Create engagement opportunities and position yourself as helpful expert

**Format (rotating weekly):**
1. **Week 1:** "AMA: Navigating WSIB Claims in Ontario"
2. **Week 2:** "AMA: Disability Tech & Accessibility"
3. **Week 3:** "AMA: Building 3mpwrApp in Public"
4. **Week 4:** "AMA: Chronic Illness & Advocacy"

**Post on:** Friday afternoon (3-5 PM ET) for weekend responses

**Example:**
```
🧵 Friday AMA: Navigating WSIB Claims in Ontario

I'm an injured worker who fell through the WSIB cracks and built @3mpwrApp because the system failed me.

Ask me anything about:
• WSIB deadlines & appeals
• Evidence documentation
• Finding disability lawyers
• Accommodation letters

Fire away ⬇️ I'll answer all weekend.

#WSIB #WorkersComp #Ontario #DisabilityRights
```

**Response Strategy:**
- Answer every question (even if it takes 2 days)
- Be specific, cite sources (e.g., "WSIB Policy Manual 18-03-02")
- Mention 3mpwrApp ONLY if directly relevant solution: "I built the Evidence Locker feature exactly for this"

## Influencer & Media Outreach (1-2 hours/month)

### Micro-Influencer Outreach (20 accounts/month)
**Target:** 1K-10K followers in disability/advocacy space

**Outreach Script (DM template):**
```
Hi [Name],

I've been following your work on [specific thing they post about] - your recent post about [specific post] really resonated with me.

I'm an injured worker who built 3mpwrApp after falling through WSIB cracks. It's a free, privacy-first app with tools for evidence documentation, letter writing, benefits navigation, and community support.

**Success Metrics:**
- 20 DMs sent → 5-8 responses (25-40% response rate expected)
- 5-8 responses → 2-4 beta signups

**Important:** NEVER ask for promotion. Offer value, then let organic word-of-mouth happen.

## Platform-Specific Strategies

### Mastodon (@3mpwrApp@mastodon.social)
**Strengths:** Privacy-conscious, anti-corporate, community-driven culture

**Strategy:**
- Emphasize privacy-first architecture ("local-first, encrypted, no tracking")
- Call out corporate surveillance & data extraction
- Use content warnings (CW) for sensitive topics: `CW: Workers' comp denials`
- Engage with #FediHire hashtag (people share job hunting + disability accommodations)

**Posting Style:**
- Longer posts OK (500 char limit is generous)
- Threads work well for tutorials/how-tos
- Alt text on ALL images (Mastodon culture enforces this)

**Boosting Strategy:**
- Boost others' content generously (3:1 ratio - boost 3 posts for every 1 original)
- Community amplification >> self-promotion

### Bluesky (3mpwrapp.bsky.social)
**Strengths:** Newer platform, growing disability community, tech-forward users

**Strategy:**
- Create "Disability Advocacy" starter pack (curated account list for new users)
- Leverage custom feeds feature (create "Disability Rights Canada" feed)
- Thread format works well (longer stories, step-by-step guides)

**Posting Style:**
- Shorter than Mastodon (300 char limit)
- Use threads for complex topics
- Quote posts to add context/commentary

**Opportunity:**
- Early adopter advantage → Build community now before platform saturate

### X/Twitter (once configured)
**Strengths:** Real-time engagement, news breaking, journalist presence

**Strategy:**
- Quote-tweet disability news with 3mpwrApp perspective
- Use trending hashtags strategically when genuinely relevant (#NDIS, #DisabilityTwitter)
- Live-tweet conferences/events (ONIWG meetings, AODA consultations)

**Posting Style:**
- Punchier hooks (280 char limit forces brevity)
- Threads for storytelling
- Fast-paced replies (notifications = engagement opportunities)

**Caution:** Toxicity management - block liberally, mute keywords, protect mental health

### Facebook (if active)
**Strengths:** Older demographic, group-based communities, event organizing

**Strategy:**
- Create "3mpwrApp Community" Facebook Group (not just Page)
- Share longer-form content (blog posts work well)
- Post events (virtual meetups, webinars)
- Cross-post from blog automatically

**Posting Style:**
- Storytelling format (personal, narrative)
- Carousels (multi-image posts explaining features step-by-step)
- **Q&A posts** (Facebook's algorithm favors comment engagement)

## Engagement Tactics That Work

###✅ **Do More Of:**
1. **Personal storytelling** - "I built this because the system failed me"
2. **Validate lived experience** - "It's not just you, the system is designed to..."
3. **Specific, actionable tips** - "Your WSIB appeal deadline is 6 months, not 60 days"
4. **Celebrate community wins** - "🎤 Community Spotlight" posts
5. **Ask questions** - "What's the hardest part of navigating ODSP?" (engagement++)

### ❌ **Do Less Of:**
1. **Feature announcements without context** - "New feature: Evidence Locker" (who cares?)
2. **Corporate speak** - "Leveraging synergies..." (disability community hates this)
3. **Overselling** - "Revolutionary game-changer!" (trust-destroying language)
4. **Posting without engaging** - (post + ghost = algorithm punishes you)
5. **Self-promotion without value** - "Download our app!" (why should they?)

## Red Flags (When To Disengage)

**Block/Mute immediately if:**
- Ableism or disability denialism
- Insurance company/employer accounts engaging in bad faith
- "Just try harder" toxic positivity
- Scammers offering fake benefits/miracle cures

**Your capacity is limited:** Protect it ruthlessly. Block liberally, no guilt.

## Measuring Success (What Actually Matters)

### Vanity Metrics (Nice but not critical):
- Follower count
- Post impressions
- Like counts

### Real Metrics (What drives impact):
- **Engagement rate** (likes + shares + replies / impressions)
- **App signups FROM social** (use UTM parameters to track)
- **Community discussions** (replies, threads, DM conversations)
- **Organic mentions** (someone shares 3mpwrApp without you asking)
- **Media mentions** (journalists find you through social presence)

**The Goal:** Build a community that amplifies itself. When beta users tag friends who need help → that's success.
