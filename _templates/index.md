# Appeal Letter Templates

**Professional WSIB appeal letters in 30-45 minutes.** These templates are based on real winning cases and save you $500+ in paralegal fees.

---

## Available Templates

### [Back Injury Appeal Letter](/templates/back-injury-appeal/)
**Time to complete:** 30-45 minutes  
**Pages:** 8  
**Best for:** Workplace back injuries, lifting injuries, repetitive strain to lower back

**What's included:**
- Opening statement with case reference
- Timeline of injury and treatment
- Medical evidence section
- Work restrictions documentation
- Legal argument framework
- Closing statement

### [Chronic Pain Appeal Letter](/templates/chronic-pain-appeal/)
**Time to complete:** 30-45 minutes  
**Pages:** 7  
**Best for:** Long-term pain conditions, fibromyalgia, complex regional pain syndrome

**What's included:**
- Pain documentation framework
- Functional limitations evidence
- Medical specialist reports section
- Quality of life impact
- Legal argument for chronic conditions
- Supporting documentation checklist

### [Pre-Existing Condition Appeal](/templates/pre-existing-appeal/)
**Time to complete:** 45-60 minutes  
**Pages:** 9  
**Best for:** When WSIB denies your claim due to a pre-existing condition

**What's included:**
- Pre-existing vs. work-related injury argument
- Medical evidence separating conditions
- Aggravation vs. causation framework
- Legal precedents section
- Expert medical opinion structure
- Comprehensive evidence checklist

## How to Use These Templates

1. **Download the template** that matches your situation
2. **Fill in your information** - Replace [BRACKETED TEXT] with your details
3. **Gather supporting documents** - Follow the evidence checklist in each template
4. **Review for accuracy** - Make sure all dates, names, and medical information are correct
5. **Submit to WSIAT** - Mail or upload according to their instructions

**Important:** These templates provide the structure and legal framework, but you need to customize them with YOUR specific medical evidence and circumstances.
