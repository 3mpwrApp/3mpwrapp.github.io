Connecting the Dots: Ontario Tribunal Network (2020-2026)

Interactive keyword relationship mapping across all four Ontario tribunals: WSIAT (11,430 cases), HRTO (9,269 cases), ONSBT (13,798 cases), ONWSIB (431 cases). Complete 2020-2026 analysis. Canada-wide expansion in progress.

📊 What This Visualization Shows:

  • Nodes (circles): Keywords from 34,928 Ontario tribunal decisions (2020-2026) across all four tribunals
  • Coverage: WSIAT (11,430 cases), HRTO (9,269 cases), ONSBT (13,798 cases), ONWSIB (431 cases)
  • Edges (lines): Co-occurrence frequency (how often keywords appear in the same case)
  • Thicker lines: More frequent co-occurrence (statistically significant clustering)
  • Node size: Larger = appears in more cases overall (frequency-weighted)
  • Colors: Different categories (denial patterns, body parts, medical/legal terms, tribunal nodes)

What This Shows: Keyword clustering patterns and tribunal-specific terminology across Ontario's disability/workplace/human rights adjudication systems.
Data Sources: CanLII keyword metadata (2020-2026). Updated April 27, 2026 with complete Ontario tribunal coverage.
Limitations: Keywords show patterns in decisions but not complete case narratives, procedural details, or undetected outcomes.
