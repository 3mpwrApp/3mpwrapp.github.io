### 🔬 What We Found: Three Key Investigations

Our research is open-source, transparent, and designed for injured workers (all statistics explained in plain English—no math degree required).

#### **1. WSIB Exposed: Statistical Evidence of Systematic Patterns**
**Key Finding:** 43.9% (95% CI: 42.3-45.6%) of all tribunal cases involve "medical evidence" disputes—WSIB's #1 weapon against injured workers.

🔗 https://3mpwrapp.ca/blog/2026/04/15/wsib-exposed-statistical-evidence-proves-systematic-manipulation/

---

#### **2. The WSIB Black Box: Who's Being Hidden?**
**Key Finding:** An estimated **1.14-2.29 million Ontario injured workers** were suppressed from filing claims (2020-2026)—people who were hurt at work but never made it into the system.

**Data Darkness:** 91.8% of tribunal decisions don't report final outcomes, making it nearly impossible for community groups like yours to track what's really happening.

🔗 https://3mpwrapp.ca/blog/2026/04/16/wsib-black-box-claim-suppression-outcome-obscurity/

---

#### **3. The Hidden Language of Denial: Keyword Patterns Decoded**
**Key Finding:** "Pre-existing condition" appears in 13.3% (95% CI: 12.7-13.9%) of tribunal cases—WSIB's #1 denial tactic.

**Body-Part Bias Documented:**
- Knee injuries: 20% denied as "pre-existing" (vs. 13.3% baseline) → Statistically significant (χ² = 32.7, p < 0.001)
- Back injuries: 19%
- Shoulder injuries: 16%

If your members are dealing with these injuries, they're facing systematic bias—not isolated bad decisions.

🔗 https://3mpwrapp.ca/blog/2026/04/16/hidden-language-of-denial-wsib-keyword-decoder/
