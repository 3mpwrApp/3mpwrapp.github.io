### 🔬 New Research Published: Three Investigative Reports

We've published comprehensive analysis based on rigorous statistical testing (95% confidence intervals, chi-square tests, p-values). All research is open-source and transparent:

#### **1. WSIB Exposed: Statistical Evidence Reveals Systematic Patterns**
📊 **Key Finding:** 43.9% (95% CI: 42.3-45.6%) of all tribunal cases mention "medical evidence" disputes—WSIB's #1 weapon.

🔗 Read: https://3mpwrapp.ca/blog/2026/04/15/wsib-exposed-statistical-evidence-proves-systematic-manipulation/

---

#### **2. The WSIB Black Box: Claim Suppression & Outcome Obscurity**
🚨 **Key Finding:** Estimated 1.14-2.29 million injured workers suppressed from filing claims (2020-2026), based on IWH research rates.

📊 **Data Darkness:** 91.8% of tribunal decisions don't report final outcomes—making it nearly impossible to track system-wide denials.

🔗 Read: https://3mpwrapp.ca/blog/2026/04/16/wsib-black-box-claim-suppression-outcome-obscurity/

---

#### **3. The Hidden Language of Denial: WSIB Keyword Patterns Decoded**
🎯 **Key Finding:** "Pre-existing condition" appears in 13.3% (95% CI: 12.7-13.9%) of cases—WSIB's #1 denial tactic.

📈 **Body-Part Bias:** Knee injuries denied as "pre-existing" at 20.0% rate (vs. 13.3% baseline)—statistically significant pattern (χ² = 32.7, p < 0.001).

🔗 Read: https://3mpwrapp.ca/blog/2026/04/16/hidden-language-of-denial-wsib-keyword-decoder/
