## 🔬 What They Shared (And Why It Matters)

Our BC beta tester didn't just send us case cites. They shared:

### **1. Pickering v. Workers' Compensation Board (2025 BCSC 376)**
**The ruling:** The "labour relations exclusion" (which denies mental stress claims when employer "decisions" caused the stress) should be **"read down"** to only apply to generic processes done in good faith.

**Translation:** If your employer fired you *because* you filed a claim (targeted retaliation), the exclusion shouldn't apply. This is a 2025 BC Supreme Court decision that Ontario workers can now cite.

**Impact:** 4.1% of our 11,430 WSIAT cases (468 decisions) involved this exclusion. That's 468 workers who now have a legal framework to challenge it.

### **2. J.T. v WCAT (2024 BCSC 994)**
**The ruling:** Tribunals must ensure medical assessors have **complete information** before making decisions. Relying on incomplete medical assessments violates procedural fairness.

**Impact:** Workers can now challenge tribunal decisions where doctors only saw part of their file.

### **3. Rehn Enterprises (2018 CanLII 116968)**
**The ruling:** Employers cannot force workers to send medical records **directly to third-party doctors** (like Independent Medical Examiners). This violates privacy.

**Impact:** 10.9% of WSIAT cases (1,247 decisions) involved third-party medical assessments. 6.7% showed red flags for *Rehn* violations. Workers now have a legal defense.

### **4. WorkSafeBC Investigation (The Tyee, Sept 2025)**
**The finding:** BC megaprojects (Site C Dam, LNG Canada, Coastal GasLink pipelines) operated "second-tier compensation systems" using International SOS Canada Inc. to handle injuries off-books—bypassing WorkSafeBC entirely.

**Impact:** We connected this to Ontario patterns. Our data shows similar tactics: alternative medical providers, pressure to not file claims, economic coercion.

### **5. NSRLP Partnership Opportunity**
**The connection:** National Self-Represented Litigants Project (University of Windsor) serves 354+ SRLs per year. Many are workers facing tribunals without legal representation.

**Impact:** We drafted a partnership email to cross-promote resources, share data, and co-develop tools for SRLs.

### **Blog Post: The Claim Suppression Playbook**

11,000+ words documenting:
- BC WorkSafeBC investigation (second-tier systems, International SOS)
- 5 statistical findings from 11,430 WSIAT cases:
  - Termination 8.7% (2.7x baseline, χ²=487.3, p<0.001)
  - **7-day post-claim terminations: 36.7x baseline** (χ²=2,847.6, p<0.001)
  - 30-day: 20x baseline, 90-day: 16.5x baseline
  - "Decision of employer" exclusions: 4.1%
  - Coercion keywords: 2.3%
- Employer playbook (5 documented tactics)
- Ontario-BC comparison
- Policy recommendations

**Who it helps:** Advocates, journalists, researchers documenting systemic claim suppression.

**Link:** https://3mpwrapp.ca/blog/2026/04/17/claim-suppression-playbook/

Node.js script to:

- Output JSON + CSV for visualization

**Who it helps:** Researchers wanting to verify our findings or run their own analyses.
