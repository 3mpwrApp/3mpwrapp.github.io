Essential Guides: Reference Library for Your Situation

You shouldn’t need to hunt through confusing government websites or hope you’re reading the latest information. This is our collection of always-current, comprehensive guides on the topics that matter most to our community.

Each guide is:

  • ✅ Up-to-date - Last updated date shown on every guide
  • ✅ Step-by-step - No skipping necessary prerequisites
  • ✅ Plain English - We’ve eliminated the jargon
  • ✅ Incomplete info warned - We flag when rules change frequently

  • ✅ Free - No registration, no paywalls, no ads

📖 About These Guides

How We Keep Guides Current

Each guide shows “Last updated: [date]” at the top and bottom. We refresh guides:

  • Monthly: Core guides on benefits, WSIB, accommodation
  • As needed: When laws/policies change (flagged with ⚠️ POLICY CHANGE notice)
  • Quarterly: All other guides reviewed and refreshed

We track policy changes across:

  • Federal (CPP-D, Canada Disability Benefit, ADA)
  • Provincial (ODSP, AISH, PWD benefits, WCB/WSIB updates)
  • Regulatory (AODA, provincial human rights codes)

What Each Guide Includes

Every guide has:

  • Key terms decoded
  • Common mistakes to avoid
  • Timeline (how long things take)
  • Where to get help
  • When things change (policy updates)

We keep them current — Every guide shows the last update date. If you find outdated info, let us know.
