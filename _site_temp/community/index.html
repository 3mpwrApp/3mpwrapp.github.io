🎙️ The Injured Worker’s Legal Side Podcast

🔋 Energy: Very Light

Weekly Podcast with Richard and Paul - Expert WSIB Insights

⚖️ Real legal insights from experienced professionals helping injured workers across Ontario.

Join Richard Fink (Lawyer) and Paul Taylor (Paralegal) as they discuss various issues that many injured workers face when they are on WSIB. Weekly episodes covering your rights, navigating the system, and getting the support you deserve.

📞 Need WSIB assistance or want to participate?

Richard Fink Law: finklegal.com
Paul's Paralegal: paulsparalegal.ca

👥 Who’s Welcome

🔋 Energy: Minimal

Who is welcome in our community

Everyone is welcome in our community:

  • 🦽 Persons with disabilities - All disabilities, visible and invisible
  • 🏗️ Injured workers - Workplace injuries, occupational diseases, psychological injuries
  • 💙 Family & caregivers - Parents, spouses, siblings, chosen family
  • 🤝 Allies & advocates - Non-disabled people committed to disability justice
  • 🛠️ Union members - Organizing for workers' rights
  • 🏥 Healthcare providers - Supporting patients with compassion
  • ⚖️ Legal advocates - Fighting for justice
  • 🌍 General public - Learning about disability rights
