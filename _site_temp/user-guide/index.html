Getting Started

🌟 Personalization Setup (NEW!)

What it does: Tailors the entire app experience to your role, disabilities, wellness preferences, and advocacy needs.

How to access: Settings → Profile → Edit Profile

Step-by-Step:

  1. Select Your Role
    • Person with Disability - You have a disability or chronic condition
    • Supporter - You support someone with a disability
    • Ally - You advocate for disability rights
    • Family - Family member of someone with a disability

  2. Choose Your Details (varies by role)

    For Persons with Disabilities:

    • Disability Categories - Select all that apply (Chronic Pain, Fatigue, Mental Health, Mobility, Vision, Hearing, etc.)
    • Symptoms to Track - What you want to monitor (Pain, Energy, Mood, Sleep, etc.)
    • Wellness Tools - Features you want highlighted (Pain Tracker, Energy Tracker, Mood Logger, etc.)
    • Advocacy Needs - Help you need (Benefits appeals, Legal help, Housing, Employment, Healthcare)

    For Supporters/Allies/Family:

    • Relationship Context - How you connect to disability community
    • Advocacy Focus - Areas you want to support
    • Tools to Prioritize - Features most relevant to you
  3. Additional Settings (Optional)
    • Energy Patterns - When you have most/least energy
    • Jurisdiction - Your province/state for relevant legal info
    • Preferred Language - English, French, Spanish, etc.
    • Accommodations - Accessibility preferences
  4. Save Your Profile
    • Click SAVE at bottom
    • Personalization activates immediately
    • All selections persist across app restarts

What Gets Personalized:

  • Home Screen
    • Role-based greeting (“Good morning! Welcome back” for PWDs)
    • Top 3 recommended features
    • Profile completion prompt if incomplete
  • Wellness Tab
    • “⭐ Recommended For You” section
    • Your preferred tools appear first
    • Star icons highlight selections
    • Contextual explanations
  • Advocacy Tab
    • “⭐ Your Advocacy Needs” section
    • Top 5 needs from your profile
    • Resources prioritized to match
    • Legal/benefits/housing content filtered

Data Privacy:

  • ✅ All data stays on your device by default
  • ✅ Optional cloud sync (Google Drive or Firebase)
  • ✅ Opt-in for ML pattern learning (helps build future AI features)
  • ✅ You control all data
  • ✅ GDPR/PIPEDA compliant

Tips:

  • Update your profile anytime as needs change
  • More selections = better personalization
  • Try different roles to see different experiences
  • All your data is always accessible to you

Troubleshooting:

  • Personalization not working? → Close and reopen app
  • Selections not saving? → Check you clicked SAVE at bottom
  • Want to reset? → Settings → Profile → Reset Profile

  • Optional cloud backup via BYOC

💚 Wellness Checks

What it does: Sends alerts to emergency contacts if you’re inactive for a set period.

How to access: Settings → Wellness Checks

Setup steps:

  1. Go to Settings → Wellness Checks
  2. Enable Wellness Checks
  3. Set inactivity threshold (6 hours, 12 hours, 24 hours, custom)
  4. Add emergency contacts (name, phone, email, relationship)
  5. Choose what contacts can see (activity status only, or full details)
  6. Tap Save

How it works:

  • App tracks when you last opened it
  • If inactive beyond threshold, sends alert to contacts
  • You can manually check in anytime
  • Pause during planned absences (vacation, hospital stay)

Privacy controls:

  • Choose what information contacts receive
  • Contacts only get alert when threshold passed
  • You can update or remove contacts anytime
  • Fully encrypted communications

Core Features

🎯 Complexity Mode

What it does: Adjusts app to show 5, 20, or 150+ features based on your capacity.

How to access: Settings → Complexity Mode

Three modes:

🔋 Simple Mode (5 features)

  • Evidence Locker
  • Letter Wizard (top 3 templates)
  • Crisis Resources
  • Mood Tracker
  • Community Support

Use when: Bad days, flare-ups, brain fog, overwhelmed

📊 Standard Mode (20 features)

  • All Simple features plus:
  • AI tools, wellness tracking, campaigns, events, profile customization

Use when: Most of the time for most users

🚀 Power User Mode (150+ features)

  • Everything unlocked
  • Advanced tools, beta features, experimental functionality

Use when: You want full control and access

Bad Day Mode:

  • One-tap emergency simplification from any mode
  • Accessible from Settings or Global SOS button
  • Temporarily switches to Simple until you change back

How to switch:

  1. Settings → Complexity Mode
  2. Choose Simple, Standard, or Power User
  3. Tap Save
  4. App reloads with new feature set

What it does: Track emotional states with AI pattern detection, trigger identification, and context-aware coping strategies.

How to access: Wellness Hub → Mental Health & Crisis → Mood Tracker 2.0

Enhanced features:

  • External Factor Tracking - Log sleep, weather, exercise, social interaction
  • Trigger Identification - AI suggests what might be affecting mood

⚡ Pacing Partner

What it does: Predicts energy levels hourly, suggests pacing strategies, analyzes body-mind sync, includes compassion mode.

How to access: Wellness Hub → Energy & Pain → Pacing Partner

Features:

  • Hourly Energy Predictions - 24-hour forecast
  • Adaptive Pacing - Suggests when to rest/act
  • Body-Mind Sync - Correlates mental/physical energy
  • Compassion Mode - Gentle, non-judgmental guidance
  • Achievement System - Celebrate balanced pacing
  • Alert System - Urgent/moderate/low urgency warnings

How to use:

  1. Log energy level morning/evening for 3+ days
  2. Pacing Partner learns your patterns
  3. View energy forecast for next 24 hours
  4. Follow pacing suggestions
  5. Adjust activities based on predictions

Tips:

  • Enable Compassion Mode for gentle reminders

  3. See remaining coins
  4. Adjust plans if low

🧠 CBT Coach

What it does: Cognitive behavioral therapy techniques for challenging negative thoughts and building healthier patterns.

How to access: Wellness Hub → Communication & Growth → CBT Tools

Techniques included:

  • Thought Records - Log and challenge negative thoughts
  • Behavioral Experiments - Test beliefs safely
  • Cognitive Distortions - Identify thinking patterns

How to use Thought Records:

Interactive exercises:

  • Opposite Action - Do opposite of urge (DBT technique)
  • Acceptance Function - Practice acceptance
  • Radical Acceptance - Accept reality skillfully

Security & Privacy

🔐 Encryption

What it does: Protects all sensitive data with military-grade AES-256-GCM encryption.

What’s encrypted:

  • Evidence uploads
  • Documents
  • Messages
  • Health data
  • Personal information

How it works:

  • Data encrypted on your device before storage
  • Encryption key only you control
  • Cannot be read without your key
  • Even 3mpwr can’t access your encrypted data

No action needed - automatic for all users

📴 Air-Gapped Mode

What it does: Full functionality without internet connection - zero cloud dependencies.

Features that work offline:

  • Evidence upload (queued for sync)
  • Document management
  • Wellness tracking
  • Letter generation
  • All local data access

How to enable:

  1. Simply use app without internet
  2. All core features work
  3. Queue uploads for later
  4. Syncs automatically when reconnected

Benefits:

  • Rural areas with poor connection
  • Privacy-conscious users
  • Court dates or appointments
  • Any time you’re offline

☁️ BYOC (Bring Your Own Cloud)

What it does: Store your data in YOUR own Google Drive for complete data ownership.

How to set up:

  1. Go to Settings → BYOC
  2. Choose Provider → Google Drive
  3. Tap “Connect Google Drive”
  4. Log in with Google account
  5. Grant permissions
  6. Verify connection shows “Connected”

What gets stored:

  • Evidence files
  • Documents
  • Backups
  • Exports

Benefits:

  • You own the cloud storage
  • Access from anywhere
  • 3mpwr never stores your files
  • Cancel anytime, keep your data

What it does: You control 100% of your data.

Your rights:

  • Export all data anytime (PDF, CSV, JSON)
  • Delete all data anytime
  • Choose where data is stored (device only, BYOC)
  • Decide what to share and with whom
  • Revoke access anytime

3mpwr promises:

  • Never sell your data
  • Never share without permission
  • Never use for advertising
  • Open source roadmap for transparency

How to export:

  1. Settings → Data & Privacy

  3. Choose format
  4. Download or share

How to delete:

  1. Settings → Data & Privacy
  2. Tap “Delete My Account”
  3. Confirm (irreversible)
  4. All data permanently deleted
