❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is beta testing really free? **YES!** Beta testing is 100% free. The full app will also be 100% free forever - no catch, no paid tiers, no subscriptions.
How many people are you accepting? **Phase 1 Closed Beta:** We're accepting testers in waves based on: - Device type (balancing iOS/Android) - Province (representing all regions) - Disability type (diverse perspectives) - Tech experience (mix of novice and power users) **Everyone who signs up will get access eventually** - earlier signups get priority.
What if I find a critical bug? **Report it immediately!** We have multiple ways: 1. In-app bug reporter (Settings → Help → Report Bug) 2. Email: [empowrapp08162025@gmail.com](mailto:empowrapp08162025@gmail.com) 3. Beta tester emergency hotline (provided in welcome email) Critical bugs get fixed within 24-48 hours.
Will my beta testing data be kept after launch? **YES!** Your account, data, and progress will transition seamlessly to the full release. You won't lose anything. **Exception:** If you choose to delete your account during beta, that's permanent.
Can I use my real data during beta? **Absolutely!** The app is production-ready with: - Military-grade encryption (AES-256) - Secure cloud backups (optional) - BYOC modes (your data never touches our servers if you choose Strict mode) However, if you're cautious, feel free to use dummy data initially and switch to real data once comfortable.
What if I'm not tech-savvy? **Perfect!** We NEED non-technical testers. Your perspective is invaluable. We provide: - Step-by-step setup guides - Video tutorials - Live onboarding sessions - 1-on-1 support if needed If you can send an email, you can beta test 3mpwrApp.
What happens if I stop using the app during beta? **Nothing bad!** There's no penalty. Life happens, especially in the disability community. - Your account stays active - Your data stays secure - You can return anytime - No pressure to participate We understand spoons are limited. Use the app when you can.
Can I share the app with friends? **During Closed Beta:** Please ask them to sign up via the waitlist (fair to everyone) **During Open Beta (Q1 2026):** Yes! Share freely. **After Launch (Q2 2026):** Absolutely! Spread the word.
