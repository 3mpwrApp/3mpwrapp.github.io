## PART 2: LEGAL FRAMEWORK - THE *KRIZ* STANDARD

### The *Kriz* Decision: What It Actually Says

***Kriz v. Ontario (Workplace Safety and Insurance Board)*, 2000 CanLII 16946 (ON WSIAT)**

**Legal Standard:**

> For workers with pre-existing conditions, a workplace incident is compensable if it results in **"greater severity"** than would have occurred from natural progression of the pre-existing condition.

**What "Greater Severity" Means:**

**NOT:** "Did work CAUSE the underlying degeneration?" (No one claims that)

**YES:** "Did the workplace incident make the condition WORSE than it was before?"

**If the answer is YES → Claim is compensable under *Kriz*.**

### *Pasiechnyk* Reinforces *Kriz*: Work-Aggravated Degeneration Is Compensable

***Pasiechnyk v. Ontario (Workplace Safety and Insurance Board)*, 2015 ONCA 667**

**Key Holding:**

> **Work-related aggravation of pre-existing degenerative conditions IS compensable** if workplace exposure was a significant contributing factor.

**Application to My Case:**

WSIB argues my knee injury is "just degeneration." **But *Pasiechnyk* says:**

- Even if I have underlying degeneration (arthritis), **workplace incident can AGGRAVATE it**
- Aggravation = compensable
- WSIB's burden: Prove work was NOT a significant factor (not my burden to prove work was SOLE cause)

**My Evidence of Work Aggravation:**

1. **Temporal connection:** Symptoms worsened immediately after workplace incident
2. **Functional decline:** Baseline function lost after workplace incident
3. **Medical opinion:** Dr. [Name] states workplace incident aggravated my condition
4. **Mechanism:** [Twisting, impact, kneeling, squatting] are recognized mechanisms for knee injury

**Under *Pasiechnyk* + *Kriz* together: My claim is compensable.**
