## 🛡️ Our Funding Principles

<div style="background: #f0f8ff; padding: 1.5rem; border-radius: 8px; margin: 1.5rem 0;">

### ✅ We Will Accept:
- Individual donations (any amount)
- Community fundraising
- Mission-aligned grants
- Union partnerships
- Accessibility-focused sponsorships
- Pro-bono services

### ❌ We Will NEVER Accept:
- Venture capital
- Insurance company funding
- Corporate sponsors that exploit workers
- Funding with strings attached (ads, data selling, feature control)
- Money that compromises our values or independence

**Why?** We answer to the disability community - not investors, not corporations, not profit motives.
