### Why This Matters

**The Simple Version:**
When you're fighting WSIB, you need to know what arguments actually work. But reading through hundreds of legal decisions takes forever. We did that work for you.

**The Technical Version (for the curious):**
We're building what's called a "data flywheel." Here's how it works:

1. **Collect real tribunal data** → We scraped 1,204 cases from CanLII (free government database)
2. **Analyze patterns** → What medical evidence wins? What arguments fail?
3. **Generate knowledge base** → Turn those patterns into plain-language guides
4. **Help workers win appeals** → You use our templates and win your case
5. **Collect success stories** → Your wins become new data points
6. **Improve the system** → We learn what works and update the guides
7. **(Loop back to step 1)** → Collect more data, get smarter, help more people

**Why "flywheel"?** Because each step makes the next step easier. The more data we collect, the better our guides get. The better our guides get, the more people win. The more people win, the more we learn. It's self-reinforcing.
