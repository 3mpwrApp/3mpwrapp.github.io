## 🔥 Recent Coverage Areas

<span class="energy-cost" data-energy="2" aria-label="Energy cost: light">🔋🔋 Energy: Light</span>

The Disability Bulletin covers:

- **ODSP Updates** — Benefit changes, policy announcements, and what they mean
- **WSIB (Workers' Compensation)** — Workplace injury benefits and appeals
- **CPP-D & Disability Benefits** — Federal and provincial income programs
- **Disability Policy** — Changes to accessibility laws and regulations
- **Community Stories** — Real experiences, advocacy wins, systemic failures
- **Breaking News** — Policy changes that affect disabled Canadians
- **Access & Barriers** — Transportation, housing, employment discrimination
- **Rights & Justice** — Legal wins, tribunals, policy advocacy
